- **The final road's count now comes from forty strangers.** Most gates belong to villages with a rule and no face, and the crystal island lets you skip them.

## New notes, ranked

1. **The singers get one sentence.** The title promises this scene. "Bring the fire to her" ends "you will stand before the singers", then the ending never says what happens. You hid a burning under "if nobody saw it, you never did it", and swore at Davgi to change no word. As written, that branch is a free third way (ban #33). Direction: the singing is played. Every letter-kept oath is sung in your wording, to the faces it was said to, and the hidden fire comes out or you must stop the song.

2. **Stay loses on paper and is unwritten.** Sail gives a letter-kept gate that still opens; Stay gives a nailed one. Sail also has the duel, the rune cost and "You left". Stay has one clause. Almost nobody will stay. Direction: a fully played night with every family's house standing, and the forfeited Hearing visible on the pasture.

3. **Only one wording ever pays off.** Tree, Arch, Tower and Ribs have no wordings, despite the rule. "Bring us to water", "stand on the dyke" and the boatmen's "against the will of those who hold them" never touch the midpoint or ending, where they matter most. Direction: "bring us to water" should be keepable by walking them to the strand.

4. **The last battle has no sane enemy, and the Rain lies.** Sworn casters from either people are attacking a dyke both need standing. Direction: they fight over the gate that admits or bars the column. And a player who pays every instalment watches the number rise, then hits zero anyway. Direction: Tiziri reads out early what Rain can only delay.

5. **The scene everyone wants is missing.** After Krakadzor you can return with Zabel's stick while Shushan lives, and "On return" says only "slower each time". Direction: her hands go through your pockets, find her sister's half, and put it back.

Continuity: the boatmen cite camps that appear only afterwards.
