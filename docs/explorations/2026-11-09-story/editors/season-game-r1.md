# Game notes: Flitting Year, round 1

1. **The lift is the same shift six times.** Every exam slides regen toward Arcane, so a deep Arcane pool solves them all. At South, with pools of 1, there is no "best spell" to lose. Direction: make the lift a front crossing the arena, old band on one side, bare Arcane on the other. Feeding the sluice-stones bends that line, so the circuit's stated purpose is finally played. Only Life flips hard (Arcane is its opposite).

2. **Nothing says what a job is, or what it pays in the last fight.** Each leg names one job. Finale stations are people standing about, so the calendar pays off in a cutscene. Direction: state a count (say eight jobs, five days), and make each station a device you feed: charge the copper line and it arcs down the barges. The almanac should also count barges your hands saved.

3. **Build size.** Over a third of the jobs go unseen, and seven seats with three states is 21 regions. Direction: one map per seat; band, palette and enemy table change. Enemies migrate with their field. A skipped job returns as a harder salvage job on the same map. The tower is the single moving hub.

4. **Growth is sparse and contradicts the brief.** "You gain: Life pool" removes the choice of pool. Seven attunements over 30+ hours is one every five hours. Direction: any pool at each well; jobs pay regen fractions or workings; each newly arrived field's well offers a second attunement.

5. **Returning pays only in South and South-west.** The last four "coming back" entries are views. Direction: shrines that exist under one state only, like an Arcane constraint trial while the crater is purple. Dated windows make return trips compete for days.

6. **The mesa climax says spells don't matter.** You fight to a standstill, then a bet opens the gate, around hour 28. Direction: holding the gate buys the days Ottoline needs, and how well you held sets the days left for the fen.

7. **The Strait teaches the biggest unlock in a panic, on moving hulls.** Moving decks are a new sim feature. Direction: hulls stay fixed and the water scrolls. One forced use in the crisis, then calm days aboard before the real attack (pillars 4 and 7).

8. **Too little to fight by hour 25.** Don't add a villain. One boss is named; deep-field creatures appear only at the end. Direction: they come up through the gap from leg one, as fauna, growing. A boss per field.

## Do not touch

- Regen moving under you mid-fight, and old roads bending it differently.
- The calendar moving only on commitment.
- A different attuning ask per field, and attuning mid-finale.

## Ban list

Near 2: keep the purple neutral, never a spreading blight. Near 33 and 49: the wager is a contractual third way out; note 6 helps.
