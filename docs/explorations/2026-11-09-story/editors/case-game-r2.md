## New notes, most important first

1. **The reading rule uses shapes the grammar doesn't offer.** My note suggested spray, beam, nova, and that mixed the axes. Beam is Arcane's form and spray is Fire, Water and Cold's, so Lightning can't beam the relay without Arcane queued. Direction: hang the rule on casts, which every element has. Force reads one line, Area reads the room and ruins half, Place reads the surface it lands on. One rule, no per-prop writing.

2. **Both innate arts open at the midpoint, and the last three lands add no new verb.** That leaves about 20 hours of depth and regen only. Direction: Aaltje's thirty-one glyphs are a ladder. The cellar gives placing; Blink and the Double wait further down Lock Street, reached by bailing deeper on Fen returns, a Fen return that pays. Tie the ward to one fight on the first trip back, copied from their rune pips. With four in the cellar, every focus must go dark at once.

3. **The Purple Land is an attunement and an old man.** No local wrong, no encounters, no return. Direction: props lift off with the ground. Loose evidence rises past you, to be read before it's gone, with Piet's testable claims mixed in.

4. **Day 340 needs a rule.** "Thirty days without piles" and "river opens spring" imply a clock, and a real clock punishes the returns this game is built on. Direction: days advance only at story beats. Then make rows of piles the finale's actual resource, each row being ground to stand turrets on, so a wrong accusation costs positions you can feel.

5. **Scope.** New since round 1: accusation branches, four finding outcomes, changed statements, the box's doors. Direction: one world change per land for a wrong accusation; a fixed dozen doors, stated; one changed statement per land on the main line.
