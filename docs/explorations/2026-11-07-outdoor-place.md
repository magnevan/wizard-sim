# What the outdoor lesson's place is (2026-11-07)

The user's review of the first yard in bought art: "some of the trees clash with the rest because they seem to be lower contrast. The scene is not great, everything is too tight, and I feel like we're missing some terrain variety that could make it feel more real. Some objects are a little random too. Like why is there hay and wood chopping next to a training ground. The archery target is there, but not enough space for anyone to practice etc. we need more of a vision for what this spot is, and where it belongs in the world and what people do here."

The setting and story stay the user's (see the tutorial review decision). Below are three uses of the place, with no lore. The pictures are on [the Outdoor Art Kit page](https://claude.ai/artifact/QNKgjigTb14KmCe8mod1kT), from `tools/outdoor-kit/concepts.py`.

## Start from what people do

The tutorial's hands chapter says what the place has to hold:
- Strike and Heavy: something to hit, in a row a teacher can walk.
- Retreat: room to back off, and maybe cover.
- Defend: an archer's bolt to parry. The target in the first yard was really for this, so it needs a lane: the archer's end, open ground, your end.
- Sweep and the hands test: an open space to be surrounded in, and an edge for goblins to come from.
- Co-op: room for two to four wizards at once, and something for a partner to do (sparring).

A prop belongs only if it serves one of those, or explains where the place is. Hay is a target's backstop. Firewood belongs to a camp's fire, not a lesson.

## Three places

**A. The practice field.** Just outside the academy's wall. A cobbled yard from the gate, racks to arm up, a dummy line, a sparring ring of stones, a parry lane with butts, and a stream with a bridge into the wild, which the goblins cross.
- Gives: the first step outside, from academy to world, in one picture.
- Costs: the most orderly of the three, and it needs academy wall art.

**B. The road camp.** A drill camp at a crossroads on the road out. Tents, a fire and stores, a fenced drill square, butts stacked with hay, a pond, the forest edge.
- Gives: a place to come back to between chapters, which answers the vision's open question of where practice happens.
- Costs: the first lesson happens away from the academy, which is a story choice.

**C. The old stones.** A ruin in the meadows near the academy: a broken paved floor in a ring of standing stones, with an arch, a shrine and a lake.
- Gives: the most atmosphere, and pillars as cover for teaching Retreat.
- Costs: the most lore ("who built it" is the user's call), and little room for dummies.

**Recommendation:** A for the first lesson, with B and C kept for later places (a recurring practice stop, a duel or encounter ground). The page's `q-5-place` asks.

## What changed in the art

- **Trees:** both tree types now map their greens onto the leaf ramp by rank, so they use the full ramp, and they have the one-pixel dark outline the sheet props have. That was the clash.
- **Room:** each scene has about three times the yard's area.
- **Terrain:** layered on one map, each with its own wobble: dirt, cobble, water, tall grass. Water uses the bought set's pattern recoloured to a muted teal ramp (it came in neon cyan), with a light shoreline and wet dirt around it.
- **Trees keep off** paths, water and worked ground.

Still missing:
- Elevation (banks, slopes, cliffs).
- Academy wall art.
- An archer and goblins in the pictures.
- The water pattern repeats on large lakes.
