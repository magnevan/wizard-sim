## The question

What is an academy's outdoor training ground for, and how is it laid out, so that it teaches the hands chapter, is a low-pressure place to experiment, and shows co-op? Pillars 3 (co-op), 4 (dedicated spaces for experimenting), 6 (places that answer elements), 7 (visible approaches), 5 (being a place in a campaign).

## Seven shapes

1. **The drill yard** (conservative). A walled square with stations on the walls. Reads instantly, but it's a box: nothing says wizard, and you never go anywhere.
2. **The terraces.** The ground steps down from the academy to the wilds, and each terrace is the next set of lessons. Co-op grows as you descend. Costs height art: retaining walls and stairs.
3. **The open plateau** (borrowed: Breath of the Wild's Great Plateau). Stations in any order, one way down. Partners split up, and it's hard to pace.
4. **The element yards.** Plots that answer elements. These belong to the rune chapters, so they're a corner, not the whole.
5. **The amphitheatre** (borrowed: Hades). A summoning ring with an audience. One room; best as the bottom terrace.
6. **The living ground** (wild). One field the teacher reshapes with wards, which is today's tutorial chamber made part of the world. Not much of a place alone; best inside the field.
7. **The old stones.** Ruins in the wild. Heavy on lore; a later place.

**Recommendation: 2, with 4, 5 and 6 inside it.**

## The plan, as drawn

About 80 × 57 u inside the walls; Survive's arena is 48 × 34.

- **Upper court** (+4 u, flagstones), under the academy's wall and gate:
  - conjuring circles, one per seat, where you arm up;
  - four strike lanes (2.2 × 19 u) with a teacher's walkway;
  - the teacher's balcony, which sees every zone.
- **Sand courts** (+2 u, packed sand):
  - the parry court (24 × 5.6 u), with a shooter on a stand;
  - two sparring rings (9 u) ringed by warding stones, where friendly fire is the lesson;
  - four element yards (water, braziers, frost, iron rods).
- **Proving field** (0, trampled grass, 52 × 18 u): cover, four summoning gates, and a bank above to watch from. The teacher reshapes it between lessons.
- **Low gate** in the south wall, a bridge over the river, and the road to the wilds.
- **Route and stairs:** one route walks the zones in order. A stair joins each pair of terraces, and a drop is a wall to the sim.
