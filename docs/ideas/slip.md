# Slip

## One-liner
Ice and wet floors can make a body slip and fall, a short stagger you set up, instead of bodies sliding about on ice.

## In the hands
Not designed yet. The user (2026-11-11): sliding bodies isn't a helpful mechanic; "we may want something like a slip mechanic, though it'll require a lot of new animations". Sliding on ice was removed the same day: ice now chills whoever stands on it and freezes the wet, and only the Rime kite still skates (its Ice slide).

## Why it's interesting
A slip is a status you build toward with a partner (lay ice, then shove), and it reads at a glance if every body has a fall.

## Costs & risks
A slip, fall and get-up animation for every rig, enemies included. Another status to read on top of wet, chill, burn and stun.

## Open questions
- What triggers it: any move onto ice, a shove or hit while on ice, or running?
- How long does it last, and does it differ from knockdown (Earth)?

## Smallest test
Reuse the knockdown pose for a slip on ice when shoved, and see whether setting it up feels worth it.
