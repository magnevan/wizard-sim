## (d) Verdict

The texture has mostly separated. **Voice** is now clearly different on the page: Case is a dossier in fragments, Rival is a fond first-person gush, Oath is formal saga, and Season is monumental reportage of scale and numbers. Season and Oath are the nearest pair, both grave and declarative. **Finales** differ in kind except for Oath and Case, which became the same flood defence when Oath left its river gate. All four are still on the fen on the map. **Landmarks** no longer share the shrug, but a new shared move has replaced it. All four drafts now treat the skeleton, tree and tower with grandeur plus one uncanny physical rule, and that move produced the near-identical tree weather and upward rain. Three replacements landed on another candidate's material in substance (Oath's finale, Oath's names, Rival's Sigil), and Case's Sigil took the gesture Rival gave up. Ban-list contact is one near hit (Oath's third option) and several new near misses. The four games are now properly distinct, and what remains is a short list of local collisions and one shared habit of awe.
