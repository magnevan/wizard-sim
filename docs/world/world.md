## Geography

Browse from the continent to its regions and from a region to its areas. Give each area one parent region once it has a home. Link regions and areas to the biomes they contain so a biome can also be an entry point into the atlas.

- **Biomes** describe landscape, ecology and the creatures or encounters associated with them.
- **Regions** describe a part of the continent, its settlements, fields and constituent areas.
- **Areas** describe places the party can visit, such as the Academy or the battery. Link each to its campaign appearance and any encounters there.
