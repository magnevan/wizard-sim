# Bestiary

[Start here](../start-here.md) · [World](../world/world.md)

| Enemy | Role in a fight |
| --- | --- |
| Goblin | Melee swarmer that rewards hitting several enemies at once. |
| Brute | Durable enemy that marks a spot and sends a ground shockwave through it. |
| Shieldbearer | Frontal guard that rewards flanking. |
| Archer | Keeps its distance and fires a bolt from behind the front line when it can find cover. |
| Grabber | Drags a wizard away, giving allies an opening to break the hold. |
| Fire imp | Fire-immune melee enemy vulnerable to Cold and soaking. |

Bramble and Straw goblin are passive tutorial targets. The implemented roster and stats are in `game/packages/sim/src/archetypes.ts`. Attack logic is in `game/packages/sim/src/enemies.ts`.

Keep one row per creature until it needs a note of its own. Link to it from encounter and area notes where it appears.
