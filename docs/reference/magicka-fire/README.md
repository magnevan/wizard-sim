# Magicka's flamethrowers (the user's recordings, 2026-11-26)

Stills from two short clips the user recorded in Magicka 1 and Magicka 2 (the videos stay on their machine), for the 3D Fire spray ([spell effects in 3D](../../threads/spell-effects-3d.md), round 7). `*-sheet.jpg` is the clip at four frames a second; `*-stream.jpg` one frame cropped round the flame.

The user's notes, verbatim:

- **Magicka 2:** "bright core reminiscent of something like a welder flame, or maybe some jet engine; outer fire texture, blotches of fire that pulse large then fade; smoke patches that rapidly scale out and fade; char on the floor; small floating embers."
- **Magicka 1:** "flame particles that move outwards, scale up, fade out, change color from bright yellow to more orange/red at the end; same floating embers; Similar movement on smoke as flame, but smoke is rendered behind, and scales up larger than flame, lasts longer and has an upwards drift."

What the stills add: Magicka 2's jet is narrow and runs about half the length before the blotches bloom, with a black scorch spreading under the end that stays after; Magicka 1's puffs glow as one mass (added light) and the floor under them glows red. Both cones are narrower than our sim's.
