# What He Had on Him

**Case file, opened Day 1 at the school boat slip.** An old man drowns with seven objects in his pockets, one from each land on the circuit. You are going to all of them, and your hands can make evidence talk.

## The question

Cover sheet, two lines. **Who is he. Who let him drown.** A third gets added in his own handwriting at the Ice Road: **Who wound the gate.**

Closed as: Joost Tolhuis, gateman of Low Hask, blamed for 163 drowned, 22 years back. Gate wound by his apprentice, aged 17, to float her father's barge. Plank rotted on its own. She stood at the rail and counted ten before she shouted. Greet Vrolijk, builder of the town that replaced the one she drowned. Sold you boots at cost.

## The opening

School: river mouth, below the fen, Arcane vein, edge of the purple land. Last chore for leavers: clear the slip. Today it holds a body. Swappable. Teaches one rune each through handling him: thaw the coat, keep crabs off, conjure blade to cut pockets, water the plant.

**Found on him.** Male, about 70, right hand three fingers short, old injury.
- A. Grafting knife, pear cutting in wet moss
- B. Brass signal key
- C. Pit check, no. 77
- D. Iron door key
- E. Half a sheared bronze pin
- F. Ice cleats, storehouse chit
- G. Basket token, unknown

**Doesn't add up:** cutting is alive. Cut under a day ago, so in the water under a day. Nearest upriver is the Fen.

**G. Vrolijk, on the slip, collecting piling iron, came down on the same tide:** river's shut above the school till spring, for piling. Nobody's getting up it. Offers you boots at cost for the road.

**Reeve Sole:** body goes on ice in the fishmonger's cold-house. Fishmonger objects, on record. "You're going round anyway."

## The road

**Method.** Any spell works on any prop, always. Zap key B on the slip at minute five: it stutters —DUE —AD —ING. Locals teach the trick; wells only give depth (one pool of your choice, regen step). Shape and mix change the answer: spray reads surfaces, beam reads one line deep, nova reads the room and ruins half of it. Late props need chains, frost then relight what the frost exposed. Every key prop takes three spell routes in different elements, so any build gets through; a witness is the fallback. Shrines give extra deepenings. Evidence burns. Your friend's stray Lightning counts.

**Keeping trials:** evidence under threat, rising water or spreading fire. Solo answer: ward first, read second.

Each land has a local wrong at the edge, a harder one in the heartland, and the well as exam. Creatures dig up, eat or nest in evidence.

**Orchard Country** (south-west, Life). Orchard and Island in either order.
- **Attunement:** graft a scion to the giant tree's root and keep it alive one night. The tree's crown has its own weather. Rain falls under it on clear days.
- **Trick:** Life on soil raises seedlings along what's buried; on timber, brings up the rings.
- **Local wrong:** somebody moved a boundary hedge. Boars root up the old stakes before you can read them.
- **Exhibit A:** here he was Perrin, hired hand, bred a pear, Perrin's Late. Paid one orphan's apprenticeship.
- **Statements:** orphan, now 30: "Very tall." Three neighbours: "Short." Orphan was eight.
- **Return:** new statement, orphan knew who he was at 16 and took the money anyway. Strongbox under the rows, guarded by what's nesting on it.

**Spark Island** (north-west, Lightning).
- **Attunement:** carry a charge up the crystal peak and earth it. The peak strikes upward, at whatever on the slope holds most charge. Every Lightning rune you hold makes that you. At the top the crystal plays back every storm it has taken, all at once.
- **Trick:** Lightning into a dead device repeats its last act.
- **Local wrong:** wire station's money orders going astray. Swarms chew the line.
- **Exhibit B:** as Line-Dirk he walked the wire eleven years. Key, fully woken: TIDE DUE. PIN BAD. COMING.
- **Forecast, station log:** a shelf the size of a county has broken off the north ice and is coming down the sea. Grounds at the fen river mouth around Day 340. One surge, once. From here on it is visible from every crossing, a white line, then a coast, then a mountain range moving.
- **Operator:** nobody touches her set. Asked who sent that message: nobody. Asked who sent it nobody: "Him."
- **Return:** beam reads the main relay; every message he sent prints. Mostly money orders. Addressee list has a gap someone razored out. Receipt: G. Vrolijk bought wire here, four days before you arrived.

**The Mesa** (north-centre, Earth; always third).
- **The fortress:** four hundred years of defences, every arrow-slit pointing inward and down. It has never been attacked from outside.
- **Attunement:** hold the breach at the annual drill, when what lives in the shaft comes up. It has come every year.
- **Trick:** Earth lifts or packs rubble so you can see under it.
- **Local wrong:** Pit 9 collapse, six men never recovered. Burrowers are bringing them up in pieces.
- **Exhibit C:** he was Sill, paymaster of the pit burial club. Left eight years ago with the chest. Check 77 belonged to a man he owed. Widows here got nothing.
- **Statements:** widow Daan: "Thief." Son: "Thief." Pit foreman: "Thief, and I'd lend him money." Follow the chest: it's the same money the Orchard orphan got. The kindness was stolen.
- **Return:** with depth, settle Pit 9 under a keeping trial against the burrowers. Six bodies out. Widows paid from his strongbox, if you bring it.

**The Fen** (north-east, Water; fourth, river opens spring).
- **Attunement:** one tide-shift on the piles of High Hask, the stilt town. Greet shows you the crank herself: low, two hands. "It sticks."
- **Trick:** freeze a breach with Water and Cold, bail, soak dried ink until it lifts.
- **Encounters:** barge escorts, defending piling crews, a keeping trial in a flooding cellar. Boss: a heron tall as a cart horse that stabs through floors and hoards anything shiny. Its nest is a trove of props from Low Hask.
- **Beat:** midpoint, below.
- **Return:** a new row of piles each visit, some by your hand.

**The Burning South** (Fire).
- **Attunement:** when the swell flares, burn the firebreak above the town before the lava gets there, and don't be standing in it. The mountain lights the underside of its own clouds for a day's walk.
- **Trick:** careful Fire glows the ink on char, shows flaws inside metal.
- **Local wrong:** a foundry fire set to hide short weights.
- **Exhibit E:** gas bubble in the pin. Founder, first visit: "Impossible." Second: "It was a busy year."
- **Test:** rig force on a test pin with turrets and glyphs. That casting only shears if the gate is lifted against a full head of water. So someone wound it.
- **Order book, relit:** new pin ordered, signed G.V., the day after he drowned. Greet's statement: hadn't seen him in 22 years. He reached her alive.
- **Also:** Cas's alibi surfaces, below. Black spires: constraint shrine.
- **Return:** recast the pin in a foundry defence. The pour lights the coast.

**Ice Road** (far north, Cold).
- **The skeleton:** ribs 61 paces high. The road runs through them. Between the ribs lie the north's dead in rows, thousands, faces whole, because here cold keeps everything. Forty-one unclaimed from Low Hask. Asked why no one sleeps under the skull: all three drivers gave the same answer, the first agreement in this case. "Go and stand there." Did. Did not stay.
- **Attunement:** walk past the skull and stand still one night while things that wait come looking.
- **Trick:** chill a surface; frost forms wherever hands gripped, and deepens with how long.
- **Encounters:** rope caravans, armoured beasts, thaw rooms.
- **Exhibit F, chit:** his box. 22 years of payments, all 163 households ticked. His own case notes: gate lifted, pin sheared, barge floated off that dusk, "P. says she floated me off. Means the tide." Last line: "Need a wizard for the crank. Ask G. who." The seven objects were his file. The cutting was a present for her.
- **Crank from the inquest, frosted:** his hands on top. Under them, small, low, two-handed.
- **Unprompted, from here on:** frost works on anything. Try the boots.
- **Return:** deliver what the box still owed. Each door holds one more statement and one small fight.

**The Purple Land** (east, Arcane; seventh).
- **The ground:** pieces of it leave. Slowly, upward. The tower went first, and six residents who came back down died within the week. You go up by basket; loose coins pass you on the way. Oldest resident claims 131. Second-oldest disputes the 1.
- **Attunement:** the torn well below it, regen flooding, loose ground, aim or fall.
- **Trick:** thin Arcane beam shows inside a sealed thing.
- **Exhibit G:** Piet Oudt, 81, barge-owner, room paid in High Hask timber credit.
- **Beat:** late-game turn, below.

## The turns

**Midpoint (the Fen).** Key D opens a door on Lock Street, drowned quarter. Bail it. Name on the lintel. Up in the square, the stone: 163 names and, cut small at the foot, *Joost Tolhuis, who was drunk at his gate.*

**G. Vrolijk, the moment the name is out:** "I was his apprentice. I was at my aunt's that night." Says it before anyone asks. Later she adds, without being asked: "He left me alone with it. Seventeen, a tide night, and he went to a christening." True. He did.

Now every household he paid has a motive, and every land is reopened.
- **Orphan, new statement:** she'd written to Lock Street survivors giving his whereabouts.
- **Operator, new statement:** she did touch the set. He paid her to.
- **Widow Daan, new statement:** went to the Fen last autumn. "To look at him."
- **The Lock Street Men**, survivors who recite the names yearly, now hunt you for asking. Hedge-casters, your spell language, poorly, often at each other. You watch them ward one another and your hands get it: Shield's wider arts, off a suspect.
- **Cas Hoekstra, their head, confesses.** Method: strangled. Told body drowned: "Strangled underwater." Stands as the working answer through the Burning South, where a tavern slate puts him two lands away buying the house a round "on account of what I did."

**Host names a suspect to the Reeve,** once, with whatever you have. Wrong has visible cost: orphan's crop rots unpicked, the wire goes down, Greet named too early means thirty days without piles and fewer rows at the finale. Cas named means Lock Street gets bolder.

**Sigil (the cellar, same night).** Low Hask had a lamp-charmer, Aaltje Roos, name 88 on the stone. With the water coming she etched glyphs up Lock Street stairs. Thirty-one. They are still holding air in the drowned houses, 22 years after her. Read them and they give the order the street flooded, which is a timeline. Then the last one fails with you below it and the water rising. Your school focus can only hold what's in your hand. You jam a conjured guard into the socket, and let go, and it stays. Focus goes dark. Turrets, glyphs and Blink from here, and they land on the return to the first three lands. Nobody remarks on it, because nobody's down there.

**Late-game turn (the tower).** Beam through Piet's sealed sea-chest: barge log, last entry, "Aground at dusk. G. says twenty minutes." Piet gives a different account each visit. Each names something testable.
- **"Her aunt's shawl."** Back to Low Hask: aunt's day-book, ink lifted. "G. did not come. Cake uneaten."
- **"Rope burned her hands."** Frost on the crank shows the burn's shape.
- **One line never moves:** *She floated me off.*

Joost heard that for eleven springs and wrote "means the tide". He'd have got there with one wizard and one Cold rune.

## The ending

Day 340. Mountain range at the river mouth.

**The walk, before the surge, played.** Greet carries the lantern. Plank: rings say rot, nobody's fault. Soot on the rail, relit: a lantern ring. She sets her lantern down in it, from habit. Rail frosted: hold the spell and stand still, and her grip comes up at ten seconds, unmoved. Set your Double where he fell. Rail within arm's reach. He'd come to warn her about the pin and to ask her, of all people, who to hire. "I'll tell you after the tide."

**The surge.** Long preparation in the fen's well band. Seat the recast pin, one Arcane reading through the housing with the water at your chest. Frost the gate, wall the breaches, turrets on the walkways, fight what comes in ahead of the ice. Lock Street Men on the piles, setting each other alight. Greet on the crank, because it sticks.

**After.** She reads your page. Checks it twice. "Ten. That's right."

**The casebook takes two lines, host or quick vote.**
- **THE GATE:** *G.V. wound it.* His name goes up among the 163; hers is known; the walkways stay and people walk them looking down. Or *Stands as cut.* He left a child on a tide night and his own box says "Mine."
- **THE PLANK:** *She counted.* The Reeve comes, and next year a loose board goes uncounted. Or *Rot.* Also true. She stays. The town stays sound.

The cutting, outgrown your satchel, goes on his grave above the slip.

**Last entry.** Cold-house returned to fishmonger. They stand at the empty slab, hat off, count of ten, hat on, start shovelling herring.

## The recurring faces

- **Reeve Sole.** Writes in pencil, ink runs. Wants a name for the grave.
- **The fishmonger.** Raises the cold-house every return. Wants their ice.
- **Greet Vrolijk.** Counts twice. Her receipts sit days ahead of you in every land he worked, tracing his file. Wants the last row driven by Day 340.
- **Cas Hoekstra.** All 163 names in under three minutes. Claims to have taught you the ward. Wants the credit.
- **Bartel Sneek, packet passenger, oil salesman.** Eleven sightings in this case, including a duke, a horse-tall heron, a woman with a lantern just standing there. Wants to be asked. Confirmed so far: heron.
- **Piet Oudt.** Thanks everyone. Wants to go home, which is under water.

## Why this is fun to play

Your hands do the detecting: spray, beam, nova and mix each make the same burnt desk say something else, and the room you cleared twelve hours ago has a lead in it now you can hold a steadier Fire. Solo, you ward the cellar and read against rising water; together, one of you frosts a door handle while another fights near it with a spell that could very well reach the door handle. Going back pays twice, a stronger reading and a witness whose story has changed.

## The biggest weakness

The sharp player reads the boots at the Ice Road and knows, with a land and a half still to play. Those hours run on how and what you'll write, and on the mountain coming down the sea. Also the file is enormous: each land needs a local wrong, a changed statement and destroyable props, and the scope note only trims so much of it.

## Changed in this round

- Greet is open about being his apprentice; her alibi breaks by readings, the boots and the order book.
- Readings always physically work; shape and mix change the answer; chained late evidence.
- His pockets are his own case file, one reading short; the cutting is the exception.
- The Mesa hates him with cause: his charity was stolen from a burial club.
- Sigil opens in the Fen via a dead lamp-charmer's still-standing glyphs. Shield comes from watching suspects.
- Tide is one forecast event, a calved ice shelf visible from every crossing. Toll now 163.
- New peak, fire exam, skeleton, mesa fortress and tower; skipper replaced by Sneek.
- Two-line finding, gate and plank. Mid-game accusation with visible cost.
