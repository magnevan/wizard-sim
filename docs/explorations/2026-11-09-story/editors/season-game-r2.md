## Newly broken

- "Skip the road and Wenna is lost" contradicts "returns as a harder salvage". Can salvage save her?
- "Five of eight" and "or" are different rules. Say whether jobs clash by date.
- The 212 are fixed whatever you do, in a game about your hands changing the count. Make 212 the floor and let lost hulls add to it.
- Do your own spells count? A nova through a barge is either the best joke in the game or a named death. Pillar 3's comedy depends on the answer.

## New notes, most important first

1. **The spectacle needs a camera we don't have.** Seen from sixty miles, horizon-wide walls of light, a thousand-foot gate, a mile-long sounder: top-down shows one screenful. Direction: fronts read as light and shadow crossing the arena. Vistas happen on the plotting floor's inlaid map, plus a few painted cards. The last sounder is fought in screen-sized parts.

2. **The finale punishes you three times.** Bare posts, fewer fen days and the harder gorge all compound, so the weakest party gets the hardest fight. Direction: tune for about half the stations. A missing device removes an option and adds no enemies. The gorge is different, not worse. State that being caught lowers the count and is not a game over.

3. **Power now competes with people.** A trip back costs a stretch, and second attunements and shrines are all behind trips. Either rescuers arrive underpowered or nobody returns during the year. Direction: tune the main path without them, and make one return per leg free.

4. **The largest branch is blind.** Ottoline against the seed sledges decides the key, before anything says a key exists. Without her there is no lock-chamber hold, so nothing sets the fen's days. Direction: plant the key early, and give the gorge route its own hold that sets them.

5. **The full kit gets the least game.** The last three legs get nine of about 24 stretches, right when hands are strongest. The fen has no asks, jobs, fights or gains at all. Direction: shrink the jobs offered, not the playtime, and write the fen.
