# Minions & clones

## One-liner
Summoned or projected entities that act on your behalf: an echo totem that repeats your last spell, or a clone you play through while your body stays behind.

## Why it's interesting
- The clone gives you lower personal risk at reduced power: scouting, rescuing, or reckless plays.
- The totem adds a second source of your spells, which supports preparation.

## Costs & risks
Games rarely get summons right (they become passive DPS, add clutter, or play themselves). The user is hesitant but wants to explore this.

## Clone trade-off (user, 2026-11-03)
The clone is weaker, but its death has smaller consequences. This matters more because death is harsher (see `death-and-revival.md`). A vulnerable real body isn't the intended hook.

## Where it stands (2026-11-05)
- **The Double is built** (pure Sigil on yourself): a copy of you that enemies target more, follows you, and repeats your spells (one-shots and channels) at half power for half their runes. It binds one Sigil rune ([Sigil's identity](../decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd); [Play-test notes, round 1](../decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build)). In Survive, the Echo power-up gives a free Double until the next wave is cleared ([Survive gets Rally and Echo](../decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-gets-rally-and-echo)).
- **The echo totem was dropped** in favour of the Double, and the Echo focus weapon was later replaced by the Arcane wand ([Round-2 ratings built](../decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-ratings-built-new-retreats-and-passives-the-arcane-wand-mend-carrying-slows-you)).
- **Not built:** a clone you play through while your body stays behind. The Double is a decoy and echo, not a body you control.

- **2026-11-11 (spell audit):** the user isn't a fan of the Double and wants something cooler in its slot (Sigil alone on yourself). The Double stays until a replacement is chosen.
