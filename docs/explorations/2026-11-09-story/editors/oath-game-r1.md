# Game editor notes: In Front of Everybody, round 1

The engine can work. As drafted it fires three times in 35 hours.

## Notes, most important first

1. **Oaths fall due only at the midpoint, the ford turn and the ending.** The pitch is "coming back to pay", but the first four regions pay nothing back for about fifteen hours. Give each main oath instalments on return: a small due job, sized to what you can now cast.

2. **Only the ford rule lives in the hands, and it's the best thing here.** Make it the system. Small oaths become sworn restrictions and jobs built from the shrine kits: "no Lightning in the mill valley", "keep this lantern lit". This answers your stated weakness and makes roughly forty side oaths affordable, as templates.

3. **The final walk has too few states.** The midpoint can't be broken, tow day is always missed, and only ford and river vary. That's four worlds. "Stays due until settled" also contradicts the same-week clash. Let the player choose at the midpoint which oath to abandon, and let small oaths move most gates.

4. **The dyke needs rules.** Each device binds a Sigil rune and pools cap near 5, so five devices a player. Use this: everything left on the dyke is missing from your Hearing duel, so promise and spell budget become one decision. Unsolved:
   - What do turrets shoot at in a flood? Name the threat and the spells that stop it.
   - The player has had this art for minutes. Add a rehearsal.
   - Stretches must scale with party size.

5. **Four players will want to split up** between dyke and Hearing. State it plainly: one ferry, one party. The Hearing needs a party rule: who counts as stepping off.

6. **The Gains read as unlocks that don't exist.** All seven elements and the full queue work from hour one, so "the beam", "freeze, shatter" and "first real walls" are already castable. State each region's growth as what becomes sustainable: "shatter twice per fight".

7. **Return gates break against the fixed order and free pools.** Cold is attuned last, so Cold roads arrive post-game. Element gates would open at pool 1 on the first visit. A player who never deepened Earth is locked out of "deeper Earth". Gate on regen steps, or allow two answers per gate. Paired "equal paths" double content, so limit them to ford and river.

8. **Foresight should pay.** Sharp players see the river coming. Let return work change how the loser loses, never which side: Rain banked in the cistern moves the painted number and buys evacuation time.
