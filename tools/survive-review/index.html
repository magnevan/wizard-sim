Survive Review

Survive is the endless wave mode (#survive in the game, the 3 key). You and up to three friends hold a walled arena against waves that never stop. Between waves, three altars rise and each of you walks onto one to take a power-up for the rest of the run. When everyone is down at once, the run is over.

Everything on this page is already built, except the three power-ups marked Designed. The numbers are read from the game’s code today. Rate each power-up ★ Keep, ? Maybe or ✗ Cut, and answer the open questions at the bottom. Any question you leave blank means you accept Claude’s recommendation, which is marked on each one, along with what the game does now. In the scenes, red is you and blue is your partner. Click a scene to pause it.

The arena and the loop

    The arena
    48 × 34 units, about four times the Arena’s floor, so it never fits on screen and the camera follows the team. 40 segments of old ruin rock that never breaks: a walled keep round the altars (four 3.6 u doors), causeways west and east, six gate funnels at the far edges with a portal behind each, and cover out in the field.
    The waves
    Portals open at the gates farthest from the wizards and from each other, one enemy type per gate, a second apart. Off-screen enemies and open portals get marks on the edge of the view.
    Altars and picks
    Three altars, three different power-ups from the run’s dice. Stand on one for 0.75 s to take it; two players may take the same one. Your colour lands on the plinth and the badge appears by your name.
    Going down
    Downed wizards are out until the wave ends (unless someone holds Rally). They get up in the breather with half their health.
    Run end
    Everyone down at once: THE ARENA FELL, with waves survived and everyone’s picks. Space starts a new run.

    The pick. Three altars rise in the keep, each with its glyph and name. P1 stands on Pyre, P2 on Glass heart; standing fills the ring in your robe’s colour, then a pip of your colour lands on the plinth and the badge appears top left. The strip says who is still choosing.

    The wave curve. Eight authored waves add one enemy question at a time. Counts are ×1.3 for the big arena and a quarter more per extra wizard; past wave 8 the eight loop with 35% more enemies each time. From wave 4 every wave adds 7% enemy health and 4% damage.
