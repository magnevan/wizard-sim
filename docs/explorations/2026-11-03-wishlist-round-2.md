## Patterns in the user's judgments
1. **The user thinks in base mechanics plus variants, not in a list of spells.** Tripwire, effigy and lattice are all "rune, written differently". Contingency is "prepared spell plus auto-trigger". Lattice is "prepared spell plus rune", which could be an *implicit combo*. Quiver is "similar to other variations". This argues for a small set of composable base mechanics, as in Magicka, where these spells fall out as combinations rather than being authored one by one.
2. **Mechanically compound spells get cut.** Kindred Step was "two spells mechanically", and Hourglass Mark was confusing. A good spell does one readable thing; compound effects should come from *combining* simple ones.
3. **Redundancy gets cut.** Sticky Beacon and Timed Sigil were too close to existing ideas.
4. **The camera is pseudo-2D.** Lobbed arcs don't read well in this view, which is a constraint for the art direction too.
5. **"Magicks" stay a separate category**, for special spells (co-op Magicks like Clothesline, a resurrect ritual).
6. **Wind came up as a possible element** (Bellows Gust).
7. **Structures placed in the world** are preferred over enchanting another player (Conduit Sigil → "a structure you put out"). This matches Prism and Shatter Bulwark.
8. **Weapons as a variant axis:** Bulwark as "a weapon variant", which fits Magicka's weapon cast slot.
