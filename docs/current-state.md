## Controls and spell grammar
- **Spells are proposal C:** form × cast + payload. The strongest element picks the form (Shield / Sigil > Life > Earth > Arcane > Lightning > sprays); the others are payload. 39 cells, 678 distinct spells, 0 inert additions in the Atlas (`node tools/spell-atlas/audit-c.js`). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Seal becomes an element](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-seal-becomes-an-element-steam-and-ice-dropped-see-explorations2026-11-03-structures-review-and-elementsmd).
- **Keys:** Q/A Water/Lightning, W/S Life/Arcane, E/D Shield/Sigil, R/F Cold/Fire, G Earth (opposites stacked; T reserved for Air). Same decision.
- **The look follows the carried element** (Earth + Fire is a fireball); status shows on the body, never as icons. D: [The look rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-look-rule-ice-shards-turret-draw-the-users-feedback-on-the-vfx-gallery-and-pixel-battlefield-pass-3).
- **Runes queued = magic; nothing queued = your hands (grammar v2):** RMB tap Strike, RMB hold Heavy, Shift+RMB Special (some Specials are held: Siphon, Mend), Shift+LMB Retreat, Space Defend (hold to block, tap as a hit lands to parry). D: [Weapons review, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapons-review-round-1-explorations2026-11-05-weapons-review-1md), [Grammar v2 in the game](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Esc clears the queue if it holds runes; with an empty queue it opens the Esc menu** (Start / Options on a gamepad): Resume, Scenarios, Multiplayer (the lobby), Controls, Settings (scale, lights, debug line). Offline the menu pauses the sim; online the game keeps running and your wizard stands still. The 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 keys work in it too (offline), and a gamepad's D-pad moves it. D: [The Esc menu and the scenario registry](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-esc-menu-and-the-scenario-registry), [Final-pass review](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-final-pass-review-rules-text-room-switches-and-shared-keys).
- **The help panel (F2,** the "?" button, or D-pad up) shows what the queued runes do, one row per gesture with a live preview; with nothing queued, your hands' moves. Numbers are behind a toggle (Shift+F2); with them on, each spell row also says what one more rune of its lead element would change ([Rune scaling audit](decisions/README.md#2026-11-07-rune-scaling-audit-four-fixes-and-a-1-rune-line-in-f2-explorations2026-11-07-rune-scalingmd)). The spell text is the Atlas's own (`rules-c.js` describes the mixes, the weapons and the guards as the game builds them; [Final-pass review](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-final-pass-review-rules-text-room-switches-and-shared-keys)). D: [The help panel](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-help-panel-f2-explorations2026-11-06-help-panelmd).
- **Casting while moving** is allowed, at reduced speed while channelling or charging. D: [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Life spells only heal;** the element lending the shape sets the size, Life sets the strength. D: [Life spells, the size rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-life-spells-the-size-rule-the-inert-audits-life-group).
- **Heals lend resistance:** every non-Life element in a heal lends the healed its guard strengths for 4 s (any body, enemies included). D: [Every element in a heal lends its resistance](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-every-element-in-a-heal-lends-its-resistance-guard-ties-settled).
- **Friendly fire is on;** the sculpting wand (pure Sigil Weapon) makes your spells spare allies (but not burning ground). D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any).
- **Placed areas linger as long as they look:** Water Place is **Rain**, which falls for 5 s, each extra Water adding 1 s and 0.4 u of radius. It soaks and douses whoever is under it, and keeps its puddle topped up. Fire patches burn for 10 s; frost and ice sheets last 16 s; steam lasts 6 s; a Healing spring lasts 4 + 2n s. Each extra rune of the lead makes a Place patch 0.4 u wider (geysers and springs too) and its steam and floors last 20 % longer; Discharge keeps its +1 u reach a rune and auras don't grow. Novas, strikes, blasts and geysers stay instant. Floors (water, ice, fire) fade out over their last second, as does everything else that lingers. D: [Lingering effects last as long as they look](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-lingering-effects-last-as-long-as-they-look), [Floors fade; the consistency audit](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-floors-fade-the-consistency-audit), [Rune scaling](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rune-scaling-the-audits-recommendations-accepted).

## Runes and regen
- **About 4 runes per element** (Fire 5, Sigil 3) in the Sanctum and the Arena; **Survive starts at 2 (Sigil 1)** and refills every pool when a wave is cleared. Reserved when queued, spent on cast, refunded on Esc or a cancel. Channels are free for 5 s, then cost upkeep. D: [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any), [The rune economy](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-a-shared-regen-budget-with-a-floor-explorations2026-11-05-rune-economymd).
- **Regen is one shared budget:** R = 1 rune a second in total, split among the pools that aren't full by their max. A quarter of it (α) always reaches every pool that isn't full, so a lone empty pool refills fast and "one short everywhere" still refills. Attune (Arcane on yourself) multiplies R by 2.5 for 6 s; the wand's Siphon by 4. The HUD's refilling pips glow brighter the bigger their share. D: [The rune economy](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-a-shared-regen-budget-with-a-floor-explorations2026-11-05-rune-economymd), [built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-built).
- **Stuff costs once, energy costs upkeep;** external drains never take you below a reserve. D: [Resources and battlefield](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefield-see-explorations2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefieldmd).
- **Per-form stacking:** sprays grow with the square root, rocks with n^1.5, beams last longer (a beam's form element counts once, Life too); the rest, Arcane included, +70 % per extra rune. Ice shards each fly at 0.75 of the payload. Water's own spray and splash push, 6 u/s a Water rune. `npm run scaling` measures every ladder. D: [Balance tooling](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-balance-tooling-and-the-first-measurements), [Rune scaling audit](decisions/README.md#2026-11-07-rune-scaling-audit-four-fixes-and-a-1-rune-line-in-f2-explorations2026-11-07-rune-scalingmd).

## Shield and Sigil
- **Shield is Model A:** barriers and wards drain the Shield pool while they stand; at the floor, the oldest breaks. Earth walls are stuff (paid once). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd).
- **Sprays and arcs stop at walls:** rock and barriers end a spray and block anything behind them, and a spray spreads along the wall it hits; arcs and their jumps don't pass walls. D: [Sprays and arcs stop at walls](decisions/README.md#2026-11-09-sprays-and-arcs-stop-at-walls).
- **Walls:** Place draws a wall (no drag: a quick arc), Force a forward wave, Area a small full-strength ring. Rock stands 25 s (+5 s per extra Earth). Double-tap Shield over your wall to drop it. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Wards and auras on allies:** Self lands on the ally under the cursor only for a ward (Shield) or an aura (Sigil + elements). A caster may keep up any number of wards. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Grammar v2; wards for support](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Sigil binds:** every device binds one Sigil rune until it goes off, breaks or is dismissed; with none free, a new one recalls your oldest. Double-tap Sigil on a creation to dismiss it. D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).
- **Sigil devices:** turrets (hold Force to aim, shown by a preview; planted at the cursor on release, at most 9 u away; cast the real spell, uncharged, half price, all together at most 13 % of your regen R from their main element), glyphs and line glyphs, orbitals, the Lens (pure Sigil Place), Blink (pure Sigil Force, to the cursor), auras (Sigil + Self), the Double (pure Sigil Self). D: [Play-test round 2, answers](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-play-test-round-2-answers-and-notes-811), [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).

## Weapons and guards
- **Weapons** (Sigil + Weapon; ~30 uses; no two-handers; carrying costs 5 % speed): Tide trident, Healing hands (Healing link; Special **Mend**, held), Frost spear (javelin), Lightning whip, **Arcane wand** (bolt, charged piercing bolt; Special **Siphon**, held), Flame sword, Stone maul; without one, the staff. D: [Round-2 weapons and guards built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-weapons-and-guards-built-into-the-game-on-the-recommendations-pending-the-users-round-2-ratings), [Round-2 ratings built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-ratings-built-new-retreats-and-passives-the-arcane-wand-mend-carrying-slows-you).
- **Every weapon blow is a physical hit plus the weapon's elements** (Strike, Sweep, the staff's shove; per-weapon damage, knockback, cone and reach in `ARMS.blow*`). The trident's Strike is a narrow, long, hard-shoving thrust. Blows are drawn as the weapon thrusting or spinning, and a held RMB shows a charge ring once it's past the tap window. D: [Weapon blows fixed; the imbued burst](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapon-blows-fixed-the-imbued-swing-becomes-a-burst).
- **Weapons are the cheap option:** each weapon has its own Strike rhythm (`ARMS.strikeRecovery`, 0.35 s for the hands to 1.4 s for the maul), drawn as its follow-through. A spammed Strike sits near half the DPS of the same element's spell (wand 55% of the Arcane beam; the flame sword is the exception, because burning sets a floor). Every extra element on a weapon or guard shows on it and in its moves (`art/accent.ts`); the wand's bolts change with each extra. D: [Weapons are the cheap option; extras you can see](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapons-are-the-cheap-option-a-strike-rhythm-per-weapon-and-extras-you-can-see).
- **The imbued burst:** a Weapon cast with elements but no Sigil or Shield releases them all at full power in a short cone in front of you (2.5 u, +0.25 per extra rune); nothing stays in hand. Same decision.
- **Eight guards** (Shield + Weapon), one per lead element: Mirror buckler, Bulwark, Ember targe, Rime kite, Spark ring, Tide shield, Prism, Leaf aegis. Each has its Defend, Retreat, passive and resistances while carried. Same decisions.
- **Mixing:** the strongest element leads by type (Life > Earth > Arcane > Lightning; else the most of Water, Fire, Cold, ties in that order). Extras bring their passive and resistance row (half per rune, all at 2). The Leaf takes only the extras' strengths; other guards' extras are a coat that bites whoever hits the block. D: [How elements mix in guards](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-how-elements-mix-in-guards-merged-with-the-users-identities), [guard ties settled](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-every-element-in-a-heal-lends-its-resistance-guard-ties-settled).
- **Every guard rune past the first** adds 15 % guard HP and Retreat distance; a Prism parry returns the beam mixed with its coat. D: [Mixing ratings](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-mixing-ratings-every-rune-scales-the-prism-mixes-its-return).
- **Resistance is hyperbolic:** damage taken is 1 / (1 + r); a weakness takes 1 − r; r = 3 still blocks the element's status. D: [Guard resistance is hyperbolic](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-guard-resistance-is-hyperbolic).
- **The Prism is a Capacitor:** it stores what its raised block takes; lowering it with 10+ charge fires the Lance (7 u, 8 + charge, carrying the coat). D: [The Prism stores and Lances](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-prism-stores-and-lances-mend-and-siphon-take-their-extras), [Round 4 ratings](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-4-ratings-the-lance-prism-is-adopted).

## Rooms
- **Rooms come from LDtk maps** (`game/content/maps/world.ldtk`, one `.ldtkl` file per level; LDtk 1.5.3). Each level's Terrain IntGrid (unpainted floor, `1` rock) and Entities (PlayerStart, Dummy, Portal, Altar, free Rock segments) compile with `npm run content:build` into `game/packages/sim/src/rooms.gen.ts`, plain data the sim imports; `npm run content:check` validates the schema, references, placement and reachability and fails while the module is stale. The Sanctum, the Arena, Survive and the tutorial's chamber are ported with no change (every room's bot runs hash the same as before); the tutorial's per-beat layouts are still code. Each room has a hash, and the lobby refuses a page with different maps. How to edit: `game/content/README.md`. D: [Map pipeline, stage 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-map-pipeline-stage-1-the-rooms-are-ldtk-maps-explorations2026-11-06-map-pipeline-stage-1md).
- **Scenarios are one registry** (`game/packages/client/src/scenarios.ts`): the Esc menu and the 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 keys switch between them in place (no reload; an open lobby stays open). D: [The Esc menu and the scenario registry](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-esc-menu-and-the-scenario-registry).
- **The tutorial** (`#tutorial`, key 4; `#tutorial-N` starts at beat N) has all nineteen beats in one chamber: your hands (Walk, Strike, Heavy, Retreat, Defend with a training buckler, the hands test), then one rune at a time through all nine elements and every cast (Fire, Water on yourself, Steam, Cold, Opposites, Lightning, Freeze and shatter, Earth + Fire, Life, Shield walls, Arcane, Sigil), then a two-wave test, then an end card whose arches go to the Sanctum, Survive or the Arena. Each beat lights an exit arch when done. Beats with enemies start calm: they walk in through far gates once you step on the ready stone, or after 8 s. From beat 7 the whole rune bar shows, locked elements as empty sockets that fill in as they're given. An "I know this" stone skips a beat (until enemies are in), and you faint instead of dying. All its text is placeholder in `client/src/tutorial-text.ts`, waiting for the user's lore. Personal beats count when any one player does them, for now. D: [The tutorial prototype](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-tutorial-prototype-explorations2026-11-06-tutorialmd), [Final-pass loose ends](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-final-pass-loose-ends-and-code-quality), [The tutorial, pass 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-tutorial-pass-2-explorations2026-11-06-tutorial-pass-2md).
- **The Academy (art test)** (`#academy`, key 5): a mock room for judging art in play, not a mode: a hall with a classroom end, furnished with image props from the asset registry (`game/content/assets/props/`). **O** cycles the prop set (`lean`: PixelLab's angled props, `simple`: the same reduced, `code`: straight-on code-drawn; `?props=`), **I** toggles a more detailed wizard (`?wizard=hd`, any room), and a label says which is showing. These are client-side only. Banners, shelves and plinths are local variants of paid-for images. D: [The mock academy room](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-mock-academy-room-explorations2026-11-06-art-trial-round-2md).
- **Outdoor art is in the registry, not in a room:** 104 PixelLab props (`tools/outdoor-kit/`): fences, a gate, a pell, a sword rack, trees, farm and ruin props, and 64 small scatter pieces at half size. The game has no floor painter yet, so its bought ground exists only in the review page's picture. D: [Outdoor art](decisions/README.md#2026-11-07-outdoor-art-tier-1-and-painted-ground-explorations2026-11-07-outdoor-art-kit-resultsmd).
- **Props are the `Prop` entity in LDtk** (asset id, blocks or not). A blocking prop's footprint compiles to static rock the client doesn't draw (`propRock`), so it stops bodies and shots and has no other gameplay. Props depth-sort with the actors. Same decision.
- **A ground painter exists, and no room uses it yet:** a client-only Ground layer in LDtk (twelve materials, each with its own edge style, plus alternates and scattered props), painted per pixel through the camera. Rooms with no Ground cells keep the code-drawn floor. How to edit: `game/content/README.md`. D: [The ground painter](decisions/README.md#2026-11-09-the-ground-painter-and-the-first-general-art-for-the-opening-campaign01-openingspacemd).
- **Room walls can come from a bought kit** (the Academy's do; how: `game/content/README.md`). Far walls stand tall and near walls are cut low. Other rooms keep the drawn walls.
- **Scene lighting:** a mood per room (`?mood=day|dusk|night|interior`, `?lighting=0` for off), cast shadows, lamps on props, a colour grade. See `explorations/2026-11-09-lighting.md`. Walls stop light and their faces catch it, and spells light along their length. It hasn't been measured on a real graphics card.
- **The Sanctum** (default): three practice dummies, with the tuning panel (F1). **The Arena** (`#arena`): waves from portals, lulls and breathers. **Survive** (`#survive`): a 48 x 34 walled arena, endless waves. D: [Survive mode in the game](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-mode-in-the-game-explorations2026-11-05-survive-modemd), [Survive grows to 48 x 34](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-grows-to-48-x-34-guards-read-from-behind-a-stalled-page-no-longer-drops-the-others).
- **The camera follows your own wizard, in every room,** at one fixed scale (no zoom-out), stopping a little past the arena's edge; where the arena fits the screen it only drifts toward you. Other wizards off screen get an arrow at the edge in their seat colour (blinking while downed), beside the enemy and portal marks. Several local seats on one screen share it: the camera aims at their middle, and an arena that fits is framed still. D: [Survive Review answers, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-review-answers-round-1).
- **Every enemy's attack reads in four beats:** a telegraph pose held for the whole wind-up (goblin-sized bodies crouch and lean back), a strike frame with a smear, an impact or release (the brute's crack, debris and shake; the imp's spit of flame; the archer's string snap), and a recovery. A ring round a grabbed wizard closes as the throw nears. Drawn purely from the sim's attack state.
- **Ground warnings only where they're needed:** none for archers (the bolt's flight is the warning) or imps (their reach is short); a faint cone for the shieldbearer; the goblin's red cone and the grabber's aim line as before. **The brute's slam is a travelling shockwave:** its fists hit the floor just ahead of it and a front rolls out at 14 u/s, hitting each wizard inside the locked 2.1 u circle as it reaches them (the far side about 0.3 s after the fists); rock breaks and the impact lands when the front reaches the centre. The circle is a faint outline. D: [Attack warnings trimmed; the brute's shockwave](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-attack-warnings-trimmed-the-brutes-shockwave). D: [Enemy attacks drawn](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-enemy-attacks-drawn-explorations2026-11-06-enemy-attacksmd).
- **Downed wizards are out until the wave ends** and get up at half health in the breather. In Survive, everyone down ends the run, and **anyone can Rally** a downed ally mid-wave: stand within 1.6 u for 3 s, hands free, and they get up at 40 % (your own actions empty the ring, a hit drains 1 s). D: [Survive Review answers, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-review-answers-round-1), [Rally: a hit drains the ring](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rally-a-hit-drains-the-ring-instead-of-emptying-it).
- **Survive's pick has no time limit:** it ends once every wizard has picked. The next wave's portals open during it but release nothing until the wave starts. Same decision.
- **Survive power-ups:** between waves, each player stands on one of three altars. Twelve are built: **+1 to three** (then tap three different element keys, not Shield or Sigil), Deep (element) (+2 to one), Wellspring (+15 % R), **+1 Shield and +1 Sigil**, Bedrock, Pyre, Mirror charm, **Clockwork** (turrets bind no Sigil rune), **Echo** (until the next wave is cleared, every spell goes off again 0.4 s later, free; three times as likely before the 5th and 8th wave of every eight), **Glass heart** (+25 % damage dealt, −25 % max health, up to two stacks), **Lingering** (what you place lasts 1.5× as long, 1.25× as wide) and **Quickstep** (a permanent +15 % Haste, two stacks). Slipstream and Bloom are cut (Triage is proposed for Bloom's place); Rally is no longer a power-up. D: [Build optimistically](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-build-optimistically), [Survive Review answers, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-survive-review-answers-round-1), [The rune economy, built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-built), [Survive power-ups, pass 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-survive-power-ups-pass-2-the-maybes-explorations2026-11-06-survive-powerups-2md).

- **Cold and water:** any Cold hit on a body already wet freezes it. Water + Cold in one spell is ice: it hurts and chills without soaking, so it can't set up its own freeze, though it does freeze a target that was already wet. Chill slows an enemy's wind-up and recovery as well as its walking. D: [Cold freezes the wet](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-cold-freezes-the-wet-ice-doesnt-soak-chill-slows-attacks).

## Sound
- **Sound is going depth first, an element at a time:** each element gets a kit, with its own arrangement for each job, judged in the Sound Lab in the game (`?lab=fire` on the dev server). On the dev server the game uses a local baseline of reference sounds wherever one exists, and our own elsewhere; each is replaced as ours are approved. The public game still plays stage 1, and has none of the baseline. D: [Sound goes depth first](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-sound-goes-depth-first-explorations2026-11-06-sound-depth-firstmd)
- **Sound is built the way spells are:** each element is a layer, each cast shape a structure (nine of each), and they compose, so a partner's spell can be read by ear. Nobody has heard it yet: it's waiting on the Sound Gallery's ratings. D: [Sound, stage 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-sound-stage-1-explorations2026-11-06-soundmd).
- **The engine** is client-only, in `game/packages/client/src/audio/`: sim events and state drive it, and the sim didn't change. One-shots come from shown steps, late remote events play after the replay, and sustained sounds follow the state and fade. Same decision.
- **Every sound varies** by rune count, room (hall, cave or outdoor reverb), walls in the way, pan and distance from the camera, and random pitch and clip; a limiter keeps crowds pleasant. Same decision.
- **The sounds** are 65 tuned clips from Kenney (CC0) and Wikimedia Commons (public domain or CC0), plus code-made ones. Sources and licences: `game/content/audio/README.md`. Same decision.
- **Sound starts on your first input.** Master, Effects and Music (reserved) volumes are in the Esc menu's Settings. `?sound=0` turns it off. Same decision.
