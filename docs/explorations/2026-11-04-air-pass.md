## 1. Where Air goes

### Key: T
Logged on 2026-11-03: opposites stack vertically (Q/A, W/S, E/D, R/F), Earth sits on G, and Air would go on **T**, above Earth. The keyboard becomes two clean rows of five:

| Row | Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 | Column 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top | Q Water | W Life | E Shield | R Cold | **T Air** |
| Bottom | A Lightning | S Arcane | D Sigil | F Fire | G Earth |

Five pairs, one per column. That's the symmetry the user liked: every element has exactly one opposite directly below or above it.

### Colour: jade `#249070`
The current nine colours were measured in Lab (CIE76, the same ΔE as the Sigil note: Sigil vs Arcane comes out at 73.0, matching the log). A small node script searched the RGB cube for the colour farthest from all nine.

The palette is crowded where Air "wants" to be. Wind is usually drawn pale and whitish, but Cold (`#aeefff`) and Shield (`#ffe08a`) already own pale:

| Candidate | Nearest (ΔE76) | 2nd | 3rd | Min CIEDE2000 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| White `#ffffff` | Cold **23.7** | Shield 47.0 | Earth 51.9 | 18.2 (Cold) | Fails. Reads as Cold. |
| Turquoise `#40e0d0` | Cold **30.8** | Life 45.3 | Shield 67.0 | 16.6 (Cold) | Fails. |
| Chartreuse `#d8fc00` | Life 56.9 | Shield 57.3 | Earth 82.4 | 18.5 (Shield) | Passes ΔE76, but it's the lime that was rejected for Sigil as "too close to Life", and the pixel art already uses lime for goblins. |
| **Jade `#249070`** | **Life 45.1** | **Cold 48.6** | **Earth 54.4** | **25.2 (Life)** | **Passes (> 45).** Every other element is farther than 54. |

For comparison, the closest pair already in the palette is **Water / Lightning at ΔE76 31.7** (CIEDE2000 25.5), and Shield / Earth is 34.9. So jade, at 45.1, would not be the weakest link; it clears the bar by a little and is as far from its neighbours (CIEDE2000 25.2) as Water is from Lightning.

**Costs of jade:**
- It is darker than most elements (Lab L 53; only Sigil is as dark, the rest are 60–91). On a dark battlefield it needs a light rim, the same treatment Sigil got in the pixel art.
- In the world, wind will still be drawn as pale, translucent streaks. The jade is the rune colour and the tint of those streaks. **Pale gust streaks and pale frost spray are the readability risk**, not the HUD colour. The VFX would need Air to be read by its *motion* (fast streaks, curling eddies), and Cold by its *particles* (flakes, rime).

### Cancels: Air ⟷ Earth, and Earth stops cancelling Lightning
Air cancels Earth (body against wind; Earth is weight, Air is lift). The question is the old Earth ⟷ Lightning cancel, which in Magicka meant "earth grounds lightning" *(unverified)*.

| | Keep Earth ⟷ Lightning | Drop it (recommended) |
|---|---|---|
| Queue | Earth has two opposites (Air, Lightning); Lightning has two (Water, Earth). | Five clean pairs, one per key column. |
| Grounding | Lives in the queue. | Moves into the world, as the overnight draft already proposed: chains don't jump through or over rock walls, and stone armour shrugs off a chain. |
| New spells | none | **Earth + Lightning** opens: a rock that chains to a neighbour on impact, a charged bulldoze (whoever you shove is shocked), stone armour coated in lightning (melee attackers are shocked). That's **+135 spells** on its own. |
| In the hands | A Magicka player's habit stays. | A Magicka habit breaks, but the rule "each key's opposite is the one above or below it" is easier to learn than "plus one extra cancel". |

### Form priority: Air leads only when it's alone ("the bellows")
Today: Shield / Sigil > Earth > Arcane > Life > Lightning > the sprays (most numerous wins; ties Water, Fire, Cold). Three places Air could sit:

| Placement | What it means | Air row cells | Distinct spells | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top**, beside Earth | Air is a form like the rock. Fire + Air is a gust carrying fire; Arcane + Air is a gust, not a beam. | rich | similar | Air would eat the beam and the sprays whenever it's in a spell, and almost never be a payload. Throws away what Air is good at. |
| **Spray tier** | Air joins Water / Fire / Cold; the most numerous leads. 2 Air + Fire is a gust with burn; 2 Fire + Air is a flamethrower that shoves. | 6 spells each | 1246 | A real "count your runes" lever, but the two halves read almost the same on screen (a cone of fiery wind vs a cone of windy fire). |
| **Bottom (recommended)** | Air leads only when nothing else is queued. Otherwise it is always a payload that rides on the form. | 1 spell each | 1226 | Mirrors Earth: **Earth is always the body, Air is always what carries it.** Opposites at both ends of the priority list. |

So Air is a **payload element with a small form row of its own** (pure Air). It is not a spray element, though pure Air's Force cast is a cone like the sprays.

This interacts with S2 (one shape change per spell, from the leading payload; ties by form priority): at the bottom of the list, Air loses every tie, so its shape change only applies when Air is the payload you put *most* runes into. Its shove (the status) always applies.

## 2. Air's payload trait: **Gust**

In the overnight frame (one status, at most one shape change, interactions):

| | Air (draft) |
|---|---|
| **(a) Status** | **Shoved.** Whatever it hits is pushed hard along the spell's direction (away from the centre for Area). A body shoved into a wall or another body takes an impact, which counts as a **heavy hit**. |
| **(b) Shape** | **Carry.** The spell picks up what's loose in its path (clouds, mist, steam, flames, burning ground, loose arrows) and carries it along. Sprays reach further. This is Bellows Gust as a payload. |
| **(c) Interactions** | **Fans burning**: a burning target hit by Air burns hotter for a moment and spreads to neighbours (allies too). **Carries and disperses clouds**: Air moves the scald mist, poison and smoke the way it blows, or thins them out if it's an Area cast. **Frozen + shoved**: the frozen body slides, and shatters when it hits a wall or a body (impact = heavy hit). **Knocked down + Air**: shoved along the ground (this moves over from Water's entry in the matrix). **Can't move stone**: earth walls, stone armour and a bulldozing caster aren't shoved. |

**Not chosen:**
- **Lift** (launches targets into the air). In a pseudo-2D top-down view height reads poorly; the user cut Lobbed Shell for exactly that. It would also be a fourth "out of action" status next to frozen, knocked down and stunned.
- **Dries the wet.** Fire already dries. Two dryers would blur both. Air stays out of wet.
- **Air + Lightning** has no interaction ("air doesn't conduct"). Air stays a small element: one status, one shape, a few interactions.

**What happens to the other shoves.** Three elements would now displace bodies. To keep them apart:

| Element | Displacement | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Earth | **Drops you where you stand** (knockdown) | unchanged |
| Air | **Moves you** (shove) | new |
| Water | **Wets you** | Water's payload **loses its shove** and becomes a status element like Fire. The Water *spray* (the form) keeps a light push, because a jet of water pushing is Magicka's feel *(unverified)* and part of the form's geometry, not the trait. |
| Shield (pure wave) | — | The pure Shield wave hands its shove to Air and becomes a **Mirror wave**: it turns the shots it sweeps back at their source, and doesn't shove. That matches "Shield alone is a mirror." |

## 3. The Air row, and Air in every form

From the scratch rules (titles are working names).

### Pure Air (Air leads)
| Cast | Spell | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **Gust** | A wide, fast cone of wind. Shoves everything in it and carries clouds, flames and loose arrows along. The Bellows Gust leaf: blow an ally's fire patch onto the enemy. |
| Area | **Whirlwind** | Wind bursts out from you and throws everything back. The panic button. (Water's Splash ring would stop pushing and just soak, so the two don't duplicate.) |
| Place | **Dust devil** | A small whirlwind at the cursor that wanders a little and **pulls** clouds and light bodies into it, then spits them out. The only pull outside the Arcane hook; a co-op set-up (gather them, then the rock). |
| Weapon | **Gust swing** | One swing that shoves (the plain Weapon column, as in Magicka). |
| Self | **Gust step** | Blows you a few steps toward the cursor: a short dodge. On an ally: blows them a few steps away from you, a rescue nudge. |

### Shield + Air (barrier)
| Cast | Spell | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **Gale wave** | A wall of wind sweeps forward and breaks. Everyone it passes is shoved back hard; clouds and arrows are swept along. The user's "air would shove" wave. |
| Place | **Wind curtain** (arc or drawn) | You can push through it, but it shoves bodies back as they cross, and slows them to a crawl. **Shots pass.** Beams through it pick up Gust and push what they hit. |
| Area | **Ring of wind** | A closed cyclone around you that keeps bodies out. Shots pass. |
| Weapon | Buckler | Unchanged (Shield's Weapon column is already thin; a "fan buckler" whose bash shoves is the obvious Air version). |
| Self | **Anchored ward** | "Resists air": you can't be shoved, by enemies or by friends' gusts. A deliberate way to avoid a teammate's Gale wave (pillar 3). |

The curtain fills a gap in what barriers block:

| Barrier | Blocks bodies | Blocks shots |
|---|---|---|
| Pure barrier | no | **yes** (reflects) |
| Wind curtain | **yes, softly** | no |
| Rock wall | yes | yes |
| Elemental curtain | no (it hurts to cross) | no |

### Sigil + Air (device)
| Cast | Spell | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **Gust turret** (a bellows) | A fan planted along your aim that blows down a lane: shoves enemies back and carries clouds and flames along it. Pairs with an ally's fire patch or steam cloud (pillar 7: set it up in a lull). |
| Area | **Wind orbitals** | Orbs that each shove away the first enemy that comes close, then are spent. Release flings the rest. |
| Place | **Updraft glyph** (and line glyph) | Goes off when an enemy steps on it and throws them back the way they came. A line glyph across a choke throws back whoever crosses, once. |
| Weapon | **War fan** | Wide sweeping swings that shove everything in the arc. |
| Self | **Gale aura** | A steady wind around you pushes enemies away and blows clouds off you. |

**On the aura, carefully.** The tempting version is a wind aura that **deflects arrows**. That's defence, and defence is Shield's job (the log: "An aura affects those around you... Defence is Shield's job"). It would also bend allies' shots and spells passing near you. Gale aura only pushes bodies and clears clouds. It still overlaps Bulldoze (Earth's aura), but they are opposites in feel: **Bulldoze is "I go to them" (contact), Gale is "they can't come to me" (radius).** They can't be combined anyway, since Air and Earth cancel.

### Air as a payload in the other forms (Force cast, unless noted)
| Queue | Spell | What Air adds |
|---|---|---|
| Fire + Air | Flamethrower, fanned | Shoves, carries the flames further, fans burning (a firestorm: Spellbreak's wind + fire). Also the easiest way to set a friend on fire from far away. |
| Water + Air | Water spray, driven | The shove comes back to water through Air: a squall that soaks and shoves. |
| Water + Fire + Air | Scalding spray, carried | The scald mist is blown onto the enemy instead of hanging between you. |
| Cold + Air | Frost spray, driven (blizzard) | Chilled targets are shoved; frozen ones slide and shatter on walls. |
| Lightning + Air | Lightning arc that shoves | Chain, then shove. (Air doesn't change the chain.) |
| Arcane + Air | Pressure beam / concussive blast | A beam that pushes and pins enemies against walls; Arcane blast with Air throws everyone outward. |
| Life + Air | Life beam that pushes | Under L3 (Life leads, others lend shape), this is the rescue nudge: heal the ally and blow them out of danger. It would take over the nudge the overnight draft gave Water. |
| Earth + Air | **cancels** | |

## 4. What it costs

### Hands and eyes
- **Ten keys.** QWERT / ASDFG. Magicka used eight (QWER / ASDF) *(unverified)*. With Earth already on G, the index finger now covers **R, T, F and G**, four keys, two of them a stretch. Movement is on the mouse, so WASD isn't taken, but T is the least comfortable key in the set.
- **Gamepad.** Five pairs map more naturally than four-plus-one (one direction per pair), but ten elements is still more than Magicka's gamepad layout handled *(unverified)*. Not a blocker.
- **A tenth colour and a tenth rune pool on the HUD.** The rune HUD grows by a quarter.
- **Pale streaks next to pale frost** (see the colour section).
- **"It implies a lot of new effects"** (the user, round 3). This pass keeps Air to one status (shove), one shape (carry) and four interactions, and moves two existing shoves to Air instead of adding a third. The new world effects are clouds that move, and bodies that hit walls. Moving clouds is the expensive one: every cloud has to know about wind.

### Overlaps, and how this draft resolves them
| Overlap | Resolution | Cost of the resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Water's push | Water payload stops shoving; the water spray keeps a light push. | Changes Water's trait (it becomes a status element). |
| Earth's knockdown | Drop vs move: different verbs, and Air can't move stone. | none |
| Shield's shove wave | Pure Shield wave becomes a Mirror wave; Gale wave is the shove. | Changes a cell the user leaned towards. |
| Bulldoze vs Gale aura | Contact vs radius; they cancel anyway. | Two "keep them off me" auras. |
| Haste vs Gust step | Haste is sustained speed; Gust step is one short dodge per rune. | A dodge speeds play up (pillar 4). It costs an Air rune each time, which limits it. |
| Arcane hook vs Dust devil | Both pull, but the hook is one body to you, the dust devil is a group to a spot. | none |

- **Cells: 39 → 43.** Air gets its own Force, Area, Place and Weapon cells. Self is shared (the plain self cast gains Gust step; Shield and Sigil self gain the ward and the aura).
- **Distinct spells: 678 → 1226 (+81%).** Air alone adds 413; dropping the Earth ⟷ Lightning cancel adds 135. Every Shield and Sigil cell goes from 45 to 81 payload sets, Earth from 18 to 27, the Arcane / Life / Lightning / spray rows double.
- **Inert additions stay at 0** in every variant. Air at the bottom always changes a spell (it adds Shoved), and anything added to pure Air takes the form.
- Almost a doubling of the space is the real cost. Most of the new spells are "the same thing, but it shoves", which is a useful word, but it's the same word 400 times.

## 5. Options

### A. Air as a full tenth element (as drafted above)
T key, jade, cancels Earth; Earth stops cancelling Lightning; Air leads only alone; trait Gust (shove + carry). Water payload loses its shove; the pure Shield wave becomes a Mirror wave.
- **Gains:** the symmetry (five pairs on five columns); Bellows Gust; a wind curtain that blocks bodies but not shots; an anti-shove ward; a dodge; moving clouds as a co-op set-up (one player makes steam, the other blows it). Earth + Lightning opens.
- **Costs:** a tenth key on a stretch, a tenth colour close-ish to Life and Cold, pale-on-pale VFX against frost, +81% spells, clouds that move, and changes to Water and the Shield wave.

### B. No Air: the shove stays where it is
Water keeps Soak (wet + shove); the pure Shield wave keeps the shove; Earth keeps cancelling Lightning. Bellows Gust becomes "water spray carries clouds" or is dropped.
- **Gains:** nine keys, nine colours, nothing to relearn, and the space stays at 678 spells while Shield, Life and stacking are still being settled.
- **Costs:** no symmetry (Earth is the one element without a clean opposite, with Lightning as a half-opposite); no wind curtain, dodge, anti-shove ward or moving clouds. Optionally, drop Earth ⟷ Lightning anyway for +135 spells (Earth + Lightning combos), without Air.

### C. Air replaces an element
The only element Air could plausibly replace is **Water**, since they share the push. But Water's real identity is *wet*: the lightning combo, the freeze mix, and three ★ spells (Frost Bridge, Puddle Glyph, Tidal Wave). Air can't carry those. Replacing anything else breaks a pair (Cold/Fire, Life/Arcane) or a form (Lightning's chain, Arcane's beam). **Not recommended**; listed so it isn't proposed again without that cost in view.
- *A variant considered:* since opposites cancel and can never share a queue, one key could hold both (tap G = Earth, hold G = Air). It keeps nine keys and the user likes tap vs hold. But it hides an element behind a timing, makes Air slower to queue than every other element for no design reason (pillar 4: a spell's strength shouldn't hinge on how easy its keys are), and breaks the "opposite is above or below" layout. Not recommended either.

| 7. Preparation | + The bellows turret and updraft glyphs reward setting up a lane. | neutral |

### Claude's recommendation: A, but not yet
- **A is the better game**, mostly because of what Air does *with others*: carrying an ally's steam or fire, the wind curtain that blocks bodies but not shots, and the clean five-pair keyboard. "Earth is the body, Air is what carries it" is a rule a player can learn in one sentence.
- **Wait until the open `f-` drafts land.** Air touches all three: it takes the shove from the Shield wave (Part 2), it would be the Life rescue nudge (Part 3, L3), and its place at the bottom of the priority matters for S2's tie-break (Part 1). Adding it now would mean redoing those answers.
- **Two pieces can be decided independently, now or later:** (1) drop Earth ⟷ Lightning in the queue and move grounding into the world; (2) Water's payload loses its shove. Both make sense with or without Air.
- If the user doesn't want the extra key at all, **B** with the Earth ⟷ Lightning change is the fallback.

**Smallest test, if A goes ahead:** one corridor, a fire patch and a steam cloud already on the floor, a pack of melee enemies. Does a player with Air (Gust, Gale wave, wind curtain) find "blow their fire onto them" and "shove them into the wall" without being told, and can a partner read gust streaks apart from frost in the same frame?
