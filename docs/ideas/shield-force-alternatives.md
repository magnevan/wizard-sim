# Shield + elements on Force: the spells we didn't pick

## One-liner
The spell families proposed for Shield + elements on Force in the spell audit (2026-11-11), kept for later. The user chose Blink with a parting gift instead: the elements' Area spell goes off where you stood. That choice won on clarity, at the cost of less spell diversity.

## The candidates

**Bubble** (the user's favourite of the rest: "a cool sort of hold person effect… the flavor of a powerful spell caster being able to just completely seal someone weaker"). A shot that seals the first body it hits in a sphere for a few seconds. Nothing gets in or out, and the elements act inside. On an enemy, a prison: Fire cooks, Cold freezes, Lightning shocks, Earth a stone shell. On an ally, a sanctuary: Life heals, Water douses. Big bodies break out sooner, as with stun. The bubble runs on the Shield battery, so hits on it drain the caster's pool. A partner can pop it with an element that reacts with what's inside (Lightning into a Water bubble). The trade-off is built in: what's inside can't be hurt from outside either.

**Repulsor and Counterspell, as one spell** (the user: "very attuned to what shield is", though maybe overlapping the imbued burst). A held field in front of you that stops shots and pushes bodies back, coated in its elements. It soaks incoming spells of those elements into your battery, and on release sends them back as one shot. It overlaps the Bulwark guard (blocking), Water's push, and the Prism guard (store and return).

**Shield chain.** A chain that hooks the first body it hits and pulls it to you, with the elements riding along the chain: yank a caster out of its pack, or pull a downed ally to safety.

**Guardian link.** Hold Force on an ally: damage of the link's elements that they take goes into your battery instead. It overlaps the ward tossed on an ally, but it follows them as they move.

**Blink variants not picked:** the elemental step (each element its own way to travel, like the guards' Retreats: Earth a leap that knocks down, Water a surge, Arcane phases through walls, Life blinks to an ally), the arrival ward (you land wrapped in a brief ward of the elements), a burst where you land, damage along the path, and a swap (trade places with whatever is under the cursor).

## Why they might come back
Shield is the most open slot in the grammar. If Shield + elements on Force ever feels thin next to the other casts, the Bubble is the first to try. The Repulsor/Counterspell could also become a guard or a Magick.

## Costs & risks
Bubble: a hard lock, so it needs body-size scaling and battery drain, or it trivialises single big threats. Repulsor/Counterspell: overlaps two guards. Chain: less obviously Shield.
