## Morning notes (in chat)
1. **Combos that don't change the spell:** Shield + Weapon is Bulwark, and adding elements on top only changes it for Ice and Lightning. Claude measured this: in A and A.1, **about one in three additions of a new element changed nothing** (3,199 of 10,395).
2. **Point targets are fragile:** pointing on ground, ally and enemy each mean something different, so a slight misclick (just outside an ally) turns Stoneskin into a Geyser. Suggestion: use the Self key + cursor. Near an ally it goes on them, otherwise on you.
3. **"What is force cast vs point?"** Force starts at you and travels along your aim, so the first thing in the way catches it. Point appeared at the cursor, skipping everything in between.
4. **Biggest flaw in the gallery:** spells are shown as one-offs, but each should be seen as a part of the full set that the inputs make possible. **"Can you try to fully explore the tree?"**
