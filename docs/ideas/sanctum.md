# Sanctum (practice hub)

## One-liner
A shared tower between missions for experimenting with spells: dummies with readouts, a sandbox room, time control, and challenge scrolls.

## In the hands
Walk in, cast at dummies that show damage and status, reset the room, and try combos with no pressure. Friends can join.

## Why it's interesting
It answers "no time to experiment in battle" and makes experimentation social. It's also a natural home for the grimoire (character development).

## Costs & risks
It could feel like homework, so it should be optional and fun on its own terms.

## Open questions
- Should the hub also hold progression (grimoire, loadouts), or stay purely a playground?
- Will players just skip it? An alternative is slow-paced exploration sections within the campaign itself.
- Parked by the user until much later.

## Smallest test
This is whatever prototype comes first. Every prototype is effectively a sanctum room.
*(Since 2026-11-04 the game's default room is the Sanctum: three practice dummies, with the tuning panel (F1) for readouts, spawning and time control. It's a test room, not the shared hub this idea describes, which stays parked.)*
