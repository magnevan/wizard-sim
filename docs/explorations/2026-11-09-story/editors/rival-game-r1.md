# Game editor notes: Your Go, round 1

Ranked. The engine works as a game if the slate measures the right thing.

1. **The walls score the wrong thing.** Runes spent per head ignores regen and pools, your main growth, so "beat it with magic you didn't have" is mostly false. The cheapest run is one conjured blade and thirty free Strikes: the main pull rewards not casting. Re-sitting is undefined too: the caravan has arrived. *Direction:* each well chalks what its people care about (goose health, inches lost, friends hit), plus the queue. A re-sit is next year's job on the same ground.

2. **Regions 1 to 3 give only numbers, and withhold tools they use.** Plumb's exam needs rock walls (Shield + Earth). Windfall's keeping shrine needs wards. Both are Shield's wider arts, which arrive at Twice, around hour 30. *Direction:* walls at Plumb, wards at Windfall, one verb per early region. Bet's gift keeps something rarer.

3. **The Dunnocks leave before they fight beside you.** The first joint fight comes after Hob has gone. By Twice only Bet is left, so "late on, you and the Dunnocks fill the screen" happens once, in the finale. *Direction:* Hob on the field in the goose escort, healing you through your own fire. Departed Dunnocks answer a summons for one late fight per region.

4. **The copybook starves good players and rewards throwing spars.** *Direction:* record any queue that touches you, anywhere. Scope the AI now: four authored repertoires growing per region, chosen by simple rules. Ines's counter is a lookup on the party's most-cast element.

5. **Party size is unsolved.** Bet alone is swarmed by four players; a solo player among four Dunnocks is a spectator. *Direction:* scale spars through what the opponent has prepared (more turrets on Bet's peak), never health. Fix one rule at Plumb. Slates need a line per party size.

6. **Longlegs may ignore the player twice.** If the sluice goes on a timer, a hands-first game has scripted its midpoint. *Direction:* every stray spell damages it, and whoever breaks it is named. Then the dawn defence puts a brand-new, rune-hungry art at the hardest moment, against pillar 4. Give it three tide lulls, one new device each.

7. **The bridge is a delivery chore,** and "the rest follow in training" is a menu. *Direction:* each return is a defence on the newest span, and each span teaches one art by needing it. What you built stays standing.

8. **The Forest/Volcano swap contradicts the supper.** If the Dunnocks take the other region, nobody shares Windfall's table. *Direction:* one joint supper at the arch, where Hob announces it.

**Do not touch:** Bet's opening spell and its minute of waiting, which teaches regen; Ines's and Hob's return spars, which make leaving create content; the final queue that throws you off the bridge.

**Ban list:** the supper impression is near 32. The reprinted timetable is near 49.
