Field Atlas

A world generated the way the fiction says it formed: land, mountains, rivers and climate first; the seven fields from those; then towns where fields meet, named by the pair they sit on. Every field holds ground of its own, so you can read which is strong without a legend. Chart paints it like an old fantasy map, with made-up names and every mountain, forest and town drawn from the world’s data. Reroll the seed, switch the look, and mark the worlds that feel right.

For the paint pass

A paint model keeps the layout but garbles small lettering. So For painting is the map with no names, banners or key: run that through the model. Lettering is everything written, on a transparent layer, to lay back on top afterwards. Right-click or long-press the map to save it (3000 × 2400).

The prompt below is written for the world on screen: it names each landmark by where it sits and what it is, so the model doesn’t reinterpret it. Change the seed or cut a landmark and it updates. Give it to the model together with the For painting image.

Landmarks

What each field leaves on the land, with a reason to be there. They’re drawn plainly on purpose: clear shapes for the paint pass to work over. Mark Cut and it leaves the maps; Keep what feels like this world.

How the fields are placed

Each field is a number per spot on the map, derived from the land. A spot is in a field's zone when it's strong enough; zones overlap where they like, and towns grow where two are strong.

Fields-first generator

Our own code: the fields come from the land, and the towns from the fields, so the map argues for the world's premise. Any seed is a candidate world; a kept seed can be rendered at any size or style. Needs you once

Azgaar base, our overlay

Generate and hand-edit a continent in Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator (free, in the browser), export its data, and this page reads the heights, rivers and climate from it and lays the fields on top. To build the importer, it needs one real export file from you; its format is unverified until then.

Later

Painted over a kept seed

Pick a seed here, export it large, and paint the final map over it (by hand, a painting tool, or image generation once a description is approved). The generator decides where things are; the painting decides how it looks.
