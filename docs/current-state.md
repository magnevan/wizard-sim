## Controls and spell grammar
- **Spells are proposal C:** form × cast + payload. The strongest element picks the form (Shield / Sigil > Life > Earth > Arcane > Ice > Lightning > sprays); the others are payload. 43 cells, 944 distinct spells, 0 inert additions in the Atlas (`node tools/spell-atlas/audit-c.js`). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Seal becomes an element](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-seal-becomes-an-element-steam-and-ice-dropped-see-explorations2026-11-03-structures-review-and-elementsmd).
- **Ice is a combined element:** Water + Cold make one Ice rune (either order; cancels are checked first). Fire cancels Ice. It has no key or pool: it costs a Water and a Cold, and takes one slot. Its spells are Ice shards (the fastest missiles), Ice ring, Ice sheet and Ice burst; Earth and Arcane carry it as an Ice boulder or an Ice beam. Its identity is damage, quick flight and spikes: 16 a rune, with a little chill (half Cold's per damage); it never soaks, and freezes only the already wet. Shield + Ice is a solid spike wall, Sigil + Ice the Ice bow and the Spike aura. Steam is still a mix (Water + Fire). Element id 10 in the game. D: [Ice is an element again](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-ice-is-an-element-again-water--cold-combine), [Ice's identity is damage](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-ices-identity-is-damage-a-spike-wall-the-ice-bow).
- **Keys:** Q/A Water/Lightning, W/S Life/Arcane, E/D Shield/Sigil, R/F Cold/Fire, G Earth (opposites stacked; T reserved for Air). Same decision.
- **The look follows the carried element** (Earth + Fire is a fireball); status shows on the body, never as icons. D: [The look rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-look-rule-ice-shards-turret-draw-the-users-feedback-on-the-vfx-gallery-and-pixel-battlefield-pass-3).
- **The rings are each spray element's strongest instant hit on its axis:** the Frost ring chills twice as hard as a hit would, the Splash ring pushes twice as far; the Fire ring's burst already is. D: [Spell audit, step 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-2-part-fire-floors-burn-by-the-second-the-rings-are-each-elements-strongest-instant-hit).
- **Each single-element Area cast is one connected surface going outward** (`game/packages/client/src/art/novas.ts`): fire a wave of flame, water a rolling swell, cold a serrated ice front that shatters, life a veil of light, arcane a hard hoop. Earth and Lightning keep their own effects. D: [Novas are one surface](decisions/README.md#2026-11-10-novas-are-one-surface-not-a-row-of-pieces).
- **Casts with runes queued:** RMB Force; Shift+RMB Place (on release; drag to draw); Space Self; Shift+LMB the burst or a conjured weapon or guard. **The ring has no button of its own:** it's Shift+LMB released with the cursor on yourself (within `SHAPES.selfAim`, 1 u); for Shield and Sigil, whose Shift+LMB conjures, Shift+RMB released on yourself raises their ring, orbitals or motes. A Shield Place reads your intent from the press and the first move: pressed within 3 u of you it's the arc (the ring on you) to the end; pressed further out, a first move straight toward or away from you is the arc (and the ring if it reaches you), any other way a line, which stays a line even when it comes near you. A Sigil Place moves between its line and its motes or orbitals as the cursor goes on and off you (`placeOnYou`; issues #15, #16). An elemental Place there lands at your feet. Holding Shift+LMB shows the burst's shape on the floor to everyone, and it goes off on release; guards and weapons still conjure on the press. Pads have no ring gesture yet. Code: `sim/src/cast.ts` (`onYou`, `burstArms`). D: [The ring merges into the burst](decisions/README.md#2026-11-22-the-ring-merges-into-the-burst-aim-at-yourself-for-the-ring-explorations2026-11-21-ring-burst-mergemd).
- **Runes queued = magic; nothing queued = your hands (grammar v2):** RMB tap Strike, RMB hold Heavy, Shift+RMB Special (some Specials are held: Siphon, Mend), Shift+LMB Retreat, Space Defend (hold to block, tap as a hit lands to parry). D: [Weapons review, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapons-review-round-1-explorations2026-11-05-weapons-review-1md), [Grammar v2 in the game](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Esc clears the queue if it holds runes; with an empty queue it opens the Esc menu** (Start / Options on a gamepad): Resume, Scenarios, Multiplayer (the lobby), Controls, Settings (scale, lights, volume). Offline the menu pauses the sim; online the game keeps running and your wizard stands still. The 1 to 8 keys work in it too (offline), and a gamepad's D-pad moves it. D: [The Esc menu and the scenario registry](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-esc-menu-and-the-scenario-registry), [Final-pass review](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-final-pass-review-rules-text-room-switches-and-shared-keys).
- **F8 files an issue** (or Esc › Resume › Report an issue): a one-line box over the game (it pauses offline). Enter opens a new GitHub issue pre-filled with the note, the room, the checkpoint, the build's commit and the Stats panel's rows, and the frame is on the clipboard to paste in. `packages/client/src/report.ts`; how the backlog works: [tasks](tasks.md).
- **The help panel (F1,** the "?" button, or D-pad up) shows what the queued runes do, one row per gesture with a live preview; with nothing queued, your hands' moves. Numbers are behind a toggle (Shift+F1); with them on, each spell row also says what one more rune of its lead element would change ([Rune scaling audit](decisions/README.md#2026-11-07-rune-scaling-audit-four-fixes-and-a-1-rune-line-in-f2-explorations2026-11-07-rune-scalingmd)). The spell text is the Atlas's own (`rules-c.js` describes the mixes, the weapons and the guards as the game builds them; [Final-pass review](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-final-pass-review-rules-text-room-switches-and-shared-keys)). D: [The help panel](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-the-help-panel-f2-explorations2026-11-06-help-panelmd).
- **Casting while moving** is allowed, at reduced speed while channelling or charging. D: [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Life spells only heal;** the element lending the shape sets the size, Life sets the strength. D: [Life spells, the size rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-life-spells-the-size-rule-the-inert-audits-life-group).
- **Heals imbue resistance:** every non-Life rune in a heal builds 0.75 resistance to its element (its guard's strengths, by row) in whoever it heals, any body, enemies included; it decays 10 % a second plus 0.02 flat, so it ends (about 20 s after a strong heal). Shown as the elemental ward's outline in the colour of what it resists most ([Shield art pass](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-shield-art-pass-coated-fields-the-imbues-skin-the-stored-spell), [Fix: heal sprays](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-fix-heal-sprays-build-the-imbue)). Water in a heal shoves back only enemies; Cold heals as a Cooling spray; Ice lends the Healing shards. Life beam ×1.5, Healing ring about 25 a Life, palm about 30, Chain heal halved. D: [Spell audit, step 5](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-5-life-the-imbue-builds-and-lingers-heals-tuned-by-effective-healing).
- **Friendly fire is on;** the sculpting wand (pure Sigil Weapon) makes your spells spare allies (but not burning ground). D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any).
- **Placed effects** can change the ground for a time. Rain soaks and douses, a Fire patch burns, and a Healing spring heals in pulses. Water, ice and fire floors fade out as they expire. See [Spell balance](systems/spell-balance.md) for where to check durations and scaling.

## Runes and regen
- **About 4 runes per element** (Fire 5, Sigil 3) in the Sanctum and the Arena; **Survive starts at 2 (Sigil 1)** and refills every pool when a wave is cleared. Reserved when queued (and a queued rune doesn't regenerate until it's cast), spent on cast, refunded on Esc or a cancel. An element you have less than a rune of queues as a ghost that all your regen fills; a cast waits for its ghosts to be paid, then winds up. Channels are free for 2.5 s, then every rune in them costs upkeep (see per-form stacking below). D: [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any), [The rune economy](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-a-shared-regen-budget-with-a-floor-explorations2026-11-05-rune-economymd).
- **Regen is one shared budget:** R = 1 rune a second in total, shared equally among the pools that aren't full, with two shares for an empty one (under a whole rune); while a ghost rune is queued, all of it fills the ghost. D: [Rune rework, part 3](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-rune-rework-part-3-equal-regen-shares-two-for-an-empty-pool-no-debt). Attune (Arcane on yourself) doubles R for 3 s a rune of Arcane, stings 16 HP a rune, and leaves you taking 25 % more damage while it lasts; the wand's Siphon by 4. The HUD's refilling pips glow brighter the bigger their share. D: [The rune economy](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-a-shared-regen-budget-with-a-floor-explorations2026-11-05-rune-economymd), [built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-rune-economy-built).
- **Stuff costs once, energy costs upkeep;** external drains never take you below a reserve. D: [Resources and battlefield](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefield-see-explorations2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefieldmd).
- **Per-form stacking:** sprays grow with the square root, rocks with n^1.5, beams +70 % a rune like Arcane's other casts, Life beams full healing a rune; past 2.5 s every rune in a channel costs 0.4 a second of upkeep from its own element ([upkeep per rune](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-channels-pay-upkeep-per-rune-so-beams-buy-power-one-missile-speed)); the rest, Arcane included, +70 % per extra rune. Ice shards each fly at 0.75 of the payload. Water's own spray and splash push, 6 u/s a Water rune. `npm run scaling` measures every ladder. D: [Balance tooling](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-balance-tooling-and-the-first-measurements), [Rune scaling audit](decisions/README.md#2026-11-07-rune-scaling-audit-four-fixes-and-a-1-rune-line-in-f2-explorations2026-11-07-rune-scalingmd).

## Shield and Sigil
- **Shield is Model A:** barriers and wards drain the Shield pool while they stand; at the floor, the oldest breaks. Earth walls are stuff (paid once). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd).
- **Novas** (single-element rings) briefly follow a moving caster. Scorch, frost and wet marks are visual. Compare them in the VFX Lab as a film strip: `/vfx-lab.html?spell=fire&form=area&times=0.04,0.12,0.25,0.45,0.8,1.3` (`&move=1` walks the caster, `&spells=water;life;cold;arcane;fire` a row per element).
- **Place:** Shift+RMB shows the placement reticle at once and places on release. Cold creates freezing fog. Ice (Water + Cold) places caltrops that deal damage as targets move through them. [Ice Place decision](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-3-ice-hits-like-two-runes-a-dense-shotgun-caltrops)
- **Beams reach 30 tiles** (past the view's edge) and grow with the spell's runes: thicker, surges racing along, a halo from three, a white-hot core from four. Every missile flies at 20 u/s (ice shards still reach 7.2 u). The Earth form's missiles each have their own flight: a meteor (Fire), a snowball comet that ends in a chilling frost puff (Cold), three ice shards leaving one after another at 32 u/s, together 1.7 rocks (Water + Cold), a charged stone that bursts (Arcane), a seed of light (Life), a tumbling rock with a dust wake; each grows with the spell's runes; compare them in the VFX Lab: `/vfx-lab.html?spells=earth,fire;earth,cold;earth,water,cold;earth,arcane;life,earth;earth&grid=runes&ns=1,3,5&t=0.15`.
- **The VFX Lab** (`/vfx-lab.html` on the dev server) casts any spell through the real sim and view, in a scene (`scene=empty|crowd|dark|coop|ally`), frozen at any time (`t=`), as a 1 to 5 rune grid (`grid=runes`), a row per spell (`spells=`) or a film strip (`times=`), with the sim's hit shapes dotted over it (`hit=1`); its URL options are listed at the top of `game/packages/client/src/lab/vfx-lab/main.ts`. How effects are made and reviewed: [vfx-workflow.md](vfx-workflow.md).
- **Sprays look like streams:** Water, Fire and Cold sprays share one particle model (`art/spray-jet.ts`): each particle keeps the aim it left with, so a sweep bends like a hose. The drawn fan is the sim's hit cone. More runes look bigger and hotter, and four and five runes add features of their own. Compare them in the VFX Lab: `/vfx-lab.html?spells=water;fire;cold&grid=runes&t=0.6` (`&sweep=1` swings the aim, `&hit=1` dots the sim's hit cone). See `explorations/2026-11-10-spray-vfx.md`.
- **Sprays and arcs stop at walls:** rock and barriers end a spray and block anything behind them, and a spray spreads along the wall it hits; arcs and their jumps don't pass walls. D: [Sprays and arcs stop at walls](decisions/README.md#2026-11-09-sprays-and-arcs-stop-at-walls).
- **Walls:** Place draws a wall (no drag: a quick arc at the cursor, centred on you, 3.9 u long; pressed within 3 u of you moving the cursor only swings and resizes it, and pressed further out a sideways drag draws a line while a radial one resizes the arc; bring the cursor back to where the drag began and it's the arc again, and a glyph's line likewise), Place on yourself a small full-strength ring (an arc brought onto yourself is the ring; a line stays a line). Shield on Force is Blink (to the cursor, instant, paid with the Shield rune); with elements it leaves their Area spell where you stood at 0.8 power (the Fire blink leaves a Fire ring; the Life blink heals where you land). Barriers (Shield alone) are force fields: they stop the other side's bodies, shots and beams and let your team through, enemies batter them, and every blow drains the caster's Shield pool. With elements (not Earth or Ice) they're coated force fields: each rune resists its element, bites the enemies pressing on it, and lends your team's shots through it its elements; Life in it heals your team. Elemental wards resist by rune (40 / 57 / 67 %) and run on the caster's Shield pool as a battery: what they hold off drains it, and at its floor they break. The arc's length, nearest radius and no-line zone are `structures.arcLen`, `arcMin` and `arcZone` (with `arcRadial`, how straight a first move must be to stay the arc), and the ring's radius `structures.ringRadius`, in the tuning panel (F2). D: [The arc stands at the cursor](decisions/README.md#2026-11-22-the-shield-arc-stands-at-the-cursor-a-dead-zone-round-you-never-draws-a-line). Shield with Ice raises a solid spike barrier (the ice wall and Ring of ice): it stops bodies and shots like rock, but has 70 HP and stands 15 s, stabs whoever presses against it, and Fire melts it at 2.5 times the damage. The Ice ward shows crystals round the feet. Rock stands 25 s (+5 s per extra Earth). Double-tap Shield over your wall to drop it. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Wards and auras on allies:** Self lands on the ally under the cursor only for a ward (Shield) or an aura (Sigil + elements). A caster may keep up any number of wards. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Grammar v2; wards for support](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Sigil binds:** every device binds one Sigil rune until it goes off, breaks or is dismissed; with none free, a new one recalls your oldest. Double-tap Sigil on a creation to dismiss it. D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).

D: [Play-test round 2, answers](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-play-test-round-2-answers-and-notes-811), [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).

## Weapons and guards
- **Weapons** (Sigil + Weapon; ~30 uses; carrying costs 5 % speed; the Ice bow and Stone maul are two-handed: a guard rides on the back with them, keeping its passive and Retreat but not its Defend, `sim/src/guards.ts` `twoHanded`): Tide trident, Healing hands (Healing link; Special **Mend**, held), Frost spear (javelin), Lightning whip, **Arcane wand** (bolt, charged piercing bolt; Special **Siphon**, held), Flame sword, Stone maul, **Ice bow** (quick icicles; a drawn fan of three that pierces at full draw; Special **Splinter shot**; [built on the recommendation](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-ices-identity-is-damage-a-spike-wall-the-ice-bow)); without one, the staff. A weapon replaces the staff and is held in its hand, and a guard hangs on the other arm (on the back with a two-hander, its effects drawn behind the body there). D: [Round-2 weapons and guards built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-weapons-and-guards-built-into-the-game-on-the-recommendations-pending-the-users-round-2-ratings), [Round-2 ratings built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-ratings-built-new-retreats-and-passives-the-arcane-wand-mend-carrying-slows-you).
- **Every weapon blow is a physical hit plus the weapon's elements** (Strike, Sweep, the staff's shove; per-weapon damage, knockback, cone and reach in `ARMS.blow*`). The trident's Strike is a narrow, long, hard-shoving thrust. Blows are drawn as the weapon thrusting or spinning, and a held RMB shows a charge ring once it's past the tap window. D: [Weapon blows fixed; the imbued burst](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapon-blows-fixed-the-imbued-swing-becomes-a-burst).
- **Weapons are the cheap option:** each weapon has its own Strike rhythm (`ARMS.strikeRecovery`, 0.35 s for the hands to 1.4 s for the maul), drawn as its follow-through. A spammed Strike sits near half the DPS of the same element's spell (wand 55% of the Arcane beam; the flame sword is the exception, because burning sets a floor). A weapon's extras change its material, not add particles (issue #4): the first extra in the order Ice, Steam (Fire + Water), Fire, Water, Cold, Lightning turns the element-bearing part of the 3D prop into that material (the maul's head, the wand's orb, the bow's limbs, the trident's prongs, the whip's lash; `art/infuse.ts`, drawn by `art/live3d.ts`), each other extra adds one small effect at the head, and Arcane a glowing rune (`art/accent.ts` `infused`). Blows show the material's one colour. The flame sword with Water is a hilt with a jet of steam for a blade; Healing hands wear a shell of each extra's colour round their green core. A guard's extras still show as coloured arcs on its rim; the wand's bolts change with each extra. [Thread](archive/elemental-weapons.md). D: [Weapons are the cheap option; extras you can see](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapons-are-the-cheap-option-a-strike-rhythm-per-weapon-and-extras-you-can-see).
- **Presses during a recovery wait for it** (and `RHYTHM.castBuffer` after), and keep the meaning they had when pressed: a weapon press with no runes queued stays a Strike even if a rune is queued before it goes off. **A Strike's or Sweep's recovery can be cut short** by a waiting spell or Retreat once its committed share (`ARMS.strikeCommit`) has passed; another Strike waits it out (`sim/src/cast.ts`). The wizard's body eases from wherever it is into the next move (blended transitions, `art/live3d.ts` `blendPose`); the spear, trident and staff jab and then hold a ready stance for a moment; held, their Heavy coils in that stance and lunges on release; the trident's Sweep turns the whole body once with the weapon held out; the bow shoots from a drawn pose and stays drawn after a shot and while the aim is on an enemy in range, its arrows leaving from it (`art/sprites.ts` `MOVESETS`, `view.ts` `aimedAt`). The Model Viewer's ▶ sequences play these on game seconds. D: [Two-handers carry the guard on the back; cancels](decisions/README.md#2026-11-20-two-handed-weapons-bow-maul-carry-the-guard-on-the-back-recoveries-can-be-cancelled-into-casts-explorations2026-11-20-weapon-motion-flowmd).
- **The imbued burst:** a Weapon cast with elements but no Sigil or Shield releases them all in a cone toward the cursor (2.5 u, +0.6 per extra rune), at the ring's power; aimed further out it stretches smoothly, from 2.5 u to 9 u of aim, to twice the reach at 0.3 of the width (`SHAPES.imbueStretch*`, `imbueFar*`), Earth's line and Lightning's fork too; an Earth-led one is a long narrow line (4.5 u, +0.9 a rune) whatever else is infused, and a Lightning-led one forks to the 3 nearest targets at once, each jumping on once: its reason to exist is sparing the allies beside and behind you (`shapes.imbuePower`); nothing stays in hand. D: [Weapon blows fixed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapon-blows-fixed-the-imbued-swing-becomes-a-burst), [Earth line, Lightning fork](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-the-burst-grows-like-the-nova-earths-is-a-line-lightnings-forks), [Fire's one-shot casts](decisions/README.md#2026-11-09-fires-one-shot-casts-hit-up-front-the-burst-beats-the-ring).
- **Nine guards** (Shield + Weapon), one per lead element: Mirror buckler, Bulwark, Ember targe, Rime kite (Cold), Spike kite (Ice), Spark ring, Tide shield, Prism, Leaf aegis. Each has its Defend, Retreat, passive and resistances while carried. Two hurt melee attackers from any side: the Ember sets them alight (Searing), the Spike kite stabs them (Bristling), and its parry and Retreat leave caltrops. Same decisions, and [Ice gets its own guard](decisions/README.md#2026-11-17-ice-gets-its-own-guard-fire-and-ice-guards-hurt-attackers-explorations2026-11-17-guard-refinementmd). The live guards are a solid core (Meshy or Blender) with an animated shell and particles round it, carried at the left side, higher up and angled out (placed by the body, not the forearm); blocking, the wizard plays a guard clip (a buckler across the chest, or braced behind the Bulwark or Spike kite) and the guard comes square in front of him ([guards, round 3](explorations/2026-11-17-guard-refinement.md)).
- **Mixing:** the strongest element leads by type (Life > Earth > Arcane > Lightning; else the most of Water, Fire, Cold, ties in that order). Extras bring their passive and resistance row (half per rune, all at 2). The Leaf takes only the extras' strengths; other guards' extras are a coat that bites whoever hits the block. D: [How elements mix in guards](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-how-elements-mix-in-guards-merged-with-the-users-identities), [guard ties settled](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-every-element-in-a-heal-lends-its-resistance-guard-ties-settled).
- **Every guard rune past the first** adds 15 % guard HP and Retreat distance; a Prism parry returns the beam mixed with its coat. D: [Mixing ratings](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-mixing-ratings-every-rune-scales-the-prism-mixes-its-return).
- **Resistance is hyperbolic:** damage taken is 1 / (1 + r); a weakness takes 1 − r; r = 3 still blocks the element's status. D: [Guard resistance is hyperbolic](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-guard-resistance-is-hyperbolic).

## Statuses
- **Chill is a frost pool:** each Cold rune in a hit adds 8 frost to it, times the hit's scale (an Ice rune 0.6 of that), so a frost-heavy spell is a slow-heavy spell. The pool holds 2.5 × √max HP and drains 10 % of the whole pool a second, even under fire. Full chill is 60 % slower, walking and attacking alike. Cold never freezes on its own. A Cold hit on a wet body, or a Water hit on a chilled one, freezes it from 30 % chill, in either order. It lasts 1.5 s at 30 % up to 4 s when full, times 0.5 to 1 by how soaked the body is, using up the chill and the wet. Ice never soaks, so it can't set up its own freeze; Water in an ice spell doesn't soak either. Health bars show chill as a 1 px strip, brighter once it's enough to freeze. D: [Spell audit, step 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-1-shock-is-a-chance-burn-grows-with-fire-chill-fills-by-rune-explorations2026-11-11-spell-auditmd), [Cold freezes the wet](decisions/README.md#2026-11-06-cold-freezes-the-wet-ice-doesnt-soak-chill-slows-attacks).
- **Wet is a soak pool:** each Water rune in a hit soaks a body by 12 against 2.5 × √max HP; it drains 4 % a second; a puddle or Rain soaks you through. Lightning's wet bonus (up to ×2), its chains' reach (up to 1.5×) and a freeze's length scale with it, and each Fire rune dries 12 of it. Water deals 4 a rune. D: [Spell audit, step 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-2-wet-is-a-soak-pool-water-hits-for-4-steam-is-vapour).
- **Ice (Water + Cold in one rune) is damage and spikes:** 16 a rune, a little chill. Its shards are a dense shotgun (more Ice packs the fan tighter), its ring takes a one-shot blast like Fire's, and its Place is **caltrops**: spikes for about 10 s that stab only whoever walks through them, by the distance walked (a quarter of the payload a unit): good against the fast, where a fire floor is good against the slow. D: [Spell audit, step 3](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-3-ice-hits-like-two-runes-a-dense-shotgun-caltrops).
- **Close beats placed:** the Earthquake, the Arcane nova and their bursts hit 1.3×; Discharge zaps at 1.6× the payload, the Strike at 1.9×. The Lightning arc's zaps are 0.35 of the payload (it aims itself and chains), below the aimed Arcane beam. Earth is 28 a rune on n^1.3. D: [Spell audit, step 4](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-4-close-beats-placed-the-arc-trades-damage-for-aim-a-gentler-rock).
- **Steam is vapour:** Water + Fire never soaks, doesn't douse its own fire, lends no Push, and deals 0.8 of its damage and 0.75 of its burn, so it stays below Fire. Its mist doesn't hide anyone yet (open). Same decision.
- **Burning is a pool of damage that grows with Fire:** lit for 4, plus 1.5 × a hit's Fire damage and 0.15 of the rest, capped at 80, spent evenly over 4 s and refreshed by each hit. A fire floor lights you for 6 per Fire rune that made it on contact and adds 4 a second per Fire rune while you stand in it; a fire aura adds 3 per Fire rune each half second. Water or Cold wipes it. Health bars show in orange what it will still take.
- **Shock is a chance:** each Lightning hit on a body that isn't already stunned stuns it for 0.4 s with chance x / (1 + x), x = 0.85 × Lightning runes × the hit's scale × √(100 / max HP). A stun never renews, so the share of time stunned is a / (1 + a), like resistance: a one-Lightning arc stuns a 135-HP body about 39 % of the time, four Lightning 66 %; big bodies less. D: [Spell audit, step 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-11-spell-audit-step-1-shock-is-a-chance-burn-grows-with-fire-chill-fills-by-rune-explorations2026-11-11-spell-auditmd).
