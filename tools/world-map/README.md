# Field Atlas

A seeded world-map generator, the first of the map approaches (`docs/explorations/2026-11-07-world-and-progression.md`, R3.8). Land, mountains, rivers and climate come first; the seven fields are derived from them; towns are placed where two fields are strong and named by the pair (`PAIRS`), never with proper names (naming is the user's). Round 2 (`docs/explorations/2026-11-08-fantasy-map.md`) split it into `gen.js` (the world) and `chart.js` (the drawing), and looks: Chart (the painted fantasy map), Chart + fields, Fields (the diagram), and for the paint pass For painting (no lettering) and Lettering (a transparent layer to lay back on). Round 3 (`docs/explorations/2026-11-08-fantasy-map-round-3.md`) gave every field its own region and added `landmarks.js`, the catalogue of 32 landmarks. The page writes a paint-model prompt for the seed on screen (`writePrompt` in `index.html`, each landmark's wording in `LOOKS` in `landmarks.js`): update `LOOKS` when a landmark is added or redrawn. QA hash: `#seed=42&look=paint&plate=1` shows just the map at full size, for a headless-Chrome screenshot at 3000x2400.
