Training Grounds Blueprint

A first concept for the academy's outdoor training ground, drawn before any art. It starts from what students do there, then the shape and the sizes. Once it's right, it tells us what art to make. No story is decided here: names are working labels, and the lore is yours.

Marks and notes save as you go. An empty box means my pick stands.

What the place is for

The first chapter teaches your hands: Strike, Heavy, Retreat, Defend (parrying an archer's bolt with a buckler), Sweep, then a test against goblins walking in from gates. Later chapters teach runes one element at a time. So the ground has to hold:

Seven shapes it could take

Each is a different answer to how the lessons are arranged in space. The drawings show the difference: a yard ringed by stations, a scattered web, one field that changes, or a walk downhill.

The terraces, as a plan

My pick, with the best of the others inside it. The ground steps down from the academy's wall to a low gate on the river. Each terrace is a chapter of lessons, and you walk them in order on the gold route. Later you can come back to any of them.

The zones, one by one

The numbers follow the route. Sizes are in world units, first guesses to test in play.
