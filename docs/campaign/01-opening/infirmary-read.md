# The infirmary: the read (stage 0)

The concept art read as a plan: stage 0 of [making a place](../making-a-place.md). The source is `docs/reference/element-order/area-life-v2.jpg`, with `area-life.jpg` and the district map (`docs/reference/academy-harbor-district-concept.png`, where the infirmary's tents sit on the road outside the main gate) for where it sits. Written 2026-11-22.

Directions are screen directions: "down" is towards the camera.

## What the place does

Stop 1, the first cast, Life ([the brief](brief.md#the-route)). There are no enemies here. The obstacle is the overgrowth: you cut the vines off the well's conduits with your hands and blade, and the thorns cut you, so you heal yourself. Then the wounded are on cots, each needing a heal, with the combinations shown on screen. It's low pressure, at your own pace, and in co-op you split the cots and heal each other. It's about five minutes. It's also the segment's smallest test: does "fix it and it spills into you" land?

## The concept, as a plan

| | In the concept | Why it works, or doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Footprint** | A walled rectangle with a gate at the top and a gap at the bottom | It reads as a compound, but **it's the trap the courtyard fell into**: one flat box |
| **Height tiers** | None, except a sunken bath at lower right | Flat |
| **Edges** | Walls with banners all round | They have a cause, but there's no view out |
| **Routes** | A cross of paved paths from the gate past the well. The conduits (brass pipes) run from the well to the greenhouse, the stills, the cots and the bath | **The conduits are the best idea here.** They are paths drawn on the ground that show what the well feeds, and cutting the vines along them gives the fix a route |
| **Landmark** | The caged well with a green crystal, choked by vines | Strong, and it's the fix itself |
| **The scale ladder** | The well; then the greenhouse, the tents and the stills; then the cots, tables and people | Clear |
| **Beyond the edges** | Woods | Nothing ties it to the academy or the battery road |
| **Light** | Green glow from the crystal and the vine flowers, and violet cracks in the ground | The violet marks the morning's trouble, and the green marks the cure |

## What to keep in every plan

- The well as the landmark and the fix, with conduits running out from it to what it feeds: the greenhouse, the stills, the cots and the bath. Clearing a conduit is a visible line of progress.
- The cots under tents as their own area, close enough to the well to see the light come back.
- A place on the road between the academy's main gate and the battery: you pass it again on the way back (the wounded have moved).
- At least two height levels, after the courtyard's lessons (principles 3 and 18 to 22): for example the road above and the infirmary set down below it, or terraces stepping down a slope.
- Arrive from the north, on the well's axis (principle 18), so the well is the first thing you see.
- Small: about five minutes, with no fight. It should fit in about two screens by two.

## The user's answers (2026-11-22)

- **Plan D chosen:** "The layout feels good. The flow is very good, where you see the people to heal first."
- **The cots are open from the start:** "No they should be open, there are patients there and some nurses. But the equipment they're using has gone dark." So you can heal from the start. The tents' equipment (lamps, brass instruments) lights up when the well does.
- **For dressing:** "the blocking feels like everything will be angled a certain way (straight to the camera), but we don't necessarily have to generate the assets like that. The concept art has the objects at 45 degree angles and it looks quite good." Blockouts are drawn on the grid, so their boxes face the camera squarely. At the art stage, buildings and props can turn 45°, as in the concept.
