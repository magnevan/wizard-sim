# The tutorial (2026-11-06)

The user's request, overnight item 2: *"Design a tutorial for new players. I'm expecting something like where we start without any runes and learn attacks, then we get introduced to runes one at a time. It can have a mix of some text, but mostly practical elements. You can design it, and then build a prototype we can go to."*

This doc is the design. Another agent builds the prototype once the Esc-menu scenario picker (overnight item 3) lands. Nothing here is decided until the user says so. The calls Claude made are listed at the end as open questions, each with a recommendation.

Read with: `../vision.md` (pillars 1, 2, 4 and 7, and the open question about where practice happens), `../current-state.md`, `../ideas/sanctum.md`.

## What it should feel like

- **You learn by doing something that needs doing.** Every beat is a small room with a problem in it, like a bramble across the door, goblins standing in the rain, or an archer you can't reach. The new rune is the obvious tool for that problem. Text is one line at a time, and it names keys, never rules.
- **Each beat teaches one word of the language, then lets you use it.** It isn't a menu tour. The order builds up the grammar: hands, then one element, then a second element, then what happens when they meet (mixing, opposites), then the rule that the strongest element picks the shape (Earth + Fire is a fireball). By the end, a player should be able to guess what an untried combination does. That guessing is pillar 2.
- **Low stakes, never homework.** You can't die; you faint, stand up, and the beat resets. Enemies hit softly. Any beat can be skipped by standing on its "I know this" stone, so a returning player is out in a minute (pillar 1: a returning player is strong from minute one).
- **Tiny pools.** You start each element with 1–2 runes, so you feel the economy (runes reserved, spent, refilling) from the first cast, but a pool refills in a couple of seconds and nothing punishes you for running dry.
- **About ten minutes** for a first-timer who reads nothing twice (the beat timings below add up to about 10.5 minutes), and about a minute for a veteran who skips.

## Pillar check

- **Pillar 2 (a language):** the order teaches composition. Beat 9 (Steam) is the first mix. Beat 14 (Fireball) states the form rule in practice. The tutorial deliberately doesn't show every cell; the F2 panel and the Sanctum carry discovery.
- **Pillar 4 (room to think, a low-pressure space):** each beat waits for you. Only the last beat has a clock, and that's the announced-portal countdown, which is pillar 7's point.
- **Pillar 7 (preparing pays):** the final fight opens with announced portals and a countdown, and the prompt says to build before they come.
- **Pillar 3 (co-op):** two players can do it together. Heals hitting anyone is taught in beat 15, and in co-op the partner replaces the stand-in apprentice.
- **A tension worth naming:** the user's order puts Defend in the hands section, but in the game today **Defend needs a conjured guard**. With no guard, Space with an empty queue is a Self cast of nothing (`cast.ts`, `defend = s.guard[i] && …`). The design solves it with a **training buckler** handed to you in beat 5 (open question 1), not by adding a bare-handed block to the game.

## The flow

Nineteen beats in three chapters, then an end card. "Pools" means each unlocked element's max runes (rune keys: Q Water, A Lightning, W Life, S Arcane, E Shield, D Sigil, R Cold, F Fire, G Earth). Times are for a first-timer. Every beat has an "I know this" stone by its entry (see *Skipping*).

**Shared rules for every beat:**
- **The space** is one chamber (24 × 16 u, the Sanctum's size), redressed for each beat: rock (terrain walls), props, enemies.
- **Moving on:** when a beat completes, an exit arch lights on the far side. Walking through it fades the screen out briefly while the chamber redresses, and you come back in at the entry. The new beat's elements arrive with a banner ("Fire") and the pools fill.
- **Enemies** are the real archetypes, softened with the per-body multipliers the sim already has: `dmgMult` 0.25 (a goblin bite does about 1 instead of 5) and, for straw targets, `speedMult` 0 and `dmgMult` 0. Straw targets are goblins stuffed with straw: they don't move or hurt, but they flinch, burn and die as goblins do.

### Chapter 1: your hands (no runes; the rune bar is hidden)

**1. Walk** (≈15 s)
- **Goal:** move and aim.
- **Have:** hold LMB to walk toward the cursor.
- **Space:** an empty chamber with three glowing floor stones in a triangle.
- **Target:** the stones; each dims when you step on it.
- **Complete:** all three stepped on.
- **Prompt:** "Hold LMB to walk toward the cursor."

**2. Strike** (≈25 s)
- **Goal:** the basic attack and its rhythm.
- **Have:** RMB tap = Strike (the staff's shove, 4 physical, 0.6 s follow-through).
- **Space:** three straw goblins in a loose row.
- **Target:** straw goblins, 12 hp each, so three Strikes apiece.
- **Complete:** all three down.
- **Prompt:** "Tap RMB to strike with your staff."

**3. Heavy** (≈25 s)
- **Goal:** a held attack hits harder and shoves farther.
- **Have:** RMB hold, then release = Heavy (the staff's big shove, with the charge ring).
- **Space:** a straw sack, a goblin body that isn't rooted, with 999 hp. It sits 4 u from a ring painted on the floor.
- **Target:** the sack.
- **Complete:** the sack ends inside the ring, after at least one Heavy (a `move` event with the Heavy's name, from you).
- **Prompt:** "Hold RMB, then let go: a Heavy shoves hard."

**4. Retreat** (≈30 s)
- **Goal:** step out of a telegraph.
- **Have:** Shift+LMB = Retreat (a hop backwards without a guard).
- **Space:** an open floor with a Brute (`dmgMult` 0.25, `speedMult` 0.6) that walks to you and slams.
- **Target:** the Brute's red slam circle.
- **Complete:** two slams in a row that hit nobody. A slam that hits you resets the count.
- **Prompt:** "Shift+LMB hops you back. Get out of the red circle."

**5. Defend** (≈40 s)
- **Goal:** block and parry.
- **Have:** the **training buckler**: the tutorial gives you a Mirror buckler with plenty of guard HP. Hold Space to block; tap Space as a hit lands to parry. From now on Shift+LMB is the buckler's Backstep (3 u).
- **Space:** a low rock line across the room; an Archer behind it (bolt damage 0.25×, 20 hp).
- **Target:** the Archer's bolts.
- **Complete:** one parry that sends a bolt back. The Mirror reflects one, and it kills the 20-hp Archer.
- **Prompt:** "Hold Space to block. Tap it just as the arrow lands to send it back."
- **Why a given buckler:** see open question 1. The beat names it "training buckler" on the banner, and beat 16 tells you Shield makes your own.

**6. Hands test** (≈30 s)
- **Goal:** the Special, and proof you can fight with no magic at all.
- **Have:** Shift+RMB = Sweep (a full circle), plus everything above.
- **Space:** four goblins (`dmgMult` 0.25, 10 hp) that start in a ring around you and close in.
- **Target:** the goblins.
- **Complete:** all four down.
- **Prompt:** "Shift+RMB sweeps all the way round you."

### Chapter 2: one rune at a time

The rune bar appears with only the unlocked elements shown. Locked keys do nothing but give a small "dry" puff, as an empty pool does today.

**7. Fire** (≈40 s)
- **Pools:** Fire 2.
- **Goal:** queue a rune and cast it (Force), and learn that more runes make a bigger spell.
- **Space:** the exit is blocked by a bramble hedge between two rock walls. Off to the side is a second, thick bramble.
- **Target:** the **brambles**, a new static body that only fire hurts (open question 4). The hedge is five 10-hp bodies; the thick bramble is 40 hp.
- **Complete:** the hedge burned.
- **Prompt:** "Press F to ready Fire, then RMB to cast it. Thick brambles need F F."
- **What it teaches:** your staff bounces off the bramble, which says "this needs magic" without words. The thick one is optional, and it's where stacking two runes clicks.
- **The pool:** at 2, after one F F flamethrower you watch the pips refill (about 2 s at R = 1/s with one pool).

**8. Water, on yourself** (≈30 s)
- **Pools:** Fire 2, Water 1.
- **Goal:** the Self cast, and water douses.
- **Space:** the floor past the entry is a burning patch (a Fire Place patch the beat lays down) that you have to cross to reach the arch.
- **Target:** yourself, burning.
- **Complete:** your burning is ended by your own Water Self (Douse).
- **Prompt:** "You're on fire! Q, then Space: douse yourself."

**9. Steam: the first mix** (≈30 s)
- **Pools:** Fire 2, Water 2.
- **Goal:** two elements in one spell make something new. Wet things don't burn.
- **Space:** a Rain zone over a pen of three goblins (straw, 25 hp), so they're soaked.
- **Target:** the wet goblins. Fire alone just dries them; Water + Fire (Scalding spray) scalds through the wet.
- **Complete:** all three down.
- **Prompt:** "Wet things won't burn. Q + F together: steam scalds them anyway."

**10. Cold, and the ring around you** (≈35 s)
- **Pools:** Fire 2, Water 2, Cold 2.
- **Goal:** the Area tap. Cold slows.
- **Space:** open floor. Five goblins (real speed, `dmgMult` 0.25, 15 hp) come from all sides at once.
- **Target:** the goblins.
- **Complete:** all five chilled at the same moment (any chill on each), or all down.
- **Prompt:** "R is Cold. Tap Shift+RMB for a frost ring round you."

**11. Opposites cancel** (≈15 s)
- **Pools:** as above.
- **Goal:** a misqueued rune is undone by its opposite, and the rune comes back.
- **Space:** none.
- **Target:** none; this one is a drill.
- **Complete:** one cancel event (Fire against Cold, or Cold against Fire).
- **Prompt:** "Fire and Cold cancel: queue F, then R, and both are gone."
- **Why a drill:** the only beat that is one. It's 15 seconds, and it saves a lot of confusion: the next beat is Water and Lightning, which also cancel.

**12. Lightning on the wet** (≈40 s)
- **Pools:** + Lightning 2.
- **Goal:** the Place cast (hold), and the most famous combo: wet + Lightning does double damage, and the arc chains.
- **Space:** a cluster of five straw goblins (30 hp) out in the open.
- **Target:** the cluster.
- **Complete:** all five down. Lightning alone does it slowly; Rain first, then a Lightning arc, does it in two casts.
- **Prompt:** "Rain on them first (Q, hold Shift+RMB), then A and RMB."
- **What you learn by accident:** if you queue Q then A, they cancel. Beat 11 just told you why.

**13. Freeze and shatter** (≈35 s)
- **Pools:** Water 2, Cold 2, Earth 2 (Fire and Lightning stay unlocked).
- **Goal:** Water + Cold freezes the wet; Earth hits hard and shatters the frozen (double damage).
- **Space:** a puddle of water (a water floor) with two goblins standing in it (40 hp, `speedMult` 0.5).
- **Target:** the goblins.
- **Complete:** two shatters (the `shatter` event).
- **Prompt:** "Q + R makes ice that freezes wet things. A G rock shatters them."
- **Note:** in the game a *plain* Cold spray doesn't freeze a wet target any faster; only the Water + Cold mix (Ice shards) does. So this beat teaches the mix, and the user's "Cold on wet enemies" is open question 2.

**14. The strongest element picks the shape** (≈30 s)
- **Pools:** Fire 2, Earth 2, others as above.
- **Goal:** the grammar's core rule. Earth + Fire is a fireball: Earth gives the shape, Fire rides it.
- **Space:** a bramble 10 u away behind a rock line, out of the flamethrower's 4.5 u reach.
- **Target:** the far bramble.
- **Complete:** it's burned.
- **Prompt:** "G + F: Earth gives the shape, Fire rides along."
- **Why it's its own beat:** it's the most important single idea in the tutorial (pillar 2). After it, the player has the rule they need to guess the rest.

**15. Life heals whatever it hits** (≈35 s)
- **Pools:** + Life 2.
- **Goal:** heals land on anyone, friend or foe; aim them.
- **Space:** a wounded **apprentice** (a friendly stand-in body at 30 % health) sits beside a goblin that is **asleep at 1 hp**.
- **Target:** the apprentice. A Healing ring (Area) or a careless spray also heals the goblin, and a goblin healed past half wakes up and comes for you (softly). A Life beam (Force) aimed at the apprentice heals only them.
- **Complete:** the apprentice at full health. Whether the goblin woke is up to you, and either way you deal with it.
- **Prompt:** "W is Life. It heals whatever it touches, friend or foe."
- **In co-op:** see *Co-op*.

**16. Shield: walls** (≈40 s)
- **Pools:** + Shield 2.
- **Goal:** structures; Place draws a wall. Your buckler came from Shield.
- **Space:** two Archers at the far end (not reachable yet), and you with the apprentice on open ground.
- **Target:** the bolts.
- **Complete:** three bolts break on your walls.
- **Prompt:** "E, then hold Shift+RMB: raise a wall the arrows can't cross."
- **Banner on completion:** "Shield + Shift+LMB conjures a buckler like your training one."

**17. Arcane: reach** (≈30 s)
- **Pools:** + Arcane 2.
- **Goal:** the beam reaches far (12 u). Archers keep 8 u away.
- **Space:** the same two Archers, now with 25 hp each.
- **Target:** the Archers.
- **Complete:** both down.
- **Prompt:** "S is Arcane: hold RMB for a beam that reaches far."
- **The pools are tight here on purpose:** Arcane 2 runs dry mid-beam (upkeep). The F2 panel's S / Space row (Attune) is the answer, and the end card mentions it.

**18. Sigil: conjure** (≈45 s)
- **Pools:** + Sigil 1.
- **Goal:** the Weapon cast. Sigil makes things that last: a weapon in your hands, or a device on the ground.
- **Space:** two lanes of three goblins (15 hp), one lane released 4 s after the other.
- **Target:** the goblins.
- **Complete:** all six down, and at least one of them by a Sigil creation (a flame-sword blow or a turret).
- **Prompt:** "D + F, then Shift+LMB: a flame sword. D + F, then RMB: a turret."

### Chapter 3: the test

**19. The test** (≈90 s)
- **Pools:** every element at 2, Sigil 1: Survive's start, so the next step up feels familiar.
- **Goal:** put it together, and prepare during a lull (pillar 7).
- **Space:** the open chamber with two cover rocks. Portals open on the far edge with an announced countdown of 10 s.
- **Target:** two short waves.
  - Wave 1: four goblins and a fire imp.
  - Wave 2: a shieldbearer, an archer and three goblins.
  - Real damage at 0.6×.
- **Complete:** both waves cleared.
- **Prompt (lull):** "Portals open in 10 s. Build now: walls, turrets."
- **Prompt (wave):** none.
- **Fainting here:** retries the wave, as the Arena's wipe does.

**20. The end card** (≈10 s, not a beat)
- "That's the alphabet. F2 shows what any runes you hold will do. The rest is yours to find."
- Three arches: **Sanctum** (dummies), **Survive**, **Arena**. Walking through one switches scenario, the same as the Esc picker.

**Time:** chapter 1 ≈ 165 s, chapter 2 ≈ 375 s, chapter 3 ≈ 100 s. That's ≈ 10.7 min for a first-timer, inside the 8–12 target. A careful player who tries everything takes longer, which is fine.

### What the tutorial does *not* teach

These are left for the F2 panel, the Sanctum and play (pillar 2: discovery):
- wards and auras on allies;
- the other guards and weapons;
- Blink, the Lens, the Double, glyphs and orbitals;
- Attune (named on the end card);
- dismissing a creation by double-tapping;
- drawn walls by dragging;
- Siphon and Mend.

The tutorial teaches the grammar and one use of each element. Open question 6.

## How it gates

| Beat | Elements unlocked (max runes) |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | none (the rune bar is hidden) |
| 7 | Fire 2 |
| 8 | Fire 2, Water 1 |
| 9–11 | Fire 2, Water 2, Cold 2 (Cold from 10) |
| 12 | + Lightning 2 |
| 13–14 | + Earth 2 |
| 15 | + Life 2 |
| 16 | + Shield 2 |
| 17 | + Arcane 2 |
| 18 | + Sigil 1 |
| 19 | every element 2, Sigil 1 (Survive's start) |

- **Locked = max 0.** Pressing a locked key gives the "dry" puff, and the HUD doesn't draw the column.
- **When a beat starts:** pools refill to the beat's max, and any queued runes are refunded.
- **Regen stays the game's** (R = 1 rune/s shared, with the floor). With one or two pools unlocked, a pool refills in 1–2 s, which feels generous. With eight unlocked in beat 19, it's exactly Survive's economy.
- **The training buckler** (beat 5 onward) is a real Mirror buckler with tutorial guard HP (e.g. 4×). If it breaks, it's re-given at the next beat start. Conjuring your own guard (from beat 16) replaces it, as conjuring does now.

## Skipping

- **Per beat, "I know this":** a carved stone beside the entry of every beat, lit with a check-mark rune. Stand on it for 1 s (with a fill ring, like Survive's altars) and the beat completes as if you'd done it. Its pools are granted, the arch lights, and the chamber redresses when you walk through.
  - It's diegetic: no new input bit, it works the same on a gamepad, and it's deterministic (it's position and ticks).
  - It sits off the beat's path, so you don't stand on it by accident.
- **Skip all, from the Esc menu:** the scenario picker (overnight item 3) gets three entries:
  - **Tutorial**, from the start;
  - **Tutorial: runes** (starts at beat 7, for players who know the hands);
  - **Tutorial: the test** (beat 19 alone, as a quick warm-up).
  - Leaving is just picking another scenario.
  - Starting at a beat is a `createWorld` option (`tutorialBeat`), so every peer builds the same world.
- **A returning player** picks Sanctum or Survive and never sees the tutorial. One who drops in to check something walks over the stones: about 19 stones × 3 s ≈ a minute.

## Co-op

**Yes, two (up to four) players can do it together.** Everyone shares one beat, one chamber and one set of gates. A late joiner arrives at the current beat with its pools (`joinSeat` already fills to `runeMax`).

What changes:

- **Personal beats need everyone:** Walk, Retreat, Defend and Douse (1, 4, 5, 8) are skills each player should do.
  - Each seat has a done-bit, and the beat completes when every active wizard's bit is set. Spawns are per seat: Defend gets one Archer per player, each aimed at its own wizard; Douse sets everyone alight.
  - The HUD shows whose bit is set ("P2 ✓").
- **Team beats complete on the world:** a dead hedge is dead, whoever burned it. Enemy counts scale up by half per extra player.
- **The apprentice becomes your partner** (beat 15): the beat hurts every wizard to 30 % (a thorn pulse, shown as it happens) and completes when every wizard is back at full. You heal each other. A Healing ring heals you both and wakes the goblin, which is the friendly-fire lesson in its natural co-op form.
- **Steam (beat 9) in co-op:** the rain falls on the pen, which sits near where your partner walks. Steam sprayed carelessly scalds them too. No extra text; pillar 3 says the accident stays in.
- **"I know this" needs everyone on the stone,** so a veteran can't skip a newcomer's lesson by walking over it. The fill ring shows "1/2".
- **Fainting is per player:** you get up at full health in 1.5 s, where you stand. **The beat resets only if every wizard has fainted.** That's the co-op equivalent of the wipe rule.
- **Online:** the host's scenario choice builds the room for every peer, as today. Prompts are client-side text keyed off the shared beat number, so everyone reads the same line.

## The F2 help panel

The panel (overnight item 1) shows what your queued runes will cast, one row per cast variant. The tutorial leans on it rather than repeating it:
- **No auto-open.** The prompt line already names the keys. Opening a panel mid-beat is reading, not doing.
- **The first rune beat (7) ends its prompt with "(F2 shows what your runes will do)"**, once. The end card repeats it.
- **While the tutorial runs, the panel highlights the row the current beat teaches.** For example:
  - beat 7: Force;
  - beat 8: Self;
  - beat 10: Area;
  - beat 12: Place;
  - beat 18: Weapon.

  It does this by reading a constant `TAUGHT_CAST[beat]` table in the client. That's a few lines in the panel and nothing in the sim.
- **Locked elements can't be queued,** so the panel never shows a spell you can't cast yet. No extra gating is needed.

## What the sim needs

All of it stays pure and rollback-safe, following the pattern Survive set with `w.run`.

1. **A `tutorial` room.**
   - `Room` gains `'tutorial'`.
   - `ROOM_DIMS.tutorial = dimsOf(ARENA.w, ARENA.h)`, the Sanctum's 24 × 16, so `MAX_CELLS` doesn't change.
   - `createWorld(seed, players, { room: 'tutorial', tutorialBeat? })` makes no dummies and leaves `enc` off.
   - `#tutorial` and `?room=tutorial` (with `&beat=N`) in `main.ts`.
2. **`w.tut: Tutorial | null`,** null in every other room. `buffers()` pushes it only when present, so the Sanctum, Arena and Survive layouts and **the golden hash don't change**. It holds:
   - `state: Int32Array(8)`:
     - [0] beat;
     - [1] ticks into the beat;
     - [2] counter A and [3] counter B (bolts blocked, slams dodged…);
     - [4] transition ticks (the fade);
     - [5] skip-stone ticks;
     - [6] arch lit;
     - [7] wave index in beat 19.
   - `done: Uint8Array(MAX_SEATS)`: personal done-bits.
   - `faint: Int32Array(MAX_SEATS)`: ticks left fainted.
   - `tag: Uint8Array(MAX_BODIES)`: what each body is to the tutorial (0 none, straw, sack, bramble, apprentice, sleeper…).
3. **Gating through `runeMax`:**
   - `runeMax(w, i, id)` gains a branch: `w.tut ? BEAT_POOLS[w.tut.state[0]][id] : …`. `BEAT_POOLS` is a constant table, not state.
   - Everything downstream already reads `runeMax`: regen's shared budget, `joinSeat`, the HUD's pips and `poolCap`.
   - A press with a max of 0 is already a "dry".
4. **A beat table** (`tutorial.ts`, constant data): per beat, `pools`, `setup(w)` and `check(w) → boolean`, plus a `personal` flag.
   - `setup` clears non-wizard bodies, missiles, zones, surfaces, non-terrain walls and devices; refunds queues; refills pools; lays the beat's `TERRAIN` rock; and spawns tagged bodies at fixed spots.
   - `setup` draws from no RNG, or only from `w.rng` if a beat ever wants variety.
   - `check` reads state. It can also read this tick's `w.events`, which is deterministic: the events are produced inside the same step and rebuilt on every resimulation. The counters it bumps are state.
5. **`tickTutorial(w)` in `step`:** `if (w.run) tickSurvive(w); else if (w.tut) tickTutorial(w); else tickWaves(w);`. It runs:
   - the beat clock;
   - the skip stone;
   - the arch (when a check passes, light it; a wizard walking through starts the transition, and at its end `setup` runs for beat + 1 and the wizards are placed at the entry);
   - fainting;
   - the sleeper waking;
   - and, in beat 19, `tickWaves` with a tutorial wave list.
6. **New things in the world:**
   - **Bramble:** a new archetype with speed 0, no attack and `onlyFire`. Every non-fire part of a hit (physical included) does nothing, but it burns. It's needed by beats 7 and 14, and it's a good battlefield element later (open question 4). Draw it as a pixel thornbush that chars as it burns.
   - **Straw and sleeper targets:** existing archetypes with `speedMult`/`dmgMult` (already per-body) plus a tag. The sleeper wakes in `tickTutorial` when healed past half.
   - **The apprentice:** a `KIND.dummy` body on `TEAM.players`, tagged. It needs **one hook:** the dummy's 3-s reset (`effects.ts` tickHealth) skips tagged bodies. Enemies don't target dummies, so it's safe.
   - **The training buckler:** set `seats.guard = GUARD.mirror` with a tutorial `guardHp` in beat 5's setup. No new mechanics.
7. **Completion hooks:**
   - **Already observable as state or events:**
     - a body's kind (dead);
     - `burn` cleared plus a Douse cast event;
     - the `move` events `parry` and the Heavy;
     - `cancel`;
     - `shatter`;
     - chill on bodies;
     - a wizard's position (stones, ring, arch).
   - **Two need small new hooks:**
     - (a) **a bolt broken on a seat-owned wall** (a counter bump where a missile meets a wall in the missile code, when `w.tut`);
     - (b) **a slam that hit nobody** (in `enemies.ts` where the slam resolves, when `w.tut`).
8. **Fainting instead of dying:** with `enc` off, a wizard at 0 hp today just sits at 0. `tickTutorial` sees `hp <= 0`, sets `faint` for 1.5 s and restores full health. If every wizard is fainting, it re-runs the beat's `setup`. Beat 19 runs `enc` and uses the Arena's downed/wipe rules instead.
9. **Prompts in the HUD (client only):** a `PROMPTS[beat]` table, one line, drawn where the Arena's wave banner goes. It also shows:
   - the beat's counter ("2 / 3");
   - personal done-bits in co-op;
   - "Step 7 of 19";
   - the stone's fill ring and the arch's glow.

   The text isn't state; it's keyed off `w.tut.state[0]`.
10. **The rune bar** hides columns whose `runeMax` is 0. That's a small HUD change and helps everywhere.

**Determinism and tests:**
- a snapshot/restore round trip in the middle of each prototype beat;
- a determinism test that drives a scripted input stream through beats 1–3 twice and compares hashes;
- unit tests per beat: set the state to "done" and check that `check` passes, and that `setup` leaves no stray bodies;
- the golden replay is untouched (it's a Sanctum run, and `w.tut` is null there).

## Implementation plan: the first prototype

**The cut:** beats **1–9**, minus 6 (the hands test). That's eight beats: Walk, Strike, Heavy, Retreat, Defend, Fire, Water (Douse), Steam. Then the end card. It covers:
- the whole hands chapter;
- the first rune;
- the Self cast;
- the first mix, which is the moment pillar 2 has to land.

It's about 4 minutes. Play at `#tutorial`.

**Steps, in order:**

1. **Sim scaffolding.**
   - the `tutorial` room;
   - `w.tut` and its `buffers()` entry;
   - the `runeMax` branch;
   - `tickTutorial` with the beat clock, the arch, the transition, fainting and the skip stone.
   - Tests: purity, snapshot round trip, and the golden hash unchanged.
2. **The beat table for the eight beats,** with `setup` and `check` each:
   - **The straw sack:** a goblin with `speedMult` 0, `dmgMult` 0 and high hp.
   - **The bramble archetype:** `onlyFire` in `hit.ts`'s damage loop.
   - **The burning floor for beat 8:** a fire surface laid in setup.
