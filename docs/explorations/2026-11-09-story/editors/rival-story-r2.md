## New notes, most important first

1. **The summons turns rivals into helpers.** One per region means Hob's chair costs nothing, and Ines, who "never looks at you", turns up complaining. After Longlegs only Bet still competes, so the engine drains just as the slates should bite. Keep leavers as rivals from their posts: Dov's span measured against yours and chalked, Hob re-sitting Windfall by post. If summons stay, Ines answers silently, or never.

2. **The fifty-to-one has a hole.** Nothing says what happens if a good player wins an early spar. Scripted losses would betray "same keys". The score is in her handwriting, so let her rule on it: your early wins don't count, "you didn't mean it". That is a fresh offence, and it defines "meant it", which the peak leaves empty.

3. **The peak ignores its own mountain.** Twice sets up the second bolt someone must catch, then the duel has nothing to do with it. Make the spar decide who stands there, or have the bolt land mid-fight. Put the garbled second-self reveal inside the fight.

4. **The last supper is a curtain call.** Spoon, draughts, page, stay-offer and Pargeters land in one paragraph, five bows before the joke. Move one or two off the table. And state who lays it: loser lays the table, the table is the last span, so the fifty-to-one loser closes the bridge.

5. **The back half starts promising doom.** Things lying down in the ribs by the hundred, things in the tower at night, the bell at night: the plan says the world is fine, then foreshadows otherwise. Pay it small: the monks have started a rota for them too.
