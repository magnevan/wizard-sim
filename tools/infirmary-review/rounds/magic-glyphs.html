The glyph paths (round 7)

The glyph paths are a new kit feature: carved rune lines flush with the floor (you walk over them), glowing Life green with a slow pulse running out from the well, pads at their ends, medallions at bends and joins; each line can be set lit, dim or dark, so the fix can relight them one by one later. In the infirmary they run from the well to the greenhouse and the fields, to each pair of tents, and to the cliff top above the purifier's tower (only to things that use the well's power; the line along the road out was dropped). They replace the pipes (the purifier keeps its own pipe on the jetty). 0 errors.
