# Story notes: In Front of Everybody, round 1

1. **You never say anything.** Every oath's words are handed to you and all seven are mandatory, so "you swore" means "you accepted a quest". "Of course you say it" (the boatmen) is the writer speaking for the player. The ford's witness rule shows where the fun is: wording you can work. Direction: haggle the wording when swearing, and add a third state, kept to the letter. "You left" is already that state, once.

2. **The final walk has little to show.** Exactly two gates shut in every playthrough: one of mesa/fen, one of tree/furnaces. Arch and Ribs can't fail. The midpoint collision breaks nothing ("both sticks burn"). Tow day is missed in every ending and they let you in. That is four possible records. Nor do oaths compound: the headgate ignores everything before it. Direction: make one side of the midpoint truly abandonable, and let earlier outcomes decide who stands with you on the pier.

3. **The Hearing has no other side.** The parish you swore to never reacts, the pasture result is never stated, the opposing wizard is unnamed. Tobit has no beat before the last battle. Direction: Tobit on that line, sworn to the parish, apologising. Show a gate with Tobit's half nailed to it early; currently you see no broken stick until your own.

4. **The Cut arrives in the ending, as exposition.** "The player with a good memory goes cold" has nothing to remember: nothing before the midpoint links the mesa's water to the fen's river. Direction: a half-dug ditch in plain sight at the Mesa, where children play. Then the boatmen's request frightens, and the sharp player's foresight becomes the drama.

5. **Harl is solved two regions early, and nobody decided what he did.** Flint, hot iron, wet rag, then a man who walked off his shift: players connect it at the Furnaces, and the 4 confirms it a whole region later. Direction: let them walk back to the ford knowing. Marthe wants to hear what happened and the plan never says. It needs an uncomfortable answer, heard only if you bring him.

6. **The soup is generic.** The same mercy lands in either ending, and with "we managed" that is two soft landings on a story about the record holding. Direction: the shutter belongs to the family whose stretch of dyke held. If it failed, no soup.

7. **Debts don't press.** "Stays due until settled" removes dread between set pieces; regions 3 to 6 are where I'd stop caring. Direction: each well taken while a debt is unpaid visibly raises its price.
