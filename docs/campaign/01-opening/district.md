# The opening's district: the space diagram of the walks

Stage 3 for the walks between stops (2026-11-22). It sets where each stop sits, how long each road is, and where the small fights go. The walks' shape (leave, middle, arrive) and their moments are in [the weapons and walks exploration](../../explorations/2026-11-22-weapons-yard-and-walks.md#2-the-walks), approved 2026-11-22. The courtyard's exits are as the user approved: the battery's bridge is the main gate, the north road goes to the ward line, the stream path to the gorge, and the sea landing to the cliff road. Drawn by `tools/place-plans/plans/district.py`, which also prints the lengths. **A proposal.**

**How it's set:**
- Every walk falls within the planned 3 to 8 screens (20 to 60 s), except C, whose length is mostly the courtyard you cross.
- The total is 5.5 minutes against the brief's estimate of about 5.
- The small fights go on the three walks after you've met each enemy, as the user asked ("some small fights are good to have in several places... low challenge encounters"). That's more than the first proposal's two.
- The armillary's column on the plateau is the landmark from every road (principle 6).
- The academy stands on a 6 m plateau, with the coast and the harbor below it, so every walk out goes downhill and every walk home climbs.

**Open:** the infirmary and the waterworks stand either side of the river, and walk E crosses walk B's road. A crossing like that is a chance for a shortcut later (principle 1, for returning players).
