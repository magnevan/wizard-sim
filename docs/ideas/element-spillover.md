# Learning an element by spillover

## One-liner
When a machine that draws on a field breaks near you, its field spills into you, and that element is yours.

## In the hands
You reach a broken machine and deal with what it's causing. Either it overflows and the element soaks into you (a glowing lamp, a burst icehouse, a lightning strike), or it's starving and you drive it until it runs dry, then the same keys work from your hands (the battery). The new rune lights on everyone's bar at once. Shield is the exception: nothing spills it, so you learn it when there's no machine left to draw on.

## Why it's interesting
It gives each element a place and a reason, out of the world's own infrastructure (pillar 5). It explains innate casting without lore dumps. Varying overflow and drought keeps eight arrivals from repeating, and each stop is a natural setup for the combo with the last element learned.

## Costs & risks
- Eight places with their own machines and art is a lot of map for a tutorial; doubling up is the main lever.
- The battery is built as the first-cast moment for Fire; Life first moves or softens it.
- Shield last means no barriers, Blink or parry through most of the opening.
