# 2026-11-04: Play-test notes, round 2

The user's second session with the build, after online play went live. Seven notes. Each one gets what the code showed, what was fixed now, and what still needs a decision. Weapons (note 6) got their own long exploration: `2026-11-04-weapons-and-movesets.md`.

## Fixed now

**1. The fire wall is very thin as a vertical strip.** The fire curtain draws one 1 px flame column per pixel along the wall. On a north-south wall those columns stack on top of each other, so the wall shrinks to a 1 px line. Columns now widen as the wall turns vertical: 1 px for an east-west wall, 4 px for a north-south one. *Cost:* none. The other curtains (cold, water, lightning, arcane) are drawn as a sheet and may thin the same way. Check them on the next pass.

**3. Shield effects have no dismiss.** Shield now mirrors Sigil: double-tap Shield (Shield + Shield cancel) with the cursor on one of your walls, and the whole cast comes down, every segment raised on the same tick. With the cursor on yourself, it drops your ward and conjured guard. Energy walls stop draining your pool; rock was paid once and gives nothing back. *Cost:* none in keys; it's the gesture players already know from Sigil.

**7. Casting while moving.** The rules already allowed it: you walk at half speed while you hold a channel or a charge. But a browser bug in our input made it impossible. A second mouse button pressed while the first is held fires no "button down" event (it's a pointer *chord*), so holding LMB to walk swallowed the RMB cast, and the other way round. Input now reads the buttons mask, so LMB + RMB walks and casts at once. The slow-down is now a tunable (`body.castWalk`, 0.5), pinned in the panel's Feel section. One-shot casts don't slow you.
- *Trade-off (the Magicka 2 lesson):* with mouse and keyboard, walking and aiming both follow the cursor, so you walk *toward* what you spray, and can't kite backwards while casting. True strafing needs a move direction apart from the aim. A gamepad has one (two sticks), and that's where Magicka 2's cast-while-moving shines. On keyboard, our left hand is on the ten element keys, so WASD isn't free. We'll judge it in play before adding anything.

**5 (part). Coated rock did nothing.** A rock wall with Life, Fire or Cold looked coated, but nothing in the sim read those elements: only curtains pulsed theirs. So Shield + Life + Earth played exactly like Shield + Earth. Coated rock now pulses its coat on whoever touches it, every half second, as curtains do on whoever crosses them. A Life stone wall heals those sheltering behind it, and the enemies bashing it too (Life heals anyone, as in Magicka). A Fire stone wall burns whoever is against it.

## Needs a decision

**2. An inspect mode: hover to see what something is.** Hold a key, and a small card names what's under the cursor:
- enemies: name, health, statuses, what they resist;
- walls, curtains and wards: whose, which elements, health, what crossing or touching does;
- devices: what they do, and uses or time left;
- the floor: wet, burning, icy, scorched.

*In co-op,* it explains a partner's creations too, which is the hardest thing to read today. *Cost:* one key. The mouse hand is free, so it doesn't fight casting. **Recommended:** hold Alt (the page stops the browser from taking the key). The sim keeps running; this is reading, not pausing.

**4. Shield + Life on Area is overpowered.** Area raises a ring of 10 curtain segments (about 16 units of wall); Place raises an arc of 5 (about 4 units). Both cost the same, and every segment heals whoever is in it. The same imbalance exists for every barrier, and only the energy drain (twice the segments, twice the drain) holds it in check. Healing makes it obvious. Options:
- **A. One budget per cast (recommended).** A Shield cast's elements are paid once for one wall's worth of effect. A ring spreads that effect over twice the length, so each segment pulses at half strength. Also, a body is touched at most once per pulse, however many segments it overlaps. The geometry rule stays (Area = ring), and Life stops being special.
- **B. The user's line field.** Make Life + Shield + Area a single healing strip in front of you instead of a ring. It reads well, but it breaks "the cast picks the geometry", and dragged Place already draws a line.
- **C. Make the ring cost more.** Simple, but it makes "Area" the expensive cast for one form only.

**5. The rule: adding an element must always change the spell.** This was already the rules' first line ("any element you add changes the spell"), and the Atlas audits keep inert additions at 0, but only *in the rules text*. The game didn't keep that promise (coated rock, above). **Recommended:** make it a rule of the game itself, checked by a sim audit. For every queue of up to three runes and every cast, run the spell in a standard scene (an enemy, an ally, a wet patch, a wall) and fingerprint what happened. Adding any element must change the fingerprint. The audit finds the gaps; each gap is then a design question ("what *should* Earth do to a Life curtain?") rather than a bug we stumble on in play.

**6. Weapons.** See `2026-11-04-weapons-and-movesets.md`. In short:
- Today's problems are mostly bugs: conjuring a guard deletes your weapon (and the other way round), the swing looks like the staff's, guards ignore their elements, and the Bulwark's bash can't happen.
- The proposal: with an empty queue, every cast gesture becomes a weapon move.
- Eight guards, each blocking differently.
- A weapon hand and a guard hand: Sigil and Shield conjures can be held together, with two-handed exceptions.
