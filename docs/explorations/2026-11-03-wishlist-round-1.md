# 2026-11-03: Spell wishlist round 1

The user's verdicts on the seed list are in `ideas/spell-wishlist.md`. This file captures the themes that emerged.

## Themes from the user

### 1. Situational niches
Each shape should be *the right answer* to some battlefield situation. In Magicka:
- beams are good for long range and big enemies (less so for clusters, because they turn slowly)
- close area attacks are good when you're surrounded
- rocks and fireballs are good for clusters at a distance
- line casts are good against incoming waves

Ricochet fits this: you reach for it when the geometry (natural or built) calls for it. Cut candidates like the boomerang failed this test, because they offer nothing situational.

### 2. Preparation in downtime as a skill
Several favorites cluster around using pauses to prepare: traps, primed orbitals or seekers, sustained circles, prisms, stored spells. Magicka's mines were underused because of how they were cast and paced, not because preparation is a bad idea. → `ideas/spell-preparation.md`

### 3. New spell families
- conjured weapons (melee playstyle)
- a broader set of self and ally enchantments
- clones and summons
- stored or pre-prepared spells

## Claude's observations
- **The "situational niche" test could become a design rule.** Every shape or variation must have at least one situation where it's clearly the best choice. This is a cheap filter for the wishlist.
- **Preparation ties several threads together.** It serves the slower pace (pillar 4) and reproducible playstyles (pillar 6), creates support roles (setting up for others), and fixes a casting-rhythm complaint: a stored spell removes long-combo typing from crisis moments.
- **The prism is the first idea where two players' spells combine into something neither can do alone.** Merged beams with one player steering is a strong co-op moment and worth keeping as a reference point for support design.
- Encounter design needs lulls and visible approach (waves, telegraphed arrival) for preparation to matter. This affects level and enemy design later.
