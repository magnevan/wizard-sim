# Story direction, one page (2026-11-09)

Written after the user's review of the four candidates in `2026-11-09-story/`. A sketch to react to, not canon: the lore is the user's. Places are described by what's on the stand-in map, and nothing is named on purpose.

## The user's direction

- **The hook is a special year when the fields behave oddly.** "It's a reason to go out, and can explain a lot of conflict/issues happening in the world. Maybe veins are shifting, which could cause magical defenses to lose power. New territory has to be claimed to build wells on strong sources of power etc."
- **No explicit circuit.** "You get sent out into the field to solve an issue, this naturally gives you experience and mastery and brings you to new regions."
- **Linear, region to region,** "solving conflicts that arise from the shifting fields". No reputation system.
- **Wells are magical infrastructure.** The player isn't a wizard engineer. Legendary well-builders can exist as colour, as a legendary swordsmith would.

## The story

**Premise.** This year the veins are moving. Most people live and work on a vein and have never had to think about it. Now wards fail, forges go cold, wells run dry or overflow, and strong new veins surface in empty land that everyone suddenly wants.

**Why you.** Ordinary people can use a well but can't do anything when one goes wrong. A wizard can stand in the failing place and hold it with their own hands. There are too few wizards, so even a newly qualified one gets sent. Each job teaches you something, because each land's magic runs strong where you're standing.

**Opening.** The school's own wards falter during your last lesson, and the first call for help arrives. Easy to swap.

**The road**, each land leading to the next:

| Land | What the shifting veins have done | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Mesa fortress (Earth) | The vein feeding its defences has moved away, and what they kept out is coming in | Hold the walls, and learn this is happening everywhere |
| Great forest (Life) | A vein has moved in, and growth is swallowing farms and roads | Escort people out, and cut back what's spreading |
| Badlands and arch | A strong new vein has surfaced in empty land, and groups are fighting to claim it | First real fights against other wizards. A legendary well-builder is here, hired by one side |
| Stilt town in the fen (Water) | Water is rising and storms are worse | Defend a town you can't stay in. **Midpoint: the Sigil art opens, because you need defences that keep working after you leave** |
| Volcanic south (Fire) | Fire is running far too hot | The forges are thriving and the lava is coming. A legendary smith won't leave |
| Crystal island (Lightning) | The peak is drawing storms it never used to | Reach what's causing it |
| Northern ice (Cold) | Ice is shifting, opening ground nobody has walked | Follow the trail past the great skeleton |
| Purple land (Arcane) | Every shift traces back here | The answer, and the ending |

Coming back to old lands with new magic opens side paths, and you can see how each has coped.

**Tone.** Classic fantasy with a light touch. Troubles are real, and the humour comes from people and from friendly fire.

## Open question: why are the veins shifting?

The ending depends on this.
- **A natural event, like a river changing course.** No villain. The story is about how people cope. Risk: no climax.
- **A great new well is pulling them.** Someone built the greatest well in history, for good reasons, and it's drawing the veins towards it. Gives the story a face and fits wells being built. It's the closest of these to a familiar trope, so it depends on who built it and why.
- **Leave it open** until the first regions are built.

**The user's answer: leave it open for now.** Get into the story before deciding too much. People in the world can speculate between different reasons: natural, a big wizarding group doing something nefarious, or a big bad doing something evil. The third is the most typical and could be fine, but it stays unanswered.
