Field Atlas

A world generated the way the fiction says it formed: land, mountains, rivers and climate first; the seven fields from those; then towns where fields meet, named by the pair they sit on. No place has a proper name; naming is yours. Reroll the seed, switch the look, and mark the worlds that feel right.

How the fields are placed

Each field is a number per spot on the map, derived from the land. A spot is in a field's zone when it's strong enough; zones overlap where they like, and towns grow where two are strong.

Three approaches

This page

Fields-first generator

Our own code: the fields come from the land, and the towns from the fields, so the map argues for the world's premise. Any seed is a candidate world; a kept seed can be rendered at any size or style. Its land and coastlines are plainer than a dedicated tool's.

Needs you once

Azgaar base, our overlay

Generate and hand-edit a continent in Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator (free, in the browser), export its data, and this page reads the heights, rivers and climate from it and lays the fields on top. To build the importer, it needs one real export file from you; its format is unverified until then.

Later

Painted over a kept seed

Pick a seed here, export it large, and paint the final map over it (by hand, a painting tool, or image generation once a description is approved). The generator decides where things are; the painting decides how it looks.
