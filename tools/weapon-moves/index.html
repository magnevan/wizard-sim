Weapons

One per lead element, plus the bare staff, which gets the same grammar weakly (shoves only). Each weapon has exactly three moves: Strike (RMB tap), Heavy (RMB hold) and Special (Shift+RMB). The redesigns: the trident, the healing link, the long spear dash with the javelin on Shift+RMB, the fire wave, the directed quake, and the Echo focus in place of the hook.

Guards

Shield + the lead element picks the guard. A guard owns Defend (Space: hold to block, tap to parry) and Retreat (Shift+LMB: away from where you face), and while you carry it you get a passive and its resistances. Each guard is immune or strong against its own element and weak against its opposite. Any weapon move drops the block for its wind-up and recovery.
