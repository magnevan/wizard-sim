**6.5/10.** NOW has made a substantial move from the flat BEFORE toward grass, but its pale, uniformly combed texture and busy edges still miss TARGET’s richer olive shadows and broad, warm painted strokes. **Yes, it is presentable to the art lead as a first in-game version of the look**, with those gaps called out.

1. **Restore the broad light-and-dark composition.** The road frame and lower field read as an even yellow-green carpet; TARGET has deeper cool olive hollows separating warm sunlit sweeps. In `groundLawn`, change the sweep multiplier’s endpoints from `vec3(0.74, 0.84, 0.68)` and `vec3(1.16, 1.08, 0.8)` to `vec3(0.65, 0.77, 0.65)` and `vec3(1.18, 1.08, 0.75)`. Reduce the long-grass lift from `lg * 0.5` to `lg * 0.3` and the additive comb from `vec3(0.18, 0.15, 0.05)` to `vec3(0.10, 0.085, 0.025)`. This preserves the warm direction while letting the hollows register; check at the same cloud and wind time as the reference.

2. **Break up the all-over combed stamping.** The close-up and lower field have conspicuous repeated curved fans and a wavy, almost fur-like grain; TARGET has quieter ground between fewer, more distinct strokes. In `groundLawn`, change `b1` density from `0.92` to `0.7`, and `b2` density from `lg * (1.0 - bare)` to `0.65 * lg * (1.0 - bare)`. Change the tuft multiplier from `mix(0.7, mix(0.55, 1.6, b.x), b.y)` to `mix(0.82, mix(0.7, 1.4, b.x), b.y)`. Keep the existing jitter and atlas variety. This also reduces texture work on a laptop GPU.

3. **Calm the raised margins.** The road and worn-path edges in the close-up have a dark, continuous, frilly fringe that draws more attention than the concept’s soft broken edge. In `marginGrass`, change `step = 0.38` to `0.48`, the run threshold `(foot ? 0.3 : 0.45)` to `(foot ? 0.38 : 0.52)`, and multiply the computed `h` by `0.7`. In `marginMaterial`, change the tip lift `mix(0.95, 1.15, vMgUv.y)` to `mix(0.95, 1.08, vMgUv.y)`. Check paving, path shoulders and prop feet at game camera after the change; retain occasional fans rather than a continuous hedge.
