Structures: what builds what

Shield builds barriers: walls you draw (hold Place, drag, let go; curve the drag to bend it), a quick arc around you (let go without dragging), a forward wave (Force: Tidal wave with Water, Fault line with Earth) and a ring (Area). Sigil builds devices: turrets (Force, casting the other elements' Force spell, each shot drawn from your pool), orbitals (Area) and glyphs (Place; drag for a line glyph). Alone it places a lens (Place). Costs: stuff costs once, energy costs upkeep, and every Sigil device binds a Sigil rune. Tap a card to load it above.

What each element adds

Each element leaves one status on whatever it hits, the same in every form. Four of them also bend the spell (Water pushes, Cold shatters, Lightning chains, Arcane bursts); only the one you put most of does, so a spell has at most one shape change. Inside a Life spell the elements lend their shape or their help instead, never their harm.

Self: wards, auras, and the plain cast

Shield + Self is a ward that protects you: alone it reflects (a mirror), with elements it resists them. Sigil + Self is an aura that affects those around you and never boosts your spells (add runes for that); it binds a Sigil rune. Several elements make several auras, each weaker. Sigil alone is a Double: a copy that repeats your spells at half power, each repeat paid with half that spell's runes. Earth is the material: with Earth, the other elements coat it instead, so Shield + Earth + Fire is stone armour coated in fire and Sigil + Earth + Fire is a burning bulldoze. Without Shield or Sigil, the elements simply hit you, as in Magicka: Life heals, Water douses. Point the cursor at an ally and the same spell goes to them.
