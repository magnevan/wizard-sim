# Magicka: reference notes

These notes come from memory (the user's and Claude's). Anything marked *(unverified)* still needs checking.

## Core system

- **Eight elements** are queued with keys: Water, Life, Shield, Cold, Lightning, Arcane, Earth, Fire. Up to 5 can be queued.
- **Combined elements:** Water + Fire = Steam, Water + Cold = Ice. *(unverified: exact combine rules)*
- **Opposites cancel** when queued together, e.g. Fire/Cold, Water/Lightning, Life/Arcane, Earth/Lightning. *(unverified: full list)*
- **The shape is derived from the elements, not chosen by the player.** For example, Earth → thrown rock, Arcane/Life → beam, Fire/Water/Cold/Steam → spray, Lightning → chain arcs, Shield → wards and walls.
- **The cast mode is chosen separately**, using the elements already queued:
  - Force cast: forward (projectile, beam or spray)
  - Area cast: around yourself
  - Self cast: on yourself (heal, elemental ward)
  - Weapon cast: imbues your melee swing, in a line in front of you
- **Magicks** are named special spells, unlocked by finding tomes and cast with specific element sequences (e.g. Haste, Revive, Time Warp). *(unverified: exact combos)*

## Spell shapes as the user remembers them

Thrown balls, darts (weak and rarely used), beams, sprays (quick wetting for freeze combos), rains, mines, rocks, earthquake / arcane area attack. Self shields: an elemental ward (reflect/immunity) and a rock shield (extra health, slows you down). Special cases that only happen when an element is cast alone: Lightning, plain Shield (self, or a reflective wall in the world). Spells vary further by arc vs 360° vs line.

## What works

- Every spell is typed out of a small alphabet, so skill is pure muscle memory and carries over between playthroughs.
- Discovering new spells is a big part of the fun early on.
- Friendly fire produces chaos and comedy.

## Where it falls short (user's view)

- The set of shapes is fixed, so after a while the system feels limited compared with what a spellcasting game could be.
- Support play is thin: Haste, Time Warp, slowing or freezing enemies, healing and reviving are about all there is.
- There's no calm space for experimenting. Battles are hectic, and it's awkward to ask teammates to wait while you test.
- Character development is minimal.
