## New notes, most important first

1. **Sail beats Stay outright.** Sail keeps both oaths, one to the letter, and gets the duel. Stay keeps one, breaks one, and has no described play. Lost houses cost no record, so layout doesn't matter. If too many stretches fall, Sailing should nail the stick. Give Stay the first half's largest fight and a hearth reward Sail can't reach.

2. **The dyke can't be built as written.** Pools start at 1 with only four attunements before it, so a player who never deepened Sigil owns one device, and nobody knew to save for it. Rock walls last 25 seconds, so "Earth and Cold stop the ice" only works as device payloads. That suits the cutters: only carved work stays. Let Hovik's trial lift Sigil to about 3, and scale stretches to devices held.

3. **Instalments plus notches make a chore loop, and the ferry is gone.** A notch for each well taken with a due open forces a continent round-trip after every well, up to twenty dues. Nothing states how you travel. Cap open dues at two and restore a crossing. Say what a notch costs; right now it's only a word.

4. **Forty oaths "each with its own village gate" means forty village exteriors and a final road hours long.** Group two or three oaths per village, about fifteen gates. Only great towns send fighters to the last battle, and each ally should remove one authored threat from one flank, so seven on/off states stay testable.

5. **Wording is mostly undefined.** Only "bring the fire to her" has a consequence. What does "earns more at once" pay? Four great oaths list one wording. Fewer, real wordings beat many empty ones.

## Newly broken

- Pillar 5 asks for warmth and a light touch. Funerals, a child by lot and a starving column leave friendly fire no tone to land in: you'll be exploding friends around a bier. Restore some comedy on the road.
- A rising tide is a poor place to first learn the queue. Slow its opening minutes.
- Parishioners "will not strike to kill". Can you kill them? Make it a sworn restriction.

## Do not touch

- Runes left on the dyke, missing from the duel.
- Burning Shushan unseen, the ford rule's payoff. It sits near ban 33 but stays clear because both sticks come back unburned.
