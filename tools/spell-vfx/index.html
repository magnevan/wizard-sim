Spell VFX Gallery

Each screen loops one spell from the current rules, drawn in the Pixel Battlefield style. Red is the caster, blue is an ally, and the goblins and the brute are enemies. Before every cast, the queued runes fly to the staff in their element colours, so the recipe is on screen as well as in the caption.

The question for each one: can you tell what the spell does without reading the caption? Rate the look, not the mechanic. Click a screen to pause it.
