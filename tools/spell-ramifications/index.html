The rules applied

  • Who is still paying for it? Released (runes once, the world's now), Concentration (held against pressure) or a Binding (a made thing).
  • Arcane concentrates (beam, charge and release, delayed blast) and disrupts what's being paid for, by amounts, power against power. Matter is out of its reach.
  • Walls stop everything; coats decide what each hit costs; wards resist magic only.
  • No switches: no absolute reflection, immunity or dispel.
  • Lightning follows rules you can learn and disrupts actions. Fire consumes, Water is the medium, Cold controls.

Only the cost changes

The same spells, paid differently. Everything not listed keeps its cost: sprays, rings, missiles, beams, patches, rain, fog, heals, and every Earth, Lightning and Ice spell.
