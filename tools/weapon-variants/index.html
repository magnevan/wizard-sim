Wizard Sim · issue #4 · every conjured weapon and its infusions

Elemental Arms

A weapon is Sigil + elements cast with the Weapon key. The strongest element leads and picks the weapon; every other element in the queue rides along as an infusion. Pressing every key sequence through the game's own spell rules gives 57 distinct weapons: eight bases and the extra-element sets each can carry, counting a repeated element once.

The pictures are Codex concepts for inspiration, drawn from the approved weapon sheet. The brief for every one: show each added element as one solid feature in its own colour, sculpted into the weapon, so nothing reads as particle noise at game size. Mark the ones worth chasing.

What limits the list

The queue holds Sigil plus four runes. Opposites cancel rune for rune: Fire and Cold, Water and Lightning, Life and Arcane, Earth and Lightning, and Fire melts Ice. Water and Cold merge into Ice.

Who leads

Life, then Earth, then Arcane, then Lightning, then Ice, then whichever of Water, Fire and Cold has the most runes. An element that outranks the base takes the weapon over, so a Frost spear given Earth becomes a Stone maul.

The visual language asked for

The same motif for an element on every weapon, so a partner learns it once. Two or three extras each get their own part of the weapon (head, shaft, grip) instead of mixing.
