# 2026-11-02: Feedback round 1

The user's responses to `2026-11-02-what-i-like.md`, condensed. Resulting decisions are in `decisions/README.md` and the pillars in `vision.md` have been updated.

## Progression
Agreed that most skill should stay with the player, but not absolutely: Magicks did make you stronger, and that was fine. Favored directions:
- gradual trade-offs that lean into a preferred playstyle
- numeric growth that lets you *do more at once* or *speed up as you warm up* during play, rather than +X% damage

## Spell variety
- **A (world simulation):** elemental interactions are a given (wet → freeze, rain changing fire, lightning and frost), and evolving the battlefield is good. But don't lean on it, and don't build every level around a gimmick. Players should develop a **reproducible playstyle**.
- **B (more components):** worth exploring. Magicka was elements + implicit shape + cast variation, and much of the game knowledge was learning which elements produce which shape. Explore which components work best.
- **C (spatial casting):** hesitant. Mouse is for moving and aiming, and gamepad shouldn't be ruled out. More *aiming variations* are welcome, though.
- **Method:** list spells that would be cool → derive the tree/structure → explore the leaf nodes that structure implies. → `ideas/spell-wishlist.md`

## Support
- **Priming:** rejected. Communicating it costs an order of magnitude more than the teammate typing it themselves. It feels like sabotage.
- **Lenses/conduits:** yes.
- **Rituals:** yes, in two flavors: (1) a big channeled spell where the caster needs protection, and (2) an ongoing ritual by the supporter that provides a continuous benefit.
- **Rescue:** yes, this was lacking in Magicka. Healing one ally meant healing everyone near them; crowd control around an ally hit the ally too; in-world shields were always centered on yourself. Explore:
  - shields at arbitrary locations
  - shields and enhancements cast *on* allies
  - spell shaping that deliberately avoids friendly fire (accidental friendly fire stays fun)
- **New:** drawing attention away from allies (decoys, vortices, shields as a variant of this). If there are resources beyond health, restoring them is a support action too.
- **Ally empowerment through friendly fire:** only if deliberately set up, never by accident.

## Practice and pace
- **Hub:** generally agreed. Concern: players may just skip it. An alternative is sections of the game played in a slower exploration mode. Flesh this out much later.
- **Pace:** Magicka wasn't far off. Enemies were maybe slightly too fast, or the camera slightly too zoomed in. Power was capped by click speed plus animations. That's partly fine, but a resource could encourage mindful casting.
- **Critique of casting feel:** Magicka seemed to have an undocumented, unreliable minimum gap between inputs; spamming could drop elements (the user once measured it with AutoHotkey). Spells with easy-to-type combos were stronger simply because they were faster to type. That's arbitrary. → `ideas/casting-rhythm.md`

## Platform
Top-down. Mouse and keyboard preferred; don't rule out gamepad prematurely.
