# Playtest guide (for the user)

Play at https://claude.ai/artifact/HmEKJfEUvzY7amrNTCXHSv with mouse and keyboard, or run `npm run dev` here. Press **H** in game for the controls. About 15 minutes is enough.

## 1. Sanctum warm-up (key 1)
- Type a few Magicka staples: Q+F (steam spray), S+F (arcane-fire beam), D (rock: hold to charge, and a full charge carries the dummy), Q+R (ice shards: bounce them off a wall).
- **Point cast (G or Ctrl+RMB):**
  - D+S on the ground: Geyser
  - E on the green ally dummy: Ward toss
  - S on the ally: Yank
  - W on the ally: Mend
  - E+S: Lens, then beam through it with S
- **Seal (C) and Release (V):**
  - C, then F+D with G: a rune. Do it 3 times, then V.
  - C, then D+D with Shift+RMB: orbitals. Repeat, then V.
  - C, then F+S+A with Space: a stored spell. V fires it.
- **Surfaces:** Q alone with G floods a spot. Then A with G (surge), or R with G (freeze).
- **Weapon (Shift+LMB):** Q+Q gives a tidal wave, D+D a fault line, E a bulwark.

## 2. Arena (key 2)
Waves alternate: a **lull** (portals show what's coming and a countdown) → the **wave** → a 3 s breather. Downed players stay out until the wave ends. The archetypes are swarmers, brute (telegraphed slam), shieldbearer (blocks from the front: go around or under), back-line caster, grabber (Yank your friend free) and fire imp.
- Press **K** for an AI ally, so you can try ward toss, yank and lantern on a friend.
- A gamepad joins as player 2 when you press any button.
- After each wave the banner shows how many sealed spells you used. **Watch whether you *want* to prepare in the lulls.**

## 3. Rule set A.1 (press F2)
Try the enchants: A alone with Space for Haste, D for Stoneskin, E+S for Ethereal, S+F for Glass cannon, Q for Cleanse. Point (G) any of them on the ally (K). E with G on an enemy is a Cage. Compare with A.
