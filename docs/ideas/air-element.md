# Air as an element

## One-liner
A tenth element, Air, on the T key opposite Earth: it shoves and carries (wind that fans fire onto enemies, blows a partner's steam cloud, slides frozen bodies into walls).

## In the hands
One more element key, T, above Earth, so the keyboard becomes five clean pairs of opposites (Q/A, W/S, E/D, R/F, T/G). Air leads a spell only when it's alone (a bellows gust); in other spells its payload is a shove plus "carry". Shield + Air is a Gale wave that shoves a crowd, and a wind curtain blocks bodies but not shots. A partner sees gust streaks and clouds or flames drifting with them. Full draft: `../explorations/2026-11-04-air-pass.md`.

## Why it's interesting
- The symmetry the user liked: every element gets one opposite directly above or below it.
- Co-op set-ups: one player makes steam or fire, the other blows it where it should go.
- It takes the shove off Water and the Shield wave, so each element has one clear job.

## Costs & risks
- A tenth key on a stretch, and a tenth colour (the draft's jade `#249070`) close-ish to Life and Cold; pale wind VFX against frost.
- About +81 % spells in the Atlas, mostly with one new word (shove/carry).
- Changes to things already built and rated: Water's payload loses its shove, the pure Shield wave becomes a Mirror wave, and Earth stops cancelling Lightning.
- Shoving allies around is the most annoying kind of friendly fire (pillar 3).
- The user's own warning from wishlist round 3: "it implies a lot of new effects too".
