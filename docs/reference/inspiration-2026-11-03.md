# Inspiration from other games (research, 2026-11-03)

Three research agents gathered spell candidates for wishlist round 2. Sources come from the agents' own knowledge of the games and weren't checked on the web. Two facts are flagged to verify: Techies' Remote Mines were removed in a Dota 2 rework (~7.32), and Weaver's Time Lapse can target an ally with Aghanim's Scepter.

Candidates with a scene in the spell gallery (`tools/spell-gallery/`) are marked 🎞. The rest stay here as a pool.

## Observations from the research

**Spell systems (offense):**
- Noita (triggers, trails, path changes) and Path of Exile (support gems) treat *delivery* as a modifier layered onto a spell, not as a separate spell. Curve, Courier and Trailblazer would work as modifiers. This is relevant once we zoom into the spell grammar.
- A visible delay before impact (Helldivers 2 stratagems, Invoker's Sun Strike) suits a slower pace and gives allies time to react.
- Spells that use geometry (Fault Line, Pinning Javelin, Tripwire) reward reading the arena. Fault Line also *creates* walls to ricochet off.
- Surface Surge and Bellows Gust are two-player setups like the prism: one player makes the surface or cloud, the other uses it.

**What makes support fun (Overwatch, Dota 2, League of Legends):**
- Timing reads beat healing totals. Shallow Grave, Suzu and Immortality Field reward reading the enemy's wind-up; cast too early and they're wasted.
- Rescues where you move your own body feel heroic: Kiriko's teleport, Lifeweaver's pull and platform.
- Peeling is a form of aggression. Thresh, Axe and Braum protect allies by acting on enemies.
- The ally keeps their agency. Rewinds (Ekko, Weaver) and the Fastball Special let a newcomer play boldly inside a safety net the support set up. This fits "deliberate, never accidental" empowerment.

**Preparation (Orcs Must Die, Dungeon Defenders, Path of Exile, Hearthstone, Into the Breach):**
- Fun:
  - clear triggers: telegraphs, triggers you fire yourself, condition-based secrets
  - payoffs you can see: killboxes, a burst at peak enemy density
  - setup that pays off over several waves
- Tedious:
  - rebuilding the same setup every fight
  - traps that fire on touch and hurt their owner (the Magicka mines problem)
  - summons that fight on their own, so the player has nothing to do

## Candidates
- 🎞 **Lobbed Shell** (offense): Arc a heavy shell over walls and heads; a shadow on the ground shows where it lands. _Best when:_ Enemies sheltering behind a wall, a shield-bearer, or your own team's frontline. _Source:_ Helldivers 2 mortar sentry and Worms-style artillery: indirect fire with visible flight time. Magicka has no true lobbed shape (rock is direct fire).
- 🎞 **Curve Bolt** (offense): A bolt that bends in flight; aim beside your target and it hooks around cover. _Best when:_ One enemy hiding behind a lone pillar or a raised shield facing you. _Source:_ Noita path modifiers (Spiral arc, Orbiting arc, Slithering path) that reshape a projectile's trajectory; the 'curve around cover' idea is original.
- **Courier Bolt** (offense): A fast, tiny bolt that carries a second spell and unfolds it on impact. _Best when:_ You need a big area spell delivered far away or through a narrow gap, without walking in. _Source:_ Noita 'with trigger' and 'with timer' spells (e.g. Spark bolt with trigger), which cast the next spell in the wand at the impact point.
- 🎞 **Fault Line** (control): Crack the ground in a line: everything on it is hurled aside, and a stone ridge remains. _Best when:_ A pack charging down an open lane; split it and build instant cover or a ricochet wall. _Source:_ Dota 2 Earthshaker's Fissure (damaging line that leaves impassable terrain) and Diablo II-style fissure eruptions travelling outward.
- 🎞 **Sticky Beacon** (preparation): Tag a big enemy with a beacon; seconds later a sky lance strikes wherever it has wandered. _Best when:_ One big, slow, dangerous enemy moving through a crowd you can't easily isolate. _Source:_ Helldivers 2 stratagems: thrown beacons that can stick to enemies, with a call-in delay before the strike. Also Invoker's Sun Strike (delayed, telegraphed sky strike).
- 🎞 **Pinning Javelin** (control): Hurl a javelin that skewers the first enemy and carries it until it's pinned to a wall. _Best when:_ A big enemy standing near a wall or pillar; take it out of the fight for a while. _Source:_ Dota 2 Mars' Spear of Mars: carries the first hero hit and stuns it if it collides with terrain or a tree.
- **Contagion Seed** (offense): Plant a seed in one enemy; when it dies, the seed bursts and spreads to its neighbors. _Best when:_ A dense swarm of weak enemies: kill one seeded enemy and watch the chain reaction. _Source:_ Path of Exile's Contagion (chaos DoT that spreads to nearby enemies on death) and Risk of Rain 2's Will-o'-the-wisp (on-kill explosion).
- 🎞 **Surface Surge** (offense): Touch a puddle, slick, or grass patch and flood your element through all of it instantly. _Best when:_ Many enemies standing in one big connected surface, such as a flooded floor. _Source:_ Divinity: Original Sin 2 surfaces (electrifying a water surface affects everyone in it) and Magicka's wet-plus-lightning interaction, made into a deliberate spell shape.
- 🎞 **Bellows Gust** (control): A broad gust that carries clouds, flames, and loose projectiles forward onto your enemies. _Best when:_ A fire patch or poison cloud lies between you and the enemy; push it onto them. _Source:_ Spellbreak's wind gauntlet carrying other elements (wind plus fire makes a firestorm), and Breath of the Wild's wind spreading fire across grass.
- **Trailblazer** (offense): A slow orb that leaves a lingering elemental trail along its whole path. _Best when:_ A pack chasing you: walk a loop and they follow straight through the burning trail. _Source:_ Noita's trail modifiers (Fire trail, Poison trail, Oil trail) attached to projectiles, and Invoker's Chaos Meteor, which rolls forward leaving burning ground.
- 🎞 **Tripwire** (preparation): Stretch a crackling line between two pillars or walls; whatever crosses it gets blasted. _Best when:_ A doorway or gap enemies must funnel through, with a lull to set up first. _Source:_ Half-Life's laser tripmine (beam across a corridor that detonates when crossed) and Path of Exile's trap family; anchoring to two geometry points is original.
- **Soul Knot** (offense): Bind two enemies together: damage dealt to one is echoed to the other. _Best when:_ A dangerous enemy you can't reach, standing near one you can. _Source:_ Dota 2 Grimstroke's Soulbind (tethers two enemies; single-target spells on one also hit the other) and Ember Spirit's Searing Chains (binding multiple targets).

- 🎞 **Stay of Death** (support): Plant a pale lantern; for two seconds, nobody standing in its glow can drop below 1 HP. _Best when:_ A big, telegraphed slam is about to land on an ally who can't escape in time. _Source:_ Baptiste's Immortality Field (Overwatch), Dazzle's Shallow Grave (Dota 2)
- 🎞 **Kindred Step** (movement): Blink to an ally's side, even through walls, arriving with a brief guard around you both. _Best when:_ An ally is cornered behind a wall and needs a body and a shield there now. _Source:_ Kiriko's Swift Step (Overwatch), Ashe/Brigitte style 'go to friend' peel; Magicka's Teleport
- **Petal Lift** (support): Raise a flower platform at the cursor; anyone standing on it rises above ground effects and grasping melee. _Best when:_ An ally stands in electrified water or a swarm of small biters with nowhere to walk. _Source:_ Lifeweaver's Petal Platform (Overwatch)
- **Call Out** (support): Shout in a narrow cone; every enemy hit turns and charges you for three seconds. _Best when:_ A brute is about to finish a downed or fleeing ally and must be pulled off. _Source:_ Axe's Berserker's Call (Dota 2), Rammus's Frenzying Taunt (League of Legends), Winston/Reinhardt peel in Overwatch
- **Cleansing Chime** (support): Toss a bell; allies it rings near are scrubbed of wet, frozen and burning, and briefly untouchable. _Best when:_ A frozen ally is a breath away from being shattered by a rock. _Source:_ Kiriko's Protection Suzu (Overwatch); Magicka status combos (wet+cold=frozen, shatter)
- 🎞 **Conduit Sigil** (enchant): Mark an ally; for six seconds your spells pass through them harmlessly and fire out their staff, amplified. _Best when:_ Your ally has line of sight to a target you can't reach, and you have the bigger beam. _Source:_ Original; echoes this project's prism and Zarya's barrier-to-energy conversion (Overwatch)
- **Anchorstone** (enchant): Root an ally to the ground: no knockback, push, or pull can move them, friend or foe. _Best when:_ An ally channels near a cliff edge while the team throws water and force around. _Source:_ Crowd-control immunity like Dota 2's Black King Bar and Olaf's Ragnarok (League), applied to an ally; Magicka's pushy water/arcane
- 🎞 **Hourglass Mark** (enchant): Brand an ally; four seconds later they snap back to where they stood, undoing damage taken since. _Best when:_ An ally dives aggressively into a crowd and you want a guaranteed way back. _Source:_ Ekko's Chronobreak (League), Tracer's Recall (Overwatch), Weaver's Time Lapse on an ally (Dota 2, with Aghanim's Scepter)
- **Riposte Skin** (enchant): A half-second mirror sheen on you or an ally; projectiles that hit it fly back at the shooter. _Best when:_ A big caster is about to fire a slow, deadly orb at your teammate. _Source:_ Battlerite counter abilities, Genji's Deflect (Overwatch), Braum's Unbreakable (League)
- 🎞 **Bulwark** (weapon): Conjure a tower shield: hold it to block a frontal arc, bash to shove enemies. _Best when:_ Escorting a fragile ally across a lane swept by enemy archers or casters. _Source:_ Reinhardt's Barrier Field (Overwatch), Braum's Unbreakable (League), Magicka's rock/shield elements
- **Warden's Flail** (weapon): Conjure a chain flail: wide swings, and a hooked sweep that drags enemies toward you. _Best when:_ Small enemies are swarming your channeling ally and you need them on you instead. _Source:_ Thresh's Flay and Darius's Apprehend (League), Pudge's Meat Hook (Dota 2)
- 🎞 **Stonemason's Maul** (weapon): Conjure a maul: slamming raises a stone pillar, and striking a pillar sends it rolling as a boulder. _Best when:_ Open ground with no cover, or a lined-up wave you want to bowl through. _Source:_ Earth Spirit's Stone Remnant and Boulder Smash (Dota 2), Magicka's rock element
- 🎞 **Clothesline** (control): Two allies each cast an anchor; a crackling line forms between them that trips anything crossing it. _Best when:_ A wave of small enemies rushes through a gap between two positioned players. _Source:_ Original; in the spirit of It Takes Two's paired abilities and wire/line co-op traps; geometry, not a heal tether
- 🎞 **Fastball Sling** (movement): Conjure a sling; an ally who steps in gets flung at your cursor, landing in a burst of your element. _Best when:_ A sniper or caster sits behind a crowd, out of reach for either player alone. _Source:_ X-Men 'Fastball Special'; Lifeweaver's platform and launch pads in It Takes Two

- 🎞 **Contingency** (preparation): Compose a spell and attach a condition: 'when I'm hit', 'when surrounded'. It fires itself, once. _Best when:_ Before a melee rush you can't dodge; your pre-loaded push or ward fires the instant they reach you. _Source:_ Path of Exile Cast when Damage Taken / Cast on Death; Hearthstone Mage secrets (Ice Block); D&D Contingency
- 🎞 **Timed Sigil** (preparation): Cast a spell into the ground with a fuse. A countdown ring shows exactly when it erupts. _Best when:_ Enemies arrive through a known gap on a known beat; land the blast as they step through. _Source:_ Noita timer-trigger modifiers; Into the Breach telegraphed attacks; Warcraft 3 delayed AoE (Flame Strike)
- 🎞 **Spell Quiver** (offense): Load the same spell into your staff again and again in calm moments, then fire every copy at once. _Best when:_ A big brute appears after a lull; dump six stored bolts in one alpha strike. _Source:_ Noita wand loading / multicast; Dota 2 Invoker's banked invoked slots
- 🎞 **Anchored Rite** (offense): Root yourself and plant three candles. The rite's final blast is as strong as the candles left lit. _Best when:_ A long lull before a boss or siege wave; time to set up a fight-ending strike. _Source:_ Warcraft 3 interruptible channeled ultimates (Starfall, Blizzard); WoW Ritual of Summoning; Orcs Must Die defend-the-rift
- **Tended Hearth** (support): Kindle a hearth that empowers everyone near it. Feed it an element to keep it burning and change its gift. _Best when:_ Holding one position across several waves; the hearth keeps paying out as long as someone tends it. _Source:_ Darkest Dungeon camping; Valheim campfire Rested buff; Dungeon Defenders auras
- 🎞 **Payload Effigy** (summon): Raise a straw effigy that enemies can't resist attacking. Seal a spell inside; it fires when they break it. _Best when:_ An ally is downed in the open; the effigy pulls the pack away, then punishes them. _Source:_ Path of Exile Decoy Totem + Cast on Death; Orcs Must Die decoys; Hearthstone deathrattle
- 🎞 **Marked Hound** (summon): A spectral hound waits at your heel and does nothing until you mark a target. Then it pounces and pins. _Best when:_ A ranged caster hides behind a wall of brutes; send the hound over them to pin it. _Source:_ WoW Hunter Kill Command; Diablo IV Necromancer commanded golem; Pikmin targeted throw
- 🎞 **Tripwire** (preparation): String a glowing thread between two pillars. The first enemy through sets off your element along all of it. _Best when:_ A narrow pass between pillars where waves funnel through. _Source:_ Orcs Must Die tripwires and arrow walls; Hitman remote setups
- 🎞 **Detonation Lattice** (preparation): Seed inert runes anywhere. Nothing happens until you speak the word, then all of them go off at once. _Best when:_ A huge wave crowds into a killbox; wait for peak density, then detonate everything. _Source:_ Dota 2 Techies Remote Mines (pre-rework); Path of Exile mines + Detonate Mines; Hitman remote explosives
- **Carved Channel** (control): Gouge a trench across the ground. It fills with whatever flows into it: water, fire, frost. _Best when:_ Enemies must cross open ground; turn the approach into a moat you can wet, freeze, or shock. _Source:_ Divinity: Original Sin 2 surfaces; Magicka wet + lightning; From Dust terrain shaping
- 🎞 **Shatter Bulwark** (control): Raise a wall during the calm. When it's overrun, burst it outward into a storm of stone shards. _Best when:_ A barricade is about to break under a horde; turn its collapse into your attack. _Source:_ Dungeon Defenders barricades; Magicka rock walls; original twist (wall as stored shrapnel)
- 🎞 **Stepping Slab** (movement): Conjure floating stone slabs anywhere, even over water. Walk on them; ground enemies can't follow. _Best when:_ Melee horde with no escape route; step onto slabs across a puddle and fire back. _Source:_ Deep Rock Galactic Engineer Platform Gun; Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom builds
