# Game editor: What He Had on Him, round 1

## Notes, most important first

1. **The reading is a lockpick.** Right element, right count, point, wait, seven times. Rune count is the spell language's only dial, so "soft touch" means a memorised recipe. This is the hour-6 wall. Direction: one object answers differently to different mixes and shapes (spray, beam, nova). Late evidence needs chained readings: frost, then relight what the frost exposed.

2. **Readings sit behind wells, yet you hold every rune from hour one.** If zapping the brass key on the slip does nothing, a hidden flag overrides the spell language. Direction: readings always physically work, locals only teach the trick, and wells only give depth. An early zap gets a garbled fragment, a real discovery.

3. **The answer is told, not read (late-game turn).** The biggest fact comes from an old man talking. Direction: each of Piet's tellings names something you can test, and the name comes from a spell. The small two-handed grip off the crank frosts up on the boots she sold you.

4. **Sigil misses the return it should power.** The midpoint sends you back "with stronger hands", but those hands gained one deepening. Sigil arrives at land five of seven. Direction: open it at the Fen, straight after the flip, so turrets and Blink land on the revisit to the first three lands.

5. **Seven wells means seven deepenings across nine pools.** If the main line needs two of each field rune, all seven are spent and "your choice" is false. Direction: guarantee main-line routes use different elements so any build passes, and let shrines give extra deepenings.

6. **One beat cannot fill five hours a land.** Each land has one object and one reveal, and the first three repeat the same beat. The boars and eels have nothing to do with the case. Direction: map edge, heartland and well to a local case with its own small wrong, and make creatures threaten or dig up evidence.

7. **The co-op door-handle moment has no solo form, and the finale drops the reading.** A pre-Sigil solo player cannot frost and fight at once. Direction: evidence under threat as a keeping trial (rising water, spreading fire), solvable alone by warding first. Make the walk with Greet a played sequence of readings, and put one reading inside the surge.

8. **Scope.** Three authored clues per fact, destroyable, times second-visit reversals and live odds, is a bigger studio's job. Direction: three spell routes into one prop, a witness as fallback, odds driven by a few flags. Returns at the Mesa and foundry are look-only: give each a fight or reading.

## Do not touch

- Attunements as local acts: the graft, the drill, standing still on the ice.
- Your friend's stray Lightning destroying evidence.
- The Double set where he fell.

## Ban list

Greet turning up everywhere is near #28. Avoid the "how did you get here" joke.
