## New notes, most important first

1. **Five of eight dissolves the collisions.** Two named jobs per leg, five picks: everyone takes both named jobs and skips strangers. Wenna and Hesper never die, unless each pair shares a date, which the draft doesn't say. Direction: make each pair clash, so one named loss per leg is unavoidable.

2. **The first payoff ignores the calendar, and the 212 have no faces.** The winch is the first job with a consequence, yet "or the flawed one" changes nothing at the Strait. And "the count will have names in it" arrives with a number and no name. Direction: the flawed winch costs specific boats, and those boats carry someone you worked beside in the South.

3. **The finale swaps the date for a monster.** A mile-long sounder breaching is close to ban 38, the skeleton's kind moving in the last act. "You see what Late was" reveals nothing, because region 4 told us. Direction: keep it indifferent. It feeds, it never hunts, and it wrecks hulls by accident. You steer the fleet around a whale.

4. **"The misprint stands" can't be true.** Cold lifted eleven days early in front of two million people. Direction: everyone knows it ran early, and only she can say by how much at each seat. Without her, the new almanac prints this year's error as the rule. The closing "someone will pay for it" also sits near ban 50.

5. **The place you save is the place we never see.** Region 6 is one sentence with no asks, job, fight or person. Ninety thousand is a number, Perpetua's refusal happens offstage, and Pim's promised disaster never occurs. Direction: a full fen leg, Perpetua refusing you to your face, and Pim sent somewhere important.
