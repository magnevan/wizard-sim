## (d) Verdict

The four engines really are different: a death investigation with a moral finding, a logistics calendar with no enemy, a friendly rivalry measured in chalk and chairs, and a debt system that collides with itself. The four questions, the late turns and what attunement means do not overlap at all. The sameness is in the layer around those engines, and it is larger than the premises suggest. All four bring the climax back to water at or near the fen. All four make the mesa a proud, unused fortress with a practical problem. Three of the four answer the ban on the skeleton with the same move, a monumental thing shrugged at by locals who put it to homely use, and the fourth keeps the shrug while naming the animal. Three of the four make the floating tower something homely or hauled about on ropes. Three of the four independently wrote the same character trait, and three put a plumb reference on the mesa. All four share one voice: dry deflation, oddly specific numbers, a small wordless gesture in place of a statement. A player would recognise four different games, and a reader of all four would suspect one author.
