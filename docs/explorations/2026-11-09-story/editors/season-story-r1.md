# Story editor: Flitting Year, round 1

The engine works. It hasn't yet decided what it can take from anyone.

## Notes, most important first

1. **Nothing says what happens to whatever isn't across.** The question depends on this, and the rules undercut it. Land follows its field "within a lifetime", so why a deadline in days? Perpetua stays in bare Arcane and her soup improves, so why do forty thousand flee? Direction: one sentence defining what a front physically does, and staying costs Perpetua something visible.

2. **The calendar's choices aren't on the page.** Each region describes one job. Scarrow's winch and Ilse are plot-required, so they always reach the final stations, and the empty stations only ever belong to strangers. "The cohorts either side of you" vanish after one mention. Direction: two named, colliding jobs per leg, a named face who can truly be absent, and the year-below cohort visibly botching what you skip.

3. **The late turn has a hole in it.** Every child knows the far end gets shoved hardest, Ottoline knew enough to bet, yet the almanac misses it. Direction: a later edition dropped the old figure as a misprint. The game kept it, the book tidied it. Plant it at the Strait: the nine days run a day short, and Bettany, who counts twice, can't explain it.

4. **Someone else's joke opens the gate.** You fight "to a standstill and it changes nothing", then a bet from one leg ago removes the last obstacle while you watch. Direction: the stub is in the melting storehouse, a dated job. Skip it and the barges take the bad road. Her crate also sits idle until region 4. Keeping it cold could be a running chore.

5. **The midpoint breaks its own rule and turns nothing.** "For the first time you are standing in bare Arcane", but the basket lowered you into it, and the South's lift fight did so again. The question is unchanged afterwards. Direction: the art needs open Arcane with no land under it, and the crossing loses the first thing that never comes back.

6. **Every town jokes past its loss.** "You save most of the street", then "a promising result". Applause works once. By the island I no longer believe anyone can be hurt, and the table has told me the shape of everything left. That is where I stop caring. Direction: on return the gap in the street is still there, and Ilse doesn't mention it.

7. **"Strange deep-field creatures" in the last fight** are filler. Direction: seed them at every lift fight, smaller, with a known cause.
