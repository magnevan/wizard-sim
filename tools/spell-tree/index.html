Spell Tree Explorer

Type a spell the Magicka way: queue elements, pick a cast mode, and see what comes out. Proposal A keeps Magicka's rules and adds two things: Point, a cast at the cursor or on an ally, and Seal, which stores a spell to go off later. Release sets off everything you've sealed at once. On a keyboard: Q W E R / A S D F for elements, 1–5 for modes, Space for Seal, Esc to clear.

Rule set

A.1: Point on an ally casts your Self spell on them. Self gains enchantments (haste, glass cannon, stoneskin, ethereal, cleanse). Shield on an enemy is a Cage. Yank needs Arcane alone, so Earth + Arcane on an enemy is a Geyser under it.
