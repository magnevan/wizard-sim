# Segment 1, the opening: brief and first beat list

**Stage 1 and a first pass at stage 2. All of this is a proposal.** It assumes the innate-casting twist is taken. Critique and options: `../../explorations/2026-11-09-opening-and-innate-casting.md`. Minutes are guesses until a blockout is played.

## The brief

1. **In and out.** You arrive with nothing. You leave with your hands, an etched focus, the core elements at pool 1 and low regen, and the meditation dream.
2. **The one new thing.** The moment your spell comes out of *you*, on keys you already know.
3. **The story's one job.** Establish that the same instability breaking the world is what's giving you this.
4. **Length.** About 40 minutes, the first innate cast at about 20. Cut first: the road (beat 5).
5. **One or four.** The cohort are all sent together, and the first cast happens to everyone at the same moment. Nobody is the chosen one, so it doesn't matter how many you are. Each player has their own bench, lane and station.
6. **Returning players.** A skip at the start goes straight to beat 8 with the same unlocks. The compact tutorial stays as the quick start.
7. **Pillars.** Leans on 2 (nobody can tell you what mixes do) and 5. Bends 1 only briefly, because a veteran waits for the unlock unless they skip.
8. **Smallest test.** Beats 1 and 7 alone: a bench, then the same keys with the bench dead. Does the moment land?

## Needs from the game, for a blockout

Most exist: the hands beats, the rune beats, weapons, the etched rule (designed; not built), and LDtk rooms.

New:
- A **bench**: a fixed device you stand at, driven by the normal rune keys. Pillar 6 allows it, because its input is your spells.
- **Unlocks by story step**, per seat.
- Regen and pools at the early values.
- The meditation dream. Beat 8 can start as a plain room.
