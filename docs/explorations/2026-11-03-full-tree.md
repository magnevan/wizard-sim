# 2026-11-03: The full tree (proposal C, "form × cast + payload")

This responds to round 3 (`2026-11-03-feedback-round-3.md`). The user's diagnosis: spells were designed as one-offs, not as points in the full space the inputs allow. Proposal C rebuilds the tree so the space is regular, and every spell is a cell you can find. It's interactive in the **Spell Atlas** (https://claude.ai/artifact/GiZ7G7eQ4pMqXLaPEZK6Cw), with rules in `tools/spell-atlas/rules-c.js`. **It's a proposal for the user to react to.** It supersedes the A and A.1 rule sections of `2026-11-03-spell-tree.md`, but keeps that doc's vessels idea.

## 1. Why A and A.1 felt like one-offs
`tools/spell-atlas/audit-a1.js` adds each new element to every typeable queue (1,034 queues) in every mode and checks whether the spell changes.
- **A:** 3,199 of 10,395 additions changed nothing (31%).
- **A.1:** 3,462 of 10,395 (33%).

The cause is that A was written leaf-first: a rule per liked spell ("Shield + Weapon = Bulwark, unless Ice or Lightning"). Each rule swallowed the elements it didn't mention. Magicka mostly avoids this because its extra elements always ride along as damage types.

## 2. The rule
> **The strongest element picks the FORM. The cast picks the GEOMETRY. Every other element adds its one PAYLOAD trait.**

- **Form priority (Magicka's, made explicit):** Shield > Earth > Ice > Arcane > Life > Lightning > the spray elements. Among the spray elements the most numerous wins.
  - Shield → structure; Earth → heavy missile; Ice → shards; Arcane / Life → beam; Lightning → arc; Water / Fire / Cold / Steam → spray.
- **Payload traits, one per element, the same in every form:**
  - Water: Soak (wet, shove)
  - Fire: Burn
  - Cold: Chill → freeze
  - Lightning: Chain
  - Arcane: Burst
  - Earth: Weight (harder hits, knockdown, slower)
  - Life: Mend (heals whatever it touches, as in Magicka)
  - Steam: Scald and a sight-blocking cloud
  - Ice: Shards
  - Shield is never a payload, because it always takes the form.
- **More of the form element** means a bigger spell. **More of a payload element** means a stronger trait.

## 3. The casts (no Point key)
| Cast | Input | Geometry |
|---|---|---|
| Force | RMB | from you along your aim (as in Magicka) |
| Area | Shift+RMB **tap** | around you |
| **Place** | Shift+RMB **hold**, let go | at the cursor (replaces Point on the ground) |
| Self | MMB / Space | on you, **or on the ally the cursor is near** (replaces Point on an ally) |
| Weapon | Shift+LMB **tap** | swing plus a ground line (Tidal Wave, Fault Line) |
| **Conjure** | Shift+LMB **hold** | a weapon that stays in your hand until it breaks or you conjure another |
| Seal | hold **Ctrl** while casting | store instead of cast; the cast picks the vessel |
| Release | one key | sets off everything sealed, and shatters your own ice and rock walls |

This uses the user's tap vs hold preference twice. It answers their seal binding note (a modifier plus the click). It removes the misclick problem: no spell depends on what the cursor touches, except Self choosing an ally, and a Self miss lands on you, which is harmless.

**Self has no form.** Every element adds its own buff:
- Water: Cleanse
- Life: Mend
- Cold: Steady
- Lightning: Haste
- Arcane: Glass cannon
- Earth: Stoneskin
- Fire: Fervor
- Steam: Mist
- Ice: Frost armor

A mix splits the power, so each buff is weaker. With Shield, Self is a ward that resists the other elements (Magicka).

**Vessels:**
- Seal + Force: line rune
- Seal + Area: orbitals
- Seal + Place: rune
- Seal + Self: staff slot. Release puts the stored elements straight back in your queue, ready to cast in any mode.
- Seal + Weapon is unassigned, since Loaded Blow was cut.

## 4. The grid
10 forms × 5 casts plus Self = **52 cells, 976 distinct spells** (counting payload sets, not power). There are **0 inert additions** (`audit-c.js`). The table below lists the 50 form cells; Self adds a Ward cell and a buff cell.

| Form | Force | Area | Place | Weapon | Conjure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shield | barrier / rock wall / ice wall / elemental curtain | ring of the same | ward dome / stone dome (cage) / ice dome | shield bash | Bulwark |
| Earth | rock (charge; full charge pierces) | earthquake | Geyser | Fault line | stone maul |
| Ice | shards (ricochet) | shard nova | ice spikes | spike line | ice blade |
| Arcane | beam | nova | delayed blast | popping line | **hook (Yank, ally or enemy)** |
| Life | beam | healing ring | healing spring (lingers = Sustained Circle) | healing line | mending rod |
| Lightning | arc | discharge | strike (Surface Surge on water) | ground chain | whip |
| Water | spray | splash ring | Flood | Tidal wave | tide glaive |
| Fire | flamethrower | fire ring | fire patch | fire line | flame sword |
| Cold | frost spray | frost ring | frost patch (Frost Bridge) | frost line | frost glaive |
| Steam | jet | burst | cloud | scald wave | steam lance |

**Emergent rules, with no special spells:**
- **Beams through a structure pick up its elements.** That gives the Lens / Prism for free, as the user asked ("a consequence of some combo, not something we design around").
- **A stone dome placed over an enemy is a cage.**
- **Release shatters your walls,** which gives Shatter Bulwark and Ice Shrapnel.

## 5. What it costs
- **Liked leaves lost:** Attuned Blast ★ (Life only heals now), Contingency ? and Payload Effigy ? (auto-triggers removed), Stay of Death ? (now just a healing dome), Ethereal Shift ? (moved to Magicks). Healing Line is back to healing everyone in the line, so the user's concern about it is resolved by Magicka's rule.
- **Magicka habits that change:**
  - Self without Shield no longer hits you with the element (no "wet yourself" trick; Water is Cleanse).
  - Shield + element Force is a curtain, not mines. Mines are now Seal runes, which removes the old double meaning.
- **The rows are uneven:**
  - Spray rows are thin. Cold has exactly one spell per cast, because Cold + Water is Ice, Cold + Fire and Cold + Steam cancel, and Life outranks Cold.
  - Shield is the richest row (81 payload sets per cast) and acts as the universal container. The Earth row has 30, and the Arcane and Life rows have 12 each.
  - This could be a feature (sprays are the simple, fast tools), or a reason to tune.
- **Readability risk:** a 4-payload spell (e.g. a Bulwark that burns, chills, shocks and heals) may not read on screen. It's worth testing whether 2 payloads is the practical ceiling, or whether payload strength should split like Self buffs do.
- **Buff list:** Steady, Fervor and Mist are new and untested inventions.
