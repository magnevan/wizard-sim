# Spell wishlist (leaf nodes)

Step 1 of the bottom-up method: list spells that would be *cool to cast*, ignoring how they're input. Step 2 is finding the structure that generates them (→ `spell-grammar.md`). Step 3 is exploring the other leaves that structure implies.

**Status (wishlist round 1, 2026-11-03):** ★ keep, ? maybe, ✗ cut, ⇢ merged into another entry. The user's notes are condensed next to each entry.

## Offense
- ★ **Ricochet bolt**: bounces off walls. You reach for it when the geometry calls for it, either natural walls or walls you built yourself.
- ? **Charged lance**: hold to charge, then pierce. Similar to charging rock in Magicka. Less exciting than ricochet.
- ⇢ **Delayed charge** → trap rune.
- ✗ **Boomerang**: nothing inherently interesting about the blade coming back.
- ? **Orbiting stones**: a classic. Only worth it if we find something more interesting, e.g. *prepare them, then trigger them* (merges with seekers).
- ? **Seekers**: needs a scenario. Best framed as a preparation spell for downtime, possibly as a variant of orbiting stones.
- ★ **Geyser**: an effect at the cursor position is a reasonable baseline (≈ Magicka's rock + arcane).
- ★ **Tidal wave**: a forward-moving wave shape. Magicka only had this in some Magicks.
- ? **Drifting cloud**: possibly a slow variant of the wave.
- ★ **Trap rune**: shaping the environment. It was underused in Magicka because it was inflexible to cast, easy to blow yourself up with (trigger radius vs arena size), and the pacing didn't reward preparing.

## Support / utility
- ★ **Ward toss**: there must be some way to shield a friend, or place a barrier at an arbitrary location.
- ? **Life tether**: meh. Some form of healing must exist, but don't build on this one.
- ★ **Yank**: good. Possibly a variant of teleport (a Magick in the original).
- ✗ **Swap**
- ★ **Prism**: something cool here, but split/focus/pick-up were unclear. Better version: a built structure that trades build time and harder aiming for stronger beams.
  - Two players beam into the prism and the beams *merge*; whoever was first steers.
  - Aim the cursor close to the prism to split the beam, far away to concentrate it.
  - Picking up an element only makes sense if it buys more than the single keypress it saves.
- ★ **Sustained circle**: good concept.
- ★ **Grand ritual**: interesting, but the execution needs cleverness.
- ✗ **Vortex lure** as a designed spell. It's basically a small Black Hole; fine if it emerges from other spells.
- ? **Echo totem**: summons are interesting but rarely done well. Worth exploring (→ `minions-and-clones.md`).
- ★ **Attuned blast**: a shaping variation you *add* to a spell to spare allies. Costs extra effort, so it isn't the default, which is good.

## New from the user (round 1)
- **Clone**: leave your body behind and play through a weaker, more vulnerable copy with less personal risk. → `minions-and-clones.md`
- **Conjured weapons**: create a weapon to swing for a while, enabling a melee build: set up a weapon, protect yourself, add close-range spells. → `conjured-weapons.md`
- **More self/ally enchantments**: heal over time, run speed, glass cannon, becoming ethereal / plane shift... → `enchantments.md`
- **Spell (pre)preparation**: queue a spell now and release it later, for long combos, bursts, combo timing, or an "oh-shit" escape. → `spell-preparation.md`
