The morning everything breaks

The order you learn the elements in, and now the fights. The rule is simple: you fix the thing, and you get the spell. Each stop has a machine, a fix made with what you already have, an effect of its own, and a fight that asks the question its new element answers best. The route goes out and back through the courtyard, whose channels light up as you go. Everything here is still a proposal.

    What changed since round 1

    • Fix the thing, get the spell. Round 1's overflow and drought arrivals are gone. Each fix uses your hands or the element you learned last, so every stop practises one element while it hands you the next.
    • Life is the first cast, at the infirmary, as you chose. The battery is your second element.
    • Shield moved to third. You have wards for your friends before Lightning chains through them, and the ward alarm is what pulls you back up the hill. The biggest battle is now the courtyard finale.
    • The route is out and back through the courtyard, passed four times.
    • The harbor has surf, not rain, so you're wet only where the waves land. The waterworks' job is lifting: the water is at the bottom of the gorge and the fire is up on the hill.
    • Six stops plus a short one, with Cold and Lightning together at the harbor and Earth on the climb back. Honestly about an hour, with walking.
