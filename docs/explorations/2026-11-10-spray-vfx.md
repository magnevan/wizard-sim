# Spray effects, redone (2026-11-10)

The first pass of a spell-effects review. The user's notes on the three sprays (right click with Water, Fire or Cold):

- **General:** more runes should look like an upgrade: larger, hotter or more intense, more particles. A five-rune spell should give some awe.
- **Water:** the middle of the spray looks static, and adding Water barely changes it: the cone grows a little, and the push doesn't show.
- **Frost:** the middle looks static, a ground patch at the tip is dragged around as you turn, and more runes don't look impressive.
- **Fire:** the middle is static too; use the defence's flamethrower from the opening (they should look the same anyway).

## What was wrong underneath

- The drawn cone was a fixed 16° half-angle whatever the runes, but the sim's hit cone is 32° and grows 25 % a rune, to 65° at five (`effects.ts` channelTick). Reach grows from 4.5 to 6.5 tiles. So most of the real growth never showed, and the spray hit things it didn't seem to touch.
- The middle was three jittering lines fixed to the staff and the aim, so it looked like a picture laid over the floor.
- Every particle was placed from the current aim, so anything at the end (the frost patch) swung round with the staff.

## The redesign (`game/packages/client/src/art/spray-jet.ts`)

All three sprays now share the turret flamethrower's model: each particle has a ground spot and a height, leaves the staff at its height and comes down toward chest height. Three changes on top of that:

1. **Each particle keeps the aim it was born with.** The view keeps a short history of each spray's aim (`AimTrail`), so a sweep bends the stream like a hose and the old flame trails behind. What lands, like droplets and settling frost, stays where it fell.
2. **The fan is the sim's fan.** The drawn half-angle and reach are the hit cone's. Particles crowd the middle, so the edges read as thin spray.
3. **Runes show.** The form's runes (1 to 5) raise density, speed, size and heat, and the top tiers add their own features:

| Runes | Water | Fire | Frost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | a thin rope that frays into droplet streaks | the turret's jet: white core, cooling to red, smoke along the far two thirds | a pale breath, wind streaks, glitter, mist |
| 2–3 | a thicker rope, more droplets, mist over the far half | wider and denser, hotter | ice shards fly point first from 3 |
| 4 | a 3 px rope; pressure rings ride it out | the root burns blue-white | thicker gale |
| 5 | blowback spatters round the caster; a rainbow hangs in the mist | fireballs roll up out of the far end; an ember shower | snowflakes tumble in it; a frost ring pulses off the staff |

The light the spray casts also grows with its runes.

Frost's ground patch at the tip is gone. Frost now settles in specks where the gale actually blew, and they stay put and melt away.

Doubles, Echoes and Sigil turrets use the same jet, thinner. The healing sprays (Life + Water, Life + Fire) keep their old cones. A defence's flamethrower in the opening keeps its own `flame-jet.ts` for now: it lives on the opening-segments branch. Once both are merged, it could be drawn by `sprayJet` too.

## The Spray Lab

`game/packages/client/spray-lab.html` (dev server only, never built) draws every spray at one to five runes side by side, with the game's own code at the game's scale. `?old=1` puts the old cone beside each, `?sweep=1` swings the aim, `?hit=1` dots the sim's hit sector, `?t=1.3` freezes the clock for screenshots.

## Open

- Is a 65° fan at five runes right? It's what the sim hits, so the drawing follows it. If it reads too wide, the sim's angle should change, not just the drawing.
- Fire at five is a wall of flame between the caster and the target. Is that awe or clutter in a crowded fight?
- The water rainbow is the quietest five-rune touch; it may need more presence, or a different idea.

## Round 2: the user's notes on the sprays, and beams and the rock

The user played the sprays:
- **Frost:** perfect, no notes.
- **Water:** very good, but the central jet is too strong against the spray, more so with more runes. The rainbow looked like a static sprite hanging in the air. If there's a rainbow, it should be subtler, like droplets glimmering with colour in parts of the spray.
- **Fire:** very good, but each flame particle looked too round and distinct. The blue-white root didn't show, and the fireballs looked like odd spheres bubbling up.
- **The 65° fan feels right.**

Changes:
- Water's jet is at most 2 px thick, frays as early whatever the runes, and has fewer white surges, so the droplets carry the look. The rainbow sprite is gone. At five runes, droplets crossing a slowly drifting band of the fan glint in prism colours.
- Fire's flames are soft smears: a dithered body stretched back along each flame's path, with a solid heart. The fireballs are gone; five runes throws a heavier shower of embers instead. From four runes, a blue-white inner flame burns at the staff, drawn over the rest.

### Beams (Life, Arcane)

The notes: the range should be much longer, so the beam just runs until it hits something. More runes should look more intense. More runes didn't heal or hurt faster either.

- **Range:** 30 tiles, past the edge of the view.
- **Damage and healing:** this was a logged rule ("beams last n× longer instead of hitting harder", the rune scaling audit). It now splits the runes into power and time, √n each, so a whole beam is still worth n runes, but a 4-rune beam hits twice as hard. Logged in `decisions/README.md`.
- **Look** (`K.beamSurge`, tier = all the spell's runes, so Arcane + Fire + Fire is above Arcane + Fire): thicker with each rune; bright surges race from the staff to the end, more and longer with more runes; sparks shed off the sides from three; a halo along the length from three; a white-hot core from four; rings pulsing off the staff at five; a bigger splash where it hits. The Spray Lab shows them: `?kinds=arcane-beam,life-beam,fire-beam`.

### The Earth rock

The note: it moves but doesn't feel like it's flying, a little too slow, and needs a trail.

- A fully charged rock flies at 16 u/s, up from 12; a quick one at 60 % of that.
- In flight it tumbles, faster when it's fast, with the light kept on its upper left so the turning reads. Three air streaks trail off its edges, a wake of dust hangs where it passed, and grit falls out of it. Ice, mud and other rocks get the wake in their own colours.
- Not seen mid-flight in a screenshot: headless Chrome stalls on some frames of the Earth demo (it did before this change too), so only the launch frame was checked.

## Round 3 (2026-11-11)

Closed: water, Life and Arcane beams. Notes and changes:

- **Fire spray:** the blue-white root looked static. Now, from four runes, each flame is born blue-white and cools through white into the usual ramp, so the blue is part of every flame's life and moves out with it.
- **Beams and upkeep:** the user called the audit's "a beam's runes buy time" a mistake: a channel already lasts as long as regen pays its upkeep. Beams now gain power per rune like other forms, and upkeep is paid per rune, from each rune's element. Logged in `decisions/README.md`.
- **Missile speed:** rocks were still too slow, and projectiles should share a speed unless one has a reason not to. Every missile flies at 20 u/s; ice shards keep their short reach.
- **The Earth form's missiles** (`art/rock-flight.ts`, compared in `/missile-lab.html`), each trail being particles left where the missile passed:
  - Earth + Water: kept (a wet rock).
  - Life + Earth: the tail snapped at its midpoint and the rock read as harmful. Now a green seed of light with leaves circling it; its motes hang along the arc and drift down.
  - Earth + Cold: a smooth marble that also stood for ice. Now a fuzzy snowball with a comet's tail of snow puffs and falling flakes.
  - Earth + Water + Cold: now a cluster of three ice shards flying point first, shedding glitter.
  - Earth + Arcane: now a stone in a pulsing shell of arcane light, carved with a turning rune, three motes on orbit, a sparkle trail. On impact: a flash, two rings racing out, sparks, a rune burned on the floor that fades.
  - Earth + Fire: the ball with a stiff tail is gone. Now a meteor: a molten core in churning flame, its flames left behind to swell, rise and cool, then smoke and embers.
- Not yet seen in game: the Arcane impact and the new missiles in a fight (headless shots of the Earth demo stall; the Missile Lab shows them in flight).

## Round 4 (2026-11-11): the Earth missiles again

Notes: the speed is right now, but the missiles don't change enough with more runes. Life + Earth's new trail and colour are better, and it should grow too. The snowball is good, but its impact threw shards where a puff of frost that slows was expected; shards only with Water. Earth + Water + Cold looked like one crystal: three staggered shards, three hits, much faster. Arcane is good. Fire + Earth: the flames rising looks wrong.

- **Tiers:** every Earth missile now takes its tier from all the spell's runes. The meteor's tail lengthens and its ball grows, with an ember shower from four. The snowball's tail lengthens and fills, snowflakes tumble out from four, and a frost ring pulses at five. The ice shards' streak lengthens. The arcane stone's shell grows and gains a mote a rune, with a second, counter-turning shell from four. The seed of light grows and gains a leaf a rune, with an outer ring of sparkles from four. The rock's dust wake thickens.
- **Frost puff:** an Earth + Cold missile ends in a puff of frost (a cloud thrown out that slows and hangs, frost settling on the floor), and the sim chills everything inside it. No shards. Logged in `decisions/README.md`, with the rules text.
- **Ice shards:** Earth + Water + Cold throws three shards, staggered (the lead in the middle, the others behind on either side), each a third of the rock, at 32 u/s; each hits on its own and splinters.
- **Meteor:** its flames no longer rise; they stream straight back from where they were left, fanning a little, cooling and shrinking.
- Not yet seen in game: the frost puff's impact (checked by a test in the sim, not on screen).

## Round 5 (2026-11-11): the Area rings (novas)

Notes: novas look odd cast on the move (pin them to the body, or better, have them drag along); the back of a nova draws in front of the caster in its first frames. Water: a little too intense and slow. Life: good. Frost: good, a little too intense and slow, and the frost left behind too uniform and static. Arcane: good, a little faster, and more a pressure wave blown out from you than something from the ground. Fire: good, slightly too intense, the scorch left behind too uniform (a grid). Earth: good.

- **Two passes, behind the caster:** a nova is drawn as its far arc (with everything on the floor) and its near arc. The far arc goes behind every body standing in front of the centre, the caster included; the bodies were never in the effects' occlusion before, only props and walls (`vfx.ts` novaDepth).
- **Dragged along:** the travelling wave is drawn round a centre 80 % of the way from where it was cast to where the caster is now, so it follows someone on the move; what it leaves on the floor (scorch, frost, wet) stays where it was cast.
- **Marks in clumps:** the scorch, the frost and the wet patch are drawn as loose pixels with patchy noise (`speckle`), thinning unevenly, instead of an even ordered dither. Frost also leaves crystal sprigs that glint and melt one by one; fire leaves embers going out one by one.
- **Pace and strength:** water 0.62 → 0.45 s to full size, its wave 28 % lower, less spray and light; frost 0.5 → 0.38 s, 25 % lower, fewer shards, softer light; fire 15 % lower, fewer embers, softer light.
- **Arcane** is a pressure wave: a squashed ring at chest height racing out in 0.3 s, a white leading edge over a hazy band of compressed air, a dim trough behind, a fainter echo ring, speed lines; the floor only takes a faint ring.
- The Nova Lab (`/nova-lab.html`, `?move=1` walks the caster) shows them as filmstrips.
- Not seen in game yet: the occlusion with real sprites (headless shots of the cast stalled), checked in the lab with a stand-in caster.

## Round 6 (2026-11-11): Earth missiles, momentum

Notes: Arcane and Life + Earth look good. The snowball's frost cloud sat on the impact, which broke its momentum: it should wash on the way the ball went; the same for the fireball's burst, and its trail particles could start large and shrink (the classic tail). A big plain rock looked slow, its wake small for its size. The ice shards should leave staggered, not spaced along the path, and their damage should pay for a third rune.

- **Frost puff and fireball burst** carry on the way the missile flew: the cloud and the flames are thrown forward and settle ahead of the impact; the sim's chill disc sits half its radius ahead too.
- **Meteor tail:** each flame starts large at the ball and shrinks to nothing.
- **Big rocks:** the wake is as wide as the rock; air streaks lengthen with its size, five of them on a big rock; a short smear of the rock just behind it.
- **Ice shards:** leave the staff one after another (4 ticks apart, fanned 2°) and together hit 1.7 rocks. Logged in `decisions/README.md`.
- Not seen on screen yet: the impacts' wash and the big rock's wake (the lab doesn't draw them; headless shots of Earth casts stall).
