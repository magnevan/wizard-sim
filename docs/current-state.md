## Controls and spell grammar
- **Spells are proposal C:** form × cast + payload. The strongest element picks the form (Shield / Sigil > Life > Earth > Arcane > Lightning > sprays); the others are payload. 39 cells, 678 distinct spells, 0 inert additions in the Atlas (`node tools/spell-atlas/audit-c.js`). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Seal becomes an element](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-seal-becomes-an-element-steam-and-ice-dropped-see-explorations2026-11-03-structures-review-and-elementsmd).
- **Keys:** Q/A Water/Lightning, W/S Life/Arcane, E/D Shield/Sigil, R/F Cold/Fire, G Earth (opposites stacked; T reserved for Air). Same decision.
- **The look follows the carried element** (Earth + Fire is a fireball); status shows on the body, never as icons. D: [The look rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-look-rule-ice-shards-turret-draw-the-users-feedback-on-the-vfx-gallery-and-pixel-battlefield-pass-3).
- **Runes queued = magic; nothing queued = your hands (grammar v2):** RMB tap Strike, RMB hold Heavy, Shift+RMB Special (some Specials are held: Siphon, Mend), Shift+LMB Retreat, Space Defend (hold to block, tap as a hit lands to parry). D: [Weapons review, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-weapons-review-round-1-explorations2026-11-05-weapons-review-1md), [Grammar v2 in the game](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Casting while moving** is allowed, at reduced speed while channelling or charging. D: [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Life spells only heal;** the element lending the shape sets the size, Life sets the strength. D: [Life spells, the size rule](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-life-spells-the-size-rule-the-inert-audits-life-group).
- **Heals lend resistance:** every non-Life element in a heal lends the healed its guard strengths for 4 s (any body, enemies included). D: [Every element in a heal lends its resistance](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-every-element-in-a-heal-lends-its-resistance-guard-ties-settled).
- **Friendly fire is on;** the sculpting wand (pure Sigil Weapon) makes your spells spare allies (but not burning ground). D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any).

## Runes and regen
- **About 4 runes per element** (Fire 5, Sigil 3), regenerating 0.22 a second. Reserved when queued, spent on cast, refunded on Esc or a cancel. Attune (Arcane on yourself) multiplies regen by 2.5 for 6 s. Channels are free for 5 s, then cost upkeep. D: [Calls Claude made while building milestone 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-calls-claude-made-while-building-milestone-1-the-user-can-veto-any).
- **Stuff costs once, energy costs upkeep;** external drains never take you below a reserve. D: [Resources and battlefield](decisions/README.md#2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefield-see-explorations2026-11-03-resources-and-battlefieldmd).
- **Per-form stacking:** sprays grow with the square root, rocks with n^1.5, beams last longer; the rest +70 % per extra rune. D: [Balance tooling](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-balance-tooling-and-the-first-measurements).

## Shield and Sigil
- **Shield is Model A:** barriers and wards drain the Shield pool while they stand; at the floor, the oldest breaks. Earth walls are stuff (paid once). D: [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd).
- **Walls:** Place draws a wall (no drag: a quick arc), Force a forward wave, Area a small full-strength ring. Rock stands 25 s (+5 s per extra Earth). Double-tap Shield over your wall to drop it. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Play-test notes, round 2](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-2-explorations2026-11-04-playtest-2md).
- **Wards and auras on allies:** Self lands on the ally under the cursor only for a ward (Shield) or an aura (Sigil + elements). A caster may keep up any number of wards. D: [Rings, wall lifetime, Self on allies](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-rings-wall-lifetime-self-on-allies), [Grammar v2; wards for support](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-grammar-v2-in-the-game-wards-for-support).
- **Sigil binds:** every device binds one Sigil rune until it goes off, breaks or is dismissed; with none free, a new one recalls your oldest. Double-tap Sigil on a creation to dismiss it. D: [Sigil's identity](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-sigils-identity-see-explorations2026-11-04-sigil-identity-and-buffsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).
- **Sigil devices:** turrets (cast the real spell, uncharged, half price, all together at most 60 % of your regen), glyphs and line glyphs, orbitals, the Lens (pure Sigil Place), Blink (pure Sigil Force, to the cursor), auras (Sigil + Self), the Double (pure Sigil Self). D: [Play-test round 2, answers](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-play-test-round-2-answers-and-notes-811), [The overnight drafts, reviewed](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-the-overnight-drafts-reviewed-see-explorations2026-11-04-overnight-draftsmd), [Play-test notes, round 1](decisions/README.md#2026-11-04-play-test-notes-round-1-the-users-first-session-with-the-build).

## Weapons and guards
- **Weapons** (Sigil + Weapon; ~30 uses; no two-handers; carrying costs 5 % speed): Tide trident, Healing hands (Healing link; Special **Mend**, held), Frost spear (javelin), Lightning whip, **Arcane wand** (bolt, charged piercing bolt; Special **Siphon**, held), Flame sword, Stone maul; without one, the staff. D: [Round-2 weapons and guards built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-weapons-and-guards-built-into-the-game-on-the-recommendations-pending-the-users-round-2-ratings), [Round-2 ratings built](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-2-ratings-built-new-retreats-and-passives-the-arcane-wand-mend-carrying-slows-you).
- **Eight guards** (Shield + Weapon), one per lead element: Mirror buckler, Bulwark, Ember targe, Rime kite, Spark ring, Tide shield, Prism, Leaf aegis. Each has its Defend, Retreat, passive and resistances while carried. Same decisions.
- **Mixing:** the strongest element leads by type (Life > Earth > Arcane > Lightning; else the most of Water, Fire, Cold, ties in that order). Extras bring their passive and resistance row (half per rune, all at 2). The Leaf takes only the extras' strengths; other guards' extras are a coat that bites whoever hits the block. D: [How elements mix in guards](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-how-elements-mix-in-guards-merged-with-the-users-identities), [guard ties settled](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-every-element-in-a-heal-lends-its-resistance-guard-ties-settled).
- **Every guard rune past the first** adds 15 % guard HP and Retreat distance; a Prism parry returns the beam mixed with its coat. D: [Mixing ratings](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-mixing-ratings-every-rune-scales-the-prism-mixes-its-return).
- **Resistance is hyperbolic:** damage taken is 1 / (1 + r); a weakness takes 1 − r; r = 3 still blocks the element's status. D: [Guard resistance is hyperbolic](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-guard-resistance-is-hyperbolic).
- **The Prism is a Capacitor:** it stores what its raised block takes; lowering it with 10+ charge fires the Lance (7 u, 8 + charge, carrying the coat). D: [The Prism stores and Lances](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-the-prism-stores-and-lances-mend-and-siphon-take-their-extras), [Round 4 ratings](decisions/README.md#2026-11-05-round-4-ratings-the-lance-prism-is-adopted).
