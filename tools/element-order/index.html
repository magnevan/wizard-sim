The morning everything breaks

Your idea: each element is learned when a machine drawing on its field spills it into you. The district sits in an Arcane zone, where every field is close to the surface. You start with Life, fix a run of urgent breakdowns, and learn Shield last at the ward line, because the barrier to the wilds is down. Below is Claude's version of that order, with concept images made in Codex. Everything here is a proposal.

Connecting to saved answers…

    The proposed order

    Overflow or drought. To keep eight arrivals from repeating, the spill isn't the same each time. Some machines are overflowing and drench you. Others are starving, and you drive them until they're dry, then carry on from your own hands as at the battery. Shield is the one nothing spills: it's a caster element, so it comes from you when there's nothing left to draw on.

    Asset types the images suggest

    • A family of field machines, one per element, in the academy's brass and stone, each with its own silhouette and a working, overflowing, starving and dead state. The battery's turrets are the first of the family.
    • Ground that spreads: a fan of frost, a dry basin that floods, rift cracks, wet sheen, overgrowth creeping over paths.
    • Camp and siege dressing: tents, cots and stretchers, herb racks, covered wagons, sandbags, a guard post with a bell.
    • Harbor and waterworks: water wheel, sluice gates, aqueduct, cranes, nets, piers, a boat locked in ice.
    • People at work: healers, porters, guards, harbor workers, so it's a morning in a real place.
    • Rift-touched creatures: ordinary animals with violet crystals growing on them, a cheap way to vary the bestiary near Arcane.
